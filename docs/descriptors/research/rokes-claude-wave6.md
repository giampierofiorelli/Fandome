## guccini-folkbeatn1 (Folk Beat n. 1, 1967)
- Credits (Discogs API, release 3122897 https://api.discogs.com/releases/3122897): Guitar, Vocals Francesco Guccini; Harmonica, Guitar Alan Cooper; Lead Guitar Antonio Roveri; Producer Odoardo Veroli; La Voce del Padrone.
- AllMusic/TiVo review by Mariano Prunes (via Qobuz https://www.qobuz.com/it-it/album/folk-beat-n1-francesco-guccini/0094638479055): "the sound of this album faithfully re-creates that of The Freewheelin' Bob Dylan or All the News That's Fit to Sing" -> sounds_like Bob Dylan (The Freewheelin' Bob Dylan)|Phil Ochs (All the News That's Fit to Sing). "a base made of two acoustic guitars, one strummed and the other playing licks or arpeggios", plus harmonica and occasional tambourine -> instruments, electronic 0, polish 1. Aim: modern Italian protest and folk music -> genres folk|politicalfolk. Two strands: somber tragedies (friend killed in a car crash, Auschwitz child) and satirical portraits of Italian society -> lyrics storytelling|social|irony|political, mood 1. "Talkin' Milano": "a hilarious self-deprecating parody about the incongruence of posing as a folksinger in Italy", half Italian half English -> language it|en. Voice: untrained and nasal, strong Emilian accent: no voice token fits, left empty.
- OndaRock monografia https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/francescoguccini/: "un terreno musicale scarno, semplice, relegato alla funzione di accompagnamento" (energy 1, theatrical 1, experimental 1, polish 1); "una raccolta impetuosa di ballate e racconti"; "accorata ricerca di giustizia sociale"; rating 6.5; "vende pochissimo".
- canzonedautore: kept as in the other Guccini rows; supported only by OndaRock treating it as the work of "l'autore" (third place, weak). Discogs user styles say Folk Rock, Acoustic, Ballad: folkrock not used, since the reviews describe only acoustic guitars.
- landmark 0: Prunes calls it seminal, OndaRock says he was not yet mature; not his defining record.
- La Voce di New York (Irene Sparacello 2021): "con Auschwitz realizza la sua Blowin' in the Wind" (a song's role, not the sound: not used in sounds_like).
- confidence medium.
