## Important caveats

- `Stripped`: release year 2002 certain, precise US date varies (AllMusic Oct 29 vs reissue/Apple Oct 22). Original *20-track* programme controls. Exclude later anniversary-edition bonus tracks.
- `Christina Aguilera` (1999): original album recording/producer credits only; exclude producers who worked exclusively on later single remixes. `Reflection` includes film-music sessions but the standalone album is `studio`, not `soundtrack`.
- `Mi Reflejo`: original 11 tracks, with translated hits and newly made Latin material. Exclude 2001/2002 reissue dance remixes from credits, instrumentation and genres. Latin pop is the main genre, while salsa/bolero concern individual songs.
- `My Kind of Christmas`: Christmas standards and new pop numbers share one studio album. Producer/R&B/blues/soul descriptions have different strength than easily confirmed orchestral credits; review evidence notes. The original edition *already includes* a holiday remix of “The Christmas Song”; that does not authorize later remix-only production credits.
- `Stripped`: vocal producers are retained when explicitly credited as such; **executive-only** Ron Fair is excluded. Etta James and Jane's Addiction are specifically documented influences for this album, while sound-alike artists are editorial comparisons only.
- Critics disagree strongly on *Stripped* and *My Kind of Christmas*; the disagreement and what each source supports are recorded in `aguilera_phase01_evidence-notes.md`.
- `Just Be Free` (2001) is a commercially issued archival collection of pre-fame demos, not an original 2001 studio project by Aguilera; it is excluded as non-landmark. The 2008 greatest-hits compilation is not presently selected as a landmark. Revisit only on evidence of a specific canonical importance.
