# Funk and funk rock: three landmark albums added by Claude (Oct 2026)

Owner asked for 2-3 key funk-rock albums so that Red Hot Chili Peppers' records have real neighbours.

- **Funkadelic, Maggot Brain (1971)**: Westbound; George Clinton producer; Eddie Hazel (lead guitar, inspired by Jimi Hendrix), Bernie Worrell (keyboards), Tawl Ross, Billy Nelson, Tiki Fulwood; genres named: psychedelic funk, funk rock, R&B, acid rock, psychedelic soul; Echoplex delay in the mix; themes: urban decay, Vietnam, unity (You and Your Folks), drugs (Super Stupid). Yuval Taylor compares Wars of Armageddon to Miles Davis's Agharta. Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, Pitchfork, Rolling Stone 500, Christgau).
- **Parliament, Mothership Connection (1975)**: Casablanca; Clinton producer; Bernie Worrell (Minimoog, ARP, Wurlitzer, Hammond, Clavinet), Bootsy Collins (bass), Fred Wesley, Maceo Parker, Brecker brothers (horns); genres named: funk, R&B, funk rock, disco, progressive soul; Afrofuturist space concept. Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, Christgau, Pitchfork, Rolling Stone 500, National Recording Registry).
- **Faith No More, The Real Thing (1989)**: Slash/Reprise; Matt Wallace and the band producers; Mike Patton's first album; Billy Gould on E-mu Emax for Epic; genres named: alternative metal, funk metal, rap metal; lyrics around murder, darkly comic (Q). Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, Kerrang!, Q, Pitchfork, Billboard).

New genres: `psychedelicfunk`, `progressivesoul`, `rapmetal`. Wurlitzer recorded as `electricpiano`, Echoplex as `delay`.

## Second batch (owner: artists that emerge from the Red Hot Chili Peppers analysis)
Chosen by how often they appear in the RHCP rows' `sounds_like`: Fishbone (6 albums), Jane's Addiction (3), Living Colour (3).
- **Jane's Addiction, Ritual de lo habitual (1990)**: Warner; Dave Jerden and Perry Farrell; Navarro (later in RHCP for One Hot Minute), Avery, Perkins; violin (Charlie Bisharat), strings; genres named: alternative rock, alternative metal, funk metal, hard rock, progressive-rock influence, klezmer influence (Of Course); Q compares Stop! to RHCP, Select credits a Led Zeppelin revival; themes: stealing, anti-racism, grief (Xiola Blue). Source: en.wikipedia.
- **Living Colour, Vivid (1988)**: Epic; Ed Stasium, Mick Jagger (two tracks, harmonica); genres named: hard rock, heavy metal, funk metal, alternative metal, funk rock, with soul, jazz, pop, rap, arena rock, punk, avant-garde jazz; critics compare Hendrix, Lynyrd Skynyrd, Roxy Music, Sly Stone, Led Zeppelin, Ornette Coleman's Prime Time. Source: en.wikipedia.
- **Fishbone, Truth and Soul (1988)**: Columbia; David Kahne; horns (trombone, trumpet, saxophone), keyboards; genres named: funk rock, funk metal, hard rock, ska, punk, reggae, soul, funk, blues; socially conscious theme. Freddie's Dead is a Curtis Mayfield cover (not an influence). Source: en.wikipedia.
New genre: `klezmer`.

## Third batch (owner: complete the picture): the two direct influences the band names
- **Gang of Four, Entertainment! (1979)**: EMI/Warner; producers Andy Gill, Jon King, Rob Warr; Gill (guitar), Allen (bass), Burnham (drums), King (vocals, melodica); genres named: post-punk, dance-punk, art punk, funk-punk, with funk, reggae and dub; lyrics: Situationism, Marx's alienation, the personal is political, subverted love songs. Flea: it changed how he looked at rock and sent him on his trip as a bass player. Andy Gill produced RHCP's debut. Source: en.wikipedia.
- **James Brown, The Payback (1973)**: Polydor; Brown producer (vocals, electric piano, organ); Maceo Parker, St. Clair Pinckney, Fred Wesley, trumpets; Jimmy Nolen, Cheese Martin (guitar); Jabo Starks (drums); written for Hell Up in Harlem but not used; cyclic funk grooves plus two soul ballads. Live at the Apollo stays out (live album, owner's scope). Source: en.wikipedia.
New genre: `funkpunk`.
