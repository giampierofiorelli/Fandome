# Vasco Rossi — Fandome phase 03: evidence notes

Only these four **original studio albums**, 1989–1998. CSV judgements are editorial; facts, genres and equipment carry the supporting provenance in the other CSV files. Source labels **original 1989/1993/1996/1998** take precedence over later 2017 remasters and streaming compilations.

## `vasco-liberiliberi` — Liberi liberi (1989)

- **Production and personnel:** Original-sleeve transcriptions on [VRLIVE](https://www.vrlive.it/album/liberi-liberi) and [Chartsurfer](https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/vasco-rossi/liberi-liberi-album_ffeeru.html) list **Vasco Rossi alone** as producer (Terry Balls Srl: *executive* production). Arranged with **Rudy Trevisi**. Do not assign Guido Elmi, who is absent, or Maurizio Biancani, who mixes selected tracks. Sources credit guitars (Tullio Ferro, Paolo Gianolio, Maurizio Solieri, etc.), bass, conventional drums (notably Lele Melotti on Stasera), Trevisi's keyboards, **MIDI electronic drums**, saxophone, percussion; Serse May/Serse Mai on programming. The **Akai EWI 2000** is explicitly noted as an electronic wind controller, not arbitrarily relabelled a synth model in the instruments column. `drumprogramming` is an interpretation of printed 'MIDI drums' and programming; Claude should verify if it requires a specifically printed programming credit for drum patterns.
- **Genre order:** MusicBrainz tags rock/hard rock/pop rock; original LP marked Pop Rock. Main style `poprock|rock`; hard rock further down, accessible pop and ballad sections last. The `ballad` classification is interpretive because the label describes an introspective, melancholy record without labelling every slow track a 'ballad'.
- **Scores:** Energy 3 vs previous stadium-rock years, electronic 2 due to MIDI drums and Trevisi's electronics; mood 2 for reflective lyrics, polish 4 for professional studios, groove 2 for rock rather than dance. This is editorial album-level balancing, not a measured signal.
- **Reception divergence:** [Universal](https://shop.universalmusic.it/products/vasco-rossi-liberi-liberi-vinile) frames a mature, melancholy record; [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/vascorossi/) dislikes much of it, calling it revival rock and harmless pop. A 2024 reported statement by Vasco stresses the deliberately hard/rough production. Record earned importance for arena/stadium trajectory irrespective of unanimous critical acclaim.
- **Influence:** none tied specifically to these sessions in a direct artist/producer source. No unsubstantiated `sounds_like`.

## `vasco-glisparisopra` — Gli spari sopra (1993)

- **Year:** studio album is **1993**; *Gli spari sopra* single / advance cassette is **1992**. Do not let 1992 or remaster 2017 become album's year.
- **Producer exceptions:** Main producers **Guido Elmi and Vasco Rossi**. Original-era credits and a 1993 trade magazine statement from manager Maurizio Lolli document **Dave Stewart as producer of `Hai ragione tu` only**; preserve him as third producer with the scope in notes. **Pino Daniele** appears as guest guitarist/chorister on that track, not as producer or influence. [Original recording-credit transcription](https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/vasco-rossi/gli-spari-sopra-album_ffegfp.html) and [interview](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/UK/Music-and-Media/90s/1993/MM-1993-03-20.pdf).
- **Instrumentation:** Transcribed session credits list electric guitars and acoustic/12-string guitars, live bass and drums (Randy Jackson, Gregg Bissonette, Vinnie Colaiuta, Lele Melotti), Jonathan Cain/Ernesto Vitolo keyboards and Hammond, accordion (Cain and Fio Zanotti), Fender Rhodes, percussion (Paulinho Da Costa), violin-string ensemble and occasional whistle. `stringarrangements` from Fio Zanotti's explicit arranging credit. No flute or electric-wind controller is invented.
- **Analogue/programming conflict:** [Universal Music Italia](https://shop.universalmusic.it/products/vasco-rossi-gli-spari-sopra-doppio-vinile) says recording used analogue techniques *without computers or sequencers*. Some secondary personnel lists name Luca Bignardi / Sandro Magri / Stefano Bittelli for **keyboard programming** on this album. Because the technical assertions conflict, `production` includes `analogtape|stringarrangements` only. Before adding `programming` or `synthprogramming`, Claude should check original credit print and distinguish pre-production/keyboard patches from recorded sequencing. Do not claim it was entirely free of electronics.
- **Genre & subject:** Hard rock and pop rock mingle with unusually strong ballads (*Vivere*, *Gabri*, *Stupendo*). *Occhi blu* is called “almost country” by one reviewer, so `country` is **provisional and last**. The title track is a translated/rewritten version of An Emotional Fish's *Celebrate*; a covered/adapted song **does not constitute documented influenced_by**. Political/social commentary (title song, Stupendo, Delusa) balanced with private heartbreak.
- **Reception:** OndaRock praises several core songs yet is sceptical of image; DeBaser reviewers diverge over length and polish. Score 4 energy and 1 mood reflects heavier material plus melodic middle.

## `vasco-nessunpericoloperte` — Nessun pericolo… per te (1996)

- **Title and ID:** Match Fandome catalogue typography with **single Unicode ellipsis** `…`, despite original package spelling three dots. Existing catalogue id `vasco-nessunpericoloperte` remains unchanged.
- **Producer hierarchy and contradiction:** [VRLIVE](https://www.vrlive.it/album/nessun-pericolo-per-te) transcribes 'Prodotto da Guido Elmi e Vasco Rossi' and credits Celso Valli for *arranging/realising* tracks 5 & 10 plus partial 9. However [original vinyl credit transcription](https://musicbase.net/nessun-pericolo-per-te) additionally prints **Celso Valli as producer on tracks A5 & B5**. Accordingly `producers=Guido Elmi|Vasco Rossi|Celso Valli` (last producer **tracks 5 and 10 only**, verify against original label before finalising). The latter source also calls Andrea Braido *co-producer* while the complete sleeve thanks him for *pre-production*; he is **not** added pending physical verification. Floriano Fini is executive producer, excluded.
- **Instruments and techniques:** Detailed per-track EMI record credits on [Chartsurfer](https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/vasco-rossi/nessun-pericolo-per-te-album_ffeneh.html) show guitar/bass/drums, piano and Hammond, backing voices, strings on *Gli angeli*, tambourine, studio programming. `programming|drumprogramming` for documented programming and *Io perderò*'s drum-programming; `stringarrangements` for Valli's string parts; `overdubbing` because the original sleeve says overdubs at Record Plant. Avoid claiming specific machines/models not named.
- **Genres and themes:** rock/pop-rock originals, hard rock on multiple numbers, long ballads (*Sally*, *Gli angeli*), a blues-rock title song (single-track, near tail), and a reviewer-provided AOR tag. Not 'opera' because of string use. Themes include exclusion in *Mi si escludeva*, self-examination in *Sally*, sensuality and relationships on title track. Voice `soft` describes quieter lead delivery within individual ballads, not a separate vocalist.
- **Reception:** Some retrospective listener reviews call this a late peak; OndaRock's critic strongly disagrees. The `landmark` flag reflects key songs and centrality in catalogue, not unanimity of reviews.

## `vasco-canzoniperme` — Canzoni per me (1998)

- **Catalogue:** currently missing: add new studio entry `Canzoni per me`, year 1998, id `vasco-canzoniperme` before importing; no reissue-only versions. **Eight tracks** in original 1998 release, not a supposed nine-track edition.
- **Production:** Original [VRLIVE](https://www.vrlive.it/album/canzoni-per-me) / [Musicbase](https://musicbase.net/canzoni-per-me) list **Vasco Rossi and Celso Valli** as producers. Bignardi engineered/mixed/programmed sound and computers. Tony Levin bass; Vinnie Colaiuta and Paolo Valli drums; Mike Landau, Paolo Gianolio, Stef Burns guitars; Celso Valli keyboards; a quartet of violin/viola/cello on *L'una per te*, additional strings on *Io no*. `programming|stringarrangements` only; no inferred DAW or drum machine.
- **Style:** Label [Universal](https://shop.universalmusic.it/products/vasco-rossi-canzoni-per-me-vinile) describes more acoustic, intimate writing and blues-to-dance textures. `ballad` and `canzonedautore` are **explicitly editorial interpretations** of acoustic songwriting discussed in the sources, not direct album-tag quotes; `acousticrock` is now additionally supported by a reviewer describing the record as rock/acustico. `dance` principally applies to *Rewind* and is down-ranked. Studio instrumentation claims do not equate to `analogtape`.
- **Critical contrast:** contemporary [Rockol](https://www.rockol.it/recensioni-musicali/album/3/vasco-rossi-canzoni-per-me?amp=1) values personal songs; DeBaser reception polarized over acoustic intimacy vs dance-pop Rewind; OndaRock is sharply dismissive. Reuse of Vasco's previously **unreleased early compositions** is not a compilation and does not change studio classification.
- **Themes/scales:** nostalgic/reflective, 2 energy and 2 theatrical (close, restrained record), polish 5 reflects international studio production, groove 2 is whole-record weighted rather than promoting *Rewind* to album-wide dance. 1 electronic because actual studio programming exists but the core is conventional drums/guitars/strings.

## Across the phase

- `sounds_like` and `influenced_by` are deliberately **blank**. Reviews comparing lyric writing to Springsteen, quoting Deep Purple melodies, adapting An Emotional Fish songs, or listing Dave Stewart / Pino Daniele as participants do **not** meet the established Fandome criteria for album-specific influence or unambiguous sonic resemblance.
- Genre annotations explicitly distinguish **source naming** from **editorial interpretations** for acousticrock / ballad / canzonedautore / countryrock. Remove the last, tentative `country` if second-person review considers an almost-country *Occhi blu* insufficient evidence for country-rock proper.
- Even though the 1998 album was commercially successful, that is not grounds to assign any `mainstream` value. Reception CSV is outside this six-file batch.
