# Imagination — Phase 01 — evidence decisions and review notes

These descriptors refer to the **original releases**, not later streaming reissues. The sources table contains live references including primary album-sleeve transcriptions and producer testimony. Scales use the Fandome project's 0–5 editorial anchors, not automated acoustic feature estimates.

## imagination-bodytalk: Body Talk (1981), original seven-track LP

**Genres**: `postdisco`, `britfunk`, `funk` and `soul` are identified by release discography; `disco` appears on original pressings. Additional original pressing styles include `downtempo` (particularly the restrained first two tracks), `electronic` and `electro`. AllMusic calls it pop-oriented R&B and specifically notes tight dance tracks interspersed with slower romantic material. A later review calls 'Burnin’ Up' proto-house: **do not label its 1981 album as `house` as an established genre**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_Talk_(Imagination_album) ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/body-talk-album_ffvvph.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/release/body-talk-mr0000471485.

**Instruments**: John lead vocals/keyboards; Ingram bass/vocals; Kennedy drums/percussion; additional keyboards from Swain, percussion from Jolley. Original sleeve and CD credits name *Orphy Robinson on vibraphone solely on “In and Out of Love”*. AllMusic separately identifies piano runs, keyboard cascades, synth accents. Thus `keyboards|synth|bass|drums|percussion|piano|vibraphone`. New instrument token `vibraphone` needed. Do **not** invent a LinnDrum or a specific synth model for this debut. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/body-talk-album_ffvvph.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/release/body-talk-mr0000471485.

**Production**: Jolley and Swain produced, arranged and recorded; Khan credited as executive producer/engineer only. Christgau explicitly mentions Leee John's falsetto; AllMusic speaks of "Flashback"'s echo. `falsetto|heavyreverb` is conservative: the `heavyreverb` tag covers the conspicuously echo-filled tracks, not every moment. Generic echo/reverb distinct from specific vintage hardware. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/body-talk-album_ffvvph.html ; https://www.robertchristgau.com/get_artist.php?name=Imagination ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/release/body-talk-mr0000471485.

**Lyrics / profiles**: AllMusic characterises the opening pair as erotic and restrained, while “I'll Always Love You (But Don't Look Back)” addresses a complicated romantic attachment. `sensuality|love|heartbreak` are themes rather than claims of identical lyrics across tracks. Energy 3 reflects slower languid first side counterbalanced by “Burnin' Up” and “Flashback”. Groove 4 rather than 5: mix of dance and softly paced material. Experimental 2, theatrical 1: sound is economical and intimate despite the extravagance of *stage costumes*, which must not inflate vocal theatricality. Mood 3 (sensual, some darker tracks), polish 4 (clear, controlled Red Bus production). Critic Christgau likens John's falsetto to **Billy Griffin** and **Damon Harris**, performers in The Miracles and The Temptations respectively; `sounds_like` uses canonical group nodes. These are **comparisons, not claimed influences**. https://www.allmusic.com/album/release/body-talk-mr0000471485 ; https://www.robertchristgau.com/get_artist.php?name=Imagination.

## imagination-intheheatofthenight: In the Heat of the Night (1982), original eight-track LP

**Genres**: AllMusic post-disco and R&B; original 1982 UK/European release listings explicitly tag synth-pop, disco and downtempo; a Canadian pressing tags **boogie**. Album summaries and singer/keyboard/groove review support funk and soul. `synthpop` is not a guess from the instruments: original pressing tags it directly. https://www.allmusic.com/album/r41257 ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/in-the-heat-of-the-night-album_frpvpg.html ; https://rectangletriangle.com/products/in-the-heat-of-the-night-imagination.

**Instruments**: AllMusic's review specifically describes John's high/soft singing, Ingram's tenor, dark keyboard riffs and Kennedy's backbeat; band roles well established. `synth` and `synthbass` explicitly supported for "Just an Illusion" by primary **Tony Swain producer testimony**, plus bass guitar (Ingram), keyboard, drums/percussion from original band; however detailed session credit names for every track are not available. No unverified flutes, saxes or strings. https://www.allmusic.com/album/r41257 ; https://tonyswainproducer.blogspot.com/2012/08/tony-swain-producer-composer.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/artist/imagination-mn0000078255/biography.

**Production**: Swain explains that **Roland SH-1000** played the famous monophonic bass line of "Just an Illusion", mixing two octaves, a square/sine combination and physical portamento control. His account specifically contradicts guesses of Minimoog and Prophet-5; use `rolandsh1000` only. Producer Swain and Jolley were main producers, not 1982 cover designer or chief label executive. The Wikipedia-only TB-303 line on the title song could not be independently verified and is excluded. The singer's falsetto is explicitly named by AllMusic. https://tonyswainproducer.blogspot.com/2012/08/tony-swain-producer-composer.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/r41257 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_the_Heat_of_the_Night_(Imagination_album).

**Scores**: Energy 4 / tempo 4 / groove 5 reflect the sustained 1982 club tracks “Just an Illusion”, “Music and Lights”, “Changes” and the boogie title track, while album has smoother moments; electronic 4 reflects synth-bass prominence combined with human groove. Mood 4 is more buoyant than the debut, with romantic atmosphere. Polish 4, experimental 2, theatrical 2 reflect sleek accessible pop rather than excessive studio novelty. Lyrics `love|sensuality|heartbreak`: credible romantic themes; be cautious about assigning narratives to individual titles without quoted lyric analysis. Landmarked by both best-known singles and retrospective esteem. https://www.allmusic.com/album/r41257 ; https://www.robertchristgau.com/get_artist.php?name=Imagination.

## imagination-nightdubbing: Night Dubbing (1983), eight-track special-remix album

**Not a conventional compilation.** MusicBrainz identifies original UK album as an Album + Remix release. The original sleeve specifies special remix versions of previously released songs. Seven remixes attributed to the Jolley/Swain/Lengyel team, with **"Changes" remixed by Larry Levan**. Mixes of "Changes" recorded at Electric Lady, others mixed at Red Bus, per original UK pressings. `producers=Steve Jolley|Tony Swain`: **source original album producer credits**, not Larry's track remixer role. https://musicbrainz.org/release-group/7097de54-3213-3860-9273-f81de138f99c ; https://retrorarity.co.uk/imagination-night-dubbing-r-b-records-disc348280n6.html ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/night-dubbing-album_ffvurc.html.

**Genres**: `disco|dub|postdisco|electronic|electro|synthpop|funk|soul|rnb`. 1983 UK sleeve reports Dub and Disco, US issue Dub/Electro/Synth-pop; all eight recordings are reworked club versions of prior post-disco/funk/R&B material. `dub` here describes studio remix technique, **not an assertion that the record is dub reggae**: AllMusic expressly distinguishes them. No claim the album is Chicago house merely because Larry Levan and Frankie Knuckles adopted the music. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/night-dubbing-album_ffvurc.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/night-dubbing-mw0000373251 ; https://imagination1.bandcamp.com/album/changes-larry-levan-remixes.

**Instruments**: Since source songs were recorded in 1981–82, `synth|keyboards|bass|drums|percussion|piano` reproduces instruments directly documented on those **source LPs** (piano manipulations specifically discussed in critic review of Night Dubbing). This is NOT new instrument overdub evidence. Do **not** carry across *vibraphone*, since the one track featuring it (“In and Out of Love”) is **not** on the eight-track Night Dubbing LP. Do **not** put SH-1000 as a production token, since individual remixes are not established as preserving the exact mono bass layer in original gear form. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/body-talk-album_ffvvph.html ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/in-the-heat-of-the-night-album_frpvpg.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/night-dubbing-mw0000373251.

**Production / scales**: AllMusic highlights echo, reverberation, forceful snare emphasis, altered tempo, intermittent strange edits, piano recontextualisation. `remixing|dubeffects|heavyreverb|falsetto` (echo is documented, but specific delay hardware or processing is not identified) maps that sonic detail. Electronic 4, experimental 4 and groove 5 emphasise stark club-dub treatment; not level 5 experimental noise. The earlier singers and lyric themes remain audible but not dominant. **Critical tension:** the 1983 *Times* called the LP an examination of studio technique, while AllMusic calls it primarily relevant to DJs/completists. A historically creative method and mixed retrospective reception can coexist: no fabricated critical consensus. https://www.allmusic.com/album/night-dubbing-mw0000373251 ; https://www.fondsound.com/imagination-night-dubbing-1983/ ; https://retrorarity.co.uk/imagination-night-dubbing-r-b-records-disc348280n6.html.

## imagination-scandalous: Scandalous (1983), eight-track original UK LP

**Naming**: `Scandalous` is 1983 original; 1984 American/Canadian release `New Dimension` is the same song cycle (with remixes/edits on the first two tracks), not an additional album, even though AllMusic lists both. **Do not append a new New Dimension album id.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandalous_(album) ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/scandalous-album_ffvugc.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/new-dimension-mw0000838159.

**Genres**: post-disco, funk, soul, R&B from summary/review; original 1983 Brazilian pressing classifies `downtempo|synthpop|funk|disco` explicitly; all original pressings assign electronic categories. AllMusic's gloss-pop criticism supports broad pop orientation, not literal guitar rock. Distinguish genre from positive/negative reception: critic disliked much of the record but acknowledged disco/funk rhythm elements. Genre evidences list album-wide vs some-track scope. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/scandalous-album_ffvugc.html ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/scandalous-mw0000233687 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandalous_(album).

**Instruments**: original/reissued sleeve credits John vocals, keyboards, percussion; Ingram bass guitar, synthesizer, keyboards, vocals; Kennedy drums/percussion; Swain keys; Jolley percussion. This is not a reason to invent sax, guitar, horns, live orchestra or named drum machines. Production: main album producer credits Swain/Jolley; earlier lead vocal high register is present, but no additional model or mixing effect tied securely to 1983 UK issue. `falsetto` is the only production token. Jonathan Fearing's 1984 US **remix** of two cuts is a release-specific mix credit, not a new 1983 album producer. https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/imagination/scandalous-album_ffvugc.html ; https://www.soulandfunkmusic.com/album/imagination/scandalous.

**Critic disagreement and scale**: Amy Hanson at AllMusic regards it as too smooth and formulaic compared with the earlier LPs, and compares arrangements directly with **Duran Duran** and **Spandau Ballet**. Hence `sounds_like=Duran Duran|Spandau Ballet`, with **blank `influenced_by`**. Christgau's `C+` on the retitled US edition independently confirms weaker reception than the first two B+ LPs. The score `polish=5, experimental=1, theatrical=2` describes controlled, polished sound with reduced unusual studio edges rather than claiming "poor quality" on any numeric descriptor. Energy 3 and tempo 3 because smoother love songs balance grooves “Looking at Midnight” and “This Means War”; groove remains 4. Mood 3 sits between gentle and lively cuts. Do not tag New Wave just because AllMusic compares the band to mainstream 80s groups. https://www.allmusic.com/album/scandalous-mw0000233687 ; https://www.robertchristgau.com/get_artist.php?name=Imagination.
