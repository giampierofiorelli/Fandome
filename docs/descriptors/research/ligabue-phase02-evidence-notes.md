# Ligabue — phase 02: evidence and interpretation (29 October 2026)

This is a **draft** evidence file for the four *original studio albums* released in 1995–2005. Scales are editorial judgments, not facts from the sources. Empty `influenced_by` and `production` cells are intentional where documentation does not justify a claim. Every source is cross-indexed in `ligabue_phase02_sources.csv`. Album descriptions use original music rather than later remasters, tributes, live performances or compilations.

## ligabue-buoncompleannoelvis — Buon compleanno Elvis (1995)

- **Identity and recording:** the official discography fixes 21 September 1995, sessions at Esagono in Rubiera from 15 May to 29 July, and *artistic* co-producers **Ligabue and Fabrizio Barbacci**. **Valerio Soave is executive producer**, so he is excluded from `producers`. Original issue is 14 tracks, including the short *Rane a Rubiera blues*. The current official page's displayed tracklist omits that short track; the period track list is confirmed in the fan-club transcription and Apple/album catalogues. Do not use 2025 remake instruments or producers.
- **Sound:** new La Banda core of Poggipollini/Previte guitars, Righetti bass and Pellati drums; electric/acoustic guitars, guests' Hammond, harmonica and percussion. OndaRock documents distortion on *Vivo morto o X*. No proven synthesizer or particular effects machine for original sessions. The blues miniature is a *small* tail descriptor, not the dominant genre.
- **Genre:** a rock record with pop-rock melody and rock-and-roll songs. AllMusic names *Alternative Country-Rock* exactly; to avoid silently changing its wording, the candidate token `alternativecountryrock` is retained for Claude to add with parents countryrock/altcountry, alongside other sourced styles. If Claude prefers separating the existing tokens, record the equivalence and keep both where warranted. `alternativerock` represents AllMusic's alternative/indie-rock description; no claim this was an underground record.
- **Scales 0–5:** energy 4 (vigorous core, some slower songs), electronic 0 (acoustic/electric instruments, not synthesizer based), mood 4 (often warm and expansive), theatrical 3 (anthemic rather than operatic), experimental 1 (recognisable song forms), polish 4 (improved cohesion/recording), tempo 3 (mixed), groove 2 (rhythmic yet not primarily dance music). Editorial, album-specific evaluations.
- **Voice and words:** rasp/force in the lead vocal; themes of identity, everyday characters, nights, intimacy and spirituality. `sounds_like` Springsteen and Iggy Pop from OndaRock's comparisons only; not `influenced_by`. OndaRock mentions the *Certe notti* riff's Beatles inspiration as Poggipollini's arrangement detail: do not automatically treat that as Ligabue's own verified album-wide influence.
- **Reception:** major career-defining album per official artist biography, AllMusic and RSI. Qualitatively acclaimed, though some criticism finds the music familiar; landmark=1. AllMusic main entry currently displays **1996** in its chronological index, but its release page itself says 21 Sep **1995**, consistent with official discography; original year is 1995.
- **Sources:** https://www.ligabue.com/discografia/buon-compleanno-elvis/ ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/buon-compleanno-elvis-1995-2025-mw0000530157 ; https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/ ; https://ligaterminifanclub.wordpress.com/discografia/buon-compleanno-elvis/

## ligabue-missmondo — Miss Mondo (1999)

- **Production:** original 1999 jacket explicitly credits **Ligabue** as sole *producer*. **Claudio Maioli is executive producer**, not a co-producer. Piero Milesi arranges and conducts London Session Orchestra on *Una vita da mediano* and *Kay è stata qui*, **not** the whole album. Tracks were recorded at Zoo Studio and orchestra at Abbey Road; no claim every track is orchestral. Some metadata disagrees on **17 vs 24 September 1999**; only original year is part of Fandome row.
- **Instruments:** the library-supported local Musicisti Reggiani entry and 1999 edition credits confirm twin electric guitars, acoustic guitar, Righetti bass, Pellati drums, Simoncioni Hammond/piano/Wurlitzer/synth/clavinet and two orchestra tracks, plus trumpet, sax, dobro/resonator guitar, percussion. `ebow` is documented for *Forse mi trovo* by per-track credits; the 1999 interview transcribes an 'ibao' (plausible phonetic rendering of E-Bow), so use standard `ebow` only with original 1999 credits. Ligabue calls a brief melodic figure in *Una vita da mediano* a carillon, but original credits do not confirm a physical music box; therefore `musicbox` is omitted. Do **not** treat all generic 'chitarra' as evidence of the new guitarsynth token.
- **Effects:** source notes include long sustain, saturation and E-Bow. `stringarrangements` specifically covers Piero Milesi's original score; `distortion` for *Miss Mondo '99*'s drum saturation is marginal because the effect is on drums and not guitar. No drum programming; Ligabue says the Beck-like song *Baby, è un mondo super* **sounds like a loop but uses played instruments**, and a reversed guitar was used on **Non fai più male**, the B-side **not included in the album**. Thus `sampling`/`drumprogramming`/`backmasking` would be misleading for this original album.
- **Genres:** pop-rock/alternative rock broadly; Ligabue's period interview explicitly calls *Almeno credo* white pop-soul, *La porta dei sogni* Tex-Mex and *Kay è stata qui* a quasi-folk waltz. These are track-level, tail-weighted genres, not sweeping classifications. OndaRock calls *E* blues-like. `waltz` refers to its 6/8 sway, not a classical music record.
- **Influences:** the interview transcribed from *Musica!* (16 Sep 1999) has **first-person, song-specific** borrowing: Brian Eno's sustained motifs on *Si viene e si va* / *Miss Mondo '99*; guitar manner of **Robert Fripp**; **Beck** as intended model for unusual *Baby, è un mondo super* textures; **John Hiatt**'s *Have a Little Faith in Me* (on *Bring the Family*, 1987) as model for the piano of *Da adesso in poi*; and **R.E.M.** for the pared-back guitars in *Forse mi trovo*. These qualify as documented compositional references, albeit the available transcription is a contemporary fan repost, not a verified scan of the magazine. Confidence high for album as a whole, medium for the *interview transcription* provenance. `sounds_like` includes R.E.M. and John Hiatt as editorial proximity, not additional people from shared sessions.
- **Words/reception:** self-assessment, adulthood, public scrutiny, new fatherhood, love and social commentary. Acclaim mixed: AllMusic/Qobuz appreciates ambition, OndaRock and several 1999 reviewers are critical. Commercially significant but not as defining as 1995; landmark=0.
- **Sources:** https://groups.google.com/g/it.arti.musica.rockitalia/c/1ZVvpy8178c ; https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/miss-mondo/ ; https://musicbase.net/miss-mondo ; https://www.qobuz.com/ca-en/album/miss-mondo-remastered-version-ligabue/0085365709980

## ligabue-fuoricomeva — Fuori come va? (2002)

- **Credits:** Ligabue and Barbacci are documented **producers**, not Maioli (executive). First Italian release **26 April 2002** as corroborated by contemporaneous Rockol announcement and Italian discography; AllMusic shows 21 May 2002, likely regional release metadata. All original songs sung in Italian; the album has 12 tracks, despite a 28 March prerelease article saying eleven.
- **Sound:** Rockol's original review and the Biblioteca Panizzi Musicisti Reggiani archive document guitar, bass, drums, percussion and piano/keyboards. The critic hears a dense but very controlled, internationally polished band sound. Differentiate direct guitar-rock numbers, pop-friendly mid-tempo choruses and ballads. Rockol explicitly points to the two Who-referencing songs at the end; do not infer The Who album influence from homage, so keep in `sounds_like` only (critic comparison).
- **Scales:** energy 3, electronic 0, mood 3, theatrical 3, experimental 0, polish 5, tempo 3, groove 2. Conventional album construction and high level of studio finish explain experimental 0 / polish 5. No instrument model or production technique is specifically supported, so `production` is **empty**, despite the polished sound.
- **Reception:** contemporary Rockol praised recording quality but judged album unadventurous. Retrospective OndaRock is harsher. Not a landmark; keep confidence high on personnel and release despite divergent opinion.
- **Sources:** https://www.rockol.it/news-712190/ligabue-album-riascoltare-coronavirus-fuori-come-va-recensione ; https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/fuori-come-va/ ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/fuori-come-va--mw0000413096

## ligabue-nomeecognome — Nome e cognome (2005)

- **Credits:** original issue lists three actual producers: **Ligabue, Fabrizio Barbacci, Luca Pernici**. **Claudio Maioli** is executive producer and not in the `producers` field. Recording/mixing at Zoo Studio (Correggio); edition has 11 tracks counting the short instrumental *Intro*. Some reviews misspell **Pernici** as *Persici*, but official period news, the jacket transcription and SUONO support **Pernici**.
- **Sound:** recording involved multiple guitarists, **Niccolò Bossini** and multiple drummers including Cesare Barbi, Alessandro Lugli and Eugenio Mori. Original critics and artist interviews call this guitar/bass/drum-driven rock, alongside slower ballads. `distortion` is directly described by Ligabue for *Il giorno dei giorni*; `delay` is specifically described by Ligabue on *Lettera a G.*. Piano/keyboard credit is from the 2005 liner note transcription (Fabrizio Simoncioni). Additional `percussion` is omitted because the original sleeve sources only clearly establish drummers. No Auto-Tune, software, samples, or electronic production is supported.
- **Genres:** rock / commercial pop-rock / contemporary pop-rock; *L'amore conta* and *Cosa vuoi che sia* are slower ballads. AllMusic also labels the work Italian pop. The final `pop` token comes only from Warner retail shelving and is ranked last with weak distinctiveness. OndaRock is highly critical, while SUONO awards a strong technical/artistic assessment; do not turn an opinion into a genre or claim.
- **Lyrics:** self-portrait, death of a close relative, mature love, time and self-awareness. `heartbreak` is omitted pending a particular verified separation lyric; the themes are identity, loss and mature love.
- **Reception:** release-era critics disagree sharply, from SUONO's positive appraisal to OndaRock's 4/10. This is not a career landmark. Contrast between evidence-based sound and critics' artistic evaluation is preserved.
- **Sources:** https://content.suono.it/recensione-musica/ligabue-nome-e-cognome/ ; https://www.ondarock.it/recensioni/2005_ligabue/ ; https://ligaterminifanclub.wordpress.com/discografia/nome-e-cognome-2005/ ; https://www.tgcom24.mediaset.it/spettacolo/articoli/273836/ligabue-nome-e-cognome-del-rock.shtml
