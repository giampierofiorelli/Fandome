## 1. `vasco-macosavuoichesiaunacanzone` — *...Ma cosa vuoi che sia una canzone...* (1978)

- **Context / sonic balance:** The original May 1978 release is more Italian songwriter than stadium rock. Sky Arte emphasises the first songwriting identity and later rock premonitions. Italian discography lists *musica d'autore* and *pop rock*, while AllMusic labels it *Contemporary Singer/Songwriter* and *Italian Pop*. The four-genre row deliberately distinguishes songwriting form from commercial Italian melody. https://arte.sky.it/archivio/2018/11/quarantanni-fa-lalbum-di-debutto-di-vasco-rossi ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/ma-cosa-vuoi-che-sia-una-canzone-mw0000516226 ; https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/...Ma_cosa_vuoi_che_sia_una_canzone....
- **Structure and lyrical range:** Relationship sketches (*La nostra relazione*, *...e poi mi parli di una vita insieme*, *Silvia*) alternate with the emotional and marginal-character narrative *Jenny è pazza*, social irony in *Ambarabaciccicoccò* and introspective writing. Thus `love|heartbreak|introspection|storytelling|social|irony`. Source: https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/ma-cosa-vuoi-che-sia-una-canzone and tracklist https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/...Ma_cosa_vuoi_che_sia_una_canzone....
- **Instrumentation:** Original personnel data name acoustic and 12-string guitars played by Vasco, electric guitar (Enzo Troiano), bass, drums, Gaetano Curreri keyboards, flute/sax, electric violin and percussion. `violin` normalises electric violin to available instrument token; avoid assuming an orchestra. Source: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/...Ma_cosa_vuoi_che_sia_una_canzone....
- **Production:** Reissue reporting explicitly says the original **analogue studio master tapes** were used for the 2018 remaster, so `analogtape` refers to the original source recordings. Do not mark `remastering` as a 1978 production attribute. Taylor is described as executive producer by Rockol but producer by general metadata, while Qobuz names Mario Rapallo for one original single track. The album-level `producers` field is intentionally **blank pending original label verification**, not because no one produced it. https://www.rockol.it/news-697535/vasco-rossi-cofanetto-per-40-anni-ma-cosa-vuoi-che-sia-una-canzone?amp=1 ; https://www.qobuz.com/it-it/album/ma-cosa-vuoi-che-sia-una-canzone-vasco-rossi/0743215840121.
- **Scores:** Low electronic (0), low groove (1), mild energy (2), and moderate polish (2) reflect songwriter-centred arrangements and contemporary audio, not quantified listening data. `clear|raspy` reflects relative vocal timbre described in historical accounts; editorial. `landmark=0` because its main significance is debut and archaeological importance; its better-known successor and the 1980–81 rock records are the stronger defining recordings, not because it is unimportant.
- **Comparisons:** De Gregori and Rino Gaetano are **tentative acoustic/songwriting editorial neighbors**; neither is entered in `influenced_by`. No sources tie an individual act as a direct sonic source to this particular LP.
- **Reception caveat:** DeBaser readers acknowledge raw early qualities alongside unusually direct narratives, while the documentary coverage is more sympathetic about its historical role. No consensus critic score exists in the sources used.

## 2. `vasco-nonsiamomicagliamericani` — *Non siamo mica gli americani!* (1979)

- **Edition:** Original Lotus release on 30 April 1979, recorded November–December 1978 at Fonoprint. Later LP/CD marketed as *Albachiara* is **a retitling/reissue**, not an independent studio album. The original album contains *Albachiara*, *Fegato, fegato spappolato* and *La strega*. https://tg24.sky.it/spettacolo/musica/2019/09/06/vasco-rossi-non-siamo-mica-gli-americani ; https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non_siamo_mica_gli_americani%21.
- **Genre scope:** Album style is pop-rock/rock with the singer-songwriter sensibility still evident in *Albachiara*. A specific critical retrospective explicitly identifies punk, dance, talking blues, vaudeville and pop in different tracks; later long-tail tokens keep low rank. `ballad` applies especially to *Albachiara* and is not proof of an all-ballad LP. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non_siamo_mica_gli_americani%21 ; https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/non-siamo-mica-gli-americani/recensione-jployrow3 ; https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/non-siamo-mica-gli-americani.
- **Instrumentation:** Sky TG24 lists Maurizio Solieri on electric/acoustic guitar and mandolin, Tassoni on bass/double bass, Curreri on piano/keys/synth/effects, Pezzoli on drums/percussion and brass/reed horns including trombone/sax/clarinet. `synth` is **explicitly credited**; electronic=1 reflects limited electronics within an acoustic/electric band. Italian `cembalo` may mean harpsichord or percussion; no corresponding token used. https://tg24.sky.it/spettacolo/musica/2019/09/06/vasco-rossi-non-siamo-mica-gli-americani ; https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non_siamo_mica_gli_americani%21.
- **Techniques:** The source reports electronic sound effects in some songs, but does **not** identify a specific recording machine/process from the production vocabulary. `production` left blank instead of guessing `sampling` or `tapeeffects`. No synth model inferred.
- **Themes:** Social satire and taboo-breaking, sexuality, drugs, youthful alienation, romance and spoken narrative. No claim of explicit political ideology from indirect references. Rockol describes songwriting transition; reader reviews describe dissent about evenness. https://www.rockol.it/news-729812/vasco-rossi-anniversario-album-non-siamo-mica-gli-americani-recensione ; https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/non-siamo-mica-gli-americani/recensione-viva-li.
- **Producer conflict:** Alan Taylor appears as producer in metadata summaries, but named executive/supervisor and mix engineer in detailed Italian credits. Leave `producers` blank for original-pressing review; Curreri was an arranger. Source: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non_siamo_mica_gli_americani%21.
- **Status:** `landmark=1` primarily for *Albachiara* and the emergence of a distinct public voice. `sounds_like=Edoardo Bennato|Rino Gaetano` is tentative late-1970s satirical songwriter/rock editorial adjacency, **not a report of documented causation**.

## 3. `vasco-colpadalfredo` — *Colpa d'Alfredo* (1980)

- **Genre and historical pivot:** Rockol's 2020 detailed retrospective identifies the replacement of Curreri-centred songwriter arrangements with Maurizio Solieri's leading electric guitars and Guido Elmi's production approach. An English discographic synthesis calls the album rock, punk rock and pop rock; Treccani terms the 1980–83 style *cantautorato rock*. These are genre categories, while `italianpop` is a weaker AllMusic classification placed fifth. https://www.rockol.it/news-718295/vasco-rossi-colpa-d-alfredo-40-anni-storia-e-critiche-intervista ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colpa_d%27Alfredo ; https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/vasco-rossi_%28Lessico-del-XXI-Secolo%29/ ; https://www.allmusic.com/album/colpa-dalfredo-mw0000759764.
- **Band and instruments:** Album personnel include Solieri on electric/acoustic guitars, Corticelli/Zuppiroli/Costa on bass or double bass, drums, Curreri/Feliciati keys, Elmi guitar/percussion and backing vocals. No named producer gear or effects; `production` blank. Source: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colpa_d%27Alfredo.
- **Themes/voice:** Provocative title song and *Sensazioni forti* contrast with the emotional *Anima fragile*. First-hand interview reporting describes strong live-rock direction; voice `raspy|powerful` and numeric scores are editorial. https://www.rockol.it/news-718295/vasco-rossi-colpa-d-alfredo-40-anni-storia-e-critiche-intervista.
- **Crediting warning:** Source credits **Vasco Rossi** as producer; a Rockol account of the original 2020 reissue names **Guido Elmi** as effective producer, Taylor as executive producer. Both Vasco and Elmi proposed; confirm sleeve classification with Claude before final import. Curreri recorded a piano part but is **not** entered as producer or influence. https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colpa_d%27Alfredo ; https://www.rockol.it/news-718295/vasco-rossi-colpa-d-alfredo-40-anni-storia-e-critiche-intervista.
- **Acclaim and scale:** Early breakthrough, genre shift and enduring songs justify landmark=1; historical attention is not proof that every review was positive. Album is harder than 1978–79 but preserves softer songs. `sounds_like` choices are editorial neighbors within contemporaneous Italian rock, not sourced influences.

## 4. `vasco-siamosolonoi` — *Siamo solo noi* (1981)

- **Edition / date caution:** Published April 1981. Rockol says April **9** and an English infobox says April **3**, so only original year used. The first pressing's track grouping differs from later CDs that add *Voglio andare al mare (ripresa)*, which must not be counted as new source album. https://www.rockol.it/news-719882/vasco-rossi-siamo-solo-noi-usciva-giusto-40-anni-fa ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siamo_solo_noi.
- **Core genres:** Rock and pop-rock are documented. `aor` is **only** a DeBaser community review label and should be independently vetted; `hardrock` is explicitly one aggressive track, while `reggae` is specifically *Voglio andare al mare* and `blues` is secondary texture. These are low-weight tail styles, not the identity of the album. Both reviews and Rockol highlight the title song as a 1980s generation anthem. https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/siamo-solo-noi ; https://www.debaser.it/vasco-rossi/siamo-solo-noi/recensione-donsanfelice ; https://www.rockol.it/news-719882/vasco-rossi-siamo-solo-noi-usciva-giusto-40-anni-fa.
- **Instrument roles:** Original personnel list Solieri, Riva and Trevisani guitars; Golinelli bass; Melotti/Casini drums; Curreri/Fio Zanotti keys, piano and Hammond organ; Elmi percussion. Fandome `organ` covers Hammond, without inventing a precise instrument model in the `production` column. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siamo_solo_noi.
- **Production:** Although Rockol gives Elmi/Rapallo in the header, its article also characterises one role as executive and another as tied to distribution. Digital label track credits name **Guido Elmi** producer. Proposed `producers=Guido Elmi` pending original pressing. No explicit identifiable processing technique is evidenced; `production` stays blank. https://www.rockol.it/news-719882/vasco-rossi-siamo-solo-noi-usciva-giusto-40-anni-fa ; https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/siamo-solo-noi-vasco-rossi/0888880768599.
- **Story / editorial ratings:** Confrontational generational identity and dark humor against romantic songs (*Brava*, *Incredibile romantica*). Energy/theatrical 4, groove 3, mood 2 reflect this tension. `landmark=1` for the defining title track, historical artist role and retrospective consensus. `sounds_like` neighbors not influence statements.
- **Quotation caveat:** A Judas Priest riff appears in discussion of *Dimentichiamoci questa città*: where a source says quotation/copying rather than artistic self-identified inspiration, do **not** list Judas Priest as `influenced_by`. The same applies to Sex Pistols' musical phrase on 1979 song *Fegato*.

## Cross-phase validation and caution

- Do not convert **Alan Taylor, Guido Elmi, Gaetano Curreri, Maurizio Solieri, Mario Rapallo** into influence links merely because they performed, arranged or produced an album.
- Do not infer instruments from the period or style. Avoid invented studio equipment, synthesizer models, tape tricks and ambiguous `cembalo` until original cover checks.
- Industry reissue dates sometimes dominate AllMusic; original years verified against Rockol/MusicBrainz and not overwritten.
- All original descriptions are original English Fandome research prose, not copied lyrics or review sentences. No explicit objectionable source phrases are reproduced. Some readings are subjective; manual check required before status `reviewed`.
