## Production and reissue caveats
- **La classe operaia**: original 1971 eight-track RCA promotional LP, not 2009/2025 expanded edition; direct interview provides sound synthesis origin. Scored as largely acoustic with localized electronic sound creation (electronic=2, not 5).
- **Questa specie d’amore**: original 1972 nine-track LP; later CDs/LPs append alternates. Do not pull the *Ouverture del mattino* credit into the original row merely because later editions included it.
- **Il diavolo nel cervello**: composer-chosen ten-track 1972 stereo LP, later 20-track remasters not identical; Bruno Nicolai **conducted**, Edda Dell’Orso sang wordless passages.
- **La cosa buffa**: original December 1972 nine-track LP. 2024 13-track record and 2004 25-track CD include new *Ballabile* / reprise variants. Distinguish. James Southall praises theme variability; Joep de Bruijn strongly dislikes its monothematic repetition. Both are documented, no false consensus.
- Mood, energy, theatrical, tempo, groove, polish and experimental are **editorial 0–5 judgments** on evidence of these specific original programme tracks. Each is justified individually in evidence notes.
