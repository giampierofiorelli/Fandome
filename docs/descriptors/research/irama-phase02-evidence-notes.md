## Method and limits

The eight 0–5 scores are **comparative editorial evaluations** aligned to the Fandome guide, not numbers supplied by critics. They apply to the **entire original album**, not individual singles. Genres with only local relevance are marked `some tracks` or `texture` in the genre_evidence CSV even when listed as album descriptors. The `sources.csv` separates label credits, artist testimony, and reception. `influenced_by` is not inferred from collaborations or comparisons: it is blank for the first two albums. The 2025 entry is narrowly limited to Irama's documented album-concept homage to Fabrizio De André and specific musical inspiration by Lenny Kravitz on 'Arizona'; the former is **conceptual rather than sonic**, requiring review if the field is interpreted as musical influence exclusively. Do not infer studio hardware, DAWs, sample libraries, Auto-Tune or signal chains from genre.

### Il giorno in cui ho smesso di pensare (2022) | studio

**Original release:** 25 February 2022, **13 tracks**; exclude four additional tracks from the November deluxe expansion. Primary [Qobuz release credits](https://www.qobuz.com/it-it/album/il-giorno-in-cui-ho-smesso-di-pensare-irama/c2otngjba6dpa). Contemporary [Billboard review](https://billboard.it/musica/pop/irama-il-giorno-in-cui-ho-smesso-di-pensare-recensione/2022/02/2584592/); [Rockol review](https://www.rockol.it/recensioni-musicali/album/10052/irama-il-giorno-in-cui-ho-smesso-di-pensare); [Sky track-by-track](https://tg24.sky.it/spettacolo/musica/2022/02/24/irama-il-giorno-in-cui-ho-smesso-di-pensare-recensione).

- **Genre weighting:** core pop-rap/urban pop with Italian mainstream pop framing. Trap on 'Una lacrima' (Sfera Ebbasta), R&B on '5 gocce' and 'Una cosa sola', straightforward rap on 'Colpiscimi' and 'Iride', reggaeton on 'È la luna' and Latin/Carribean colors elsewhere; modern Afrobeats on 'Moncherie'. The `afrobeat` token represents *Afrobeats* in critics' usage; flag semantic ambiguity with historical Afrobeat and consider a future `afrobeats` token only after taxonomy governance. Ballads ('Baby', 'Goodbye', 'Ovunque sarai') demand separate songwriter tag rather than turning the entire LP into a dance record.
- **Scale reasoning:** `energy=3` because rhythm-led mid-tempo collaborations alternate with slower, emotionally expansive ballads; `electronic=3` because most urban songs use contemporary electronically constructed beats, but important guitar/piano-led material remains (qualitative ensemble reading, no invented model names); `mood=2` owing to heartbreak and loneliness despite upbeat guests; `theatrical=3` for Irama's dramatic rasp and large pop refrains, unlike a staged concept opera; `experimental=3` for a stylistically broad but conventionally structured crossover; `polish=4` for high-finish mainstream pop production with numerous specialists; `tempo=3` for mid-paced Latin/urban tracks balanced by ballads; `groove=3` because several reggaeton/afrobeat tracks are highly danceable but many are reflective.
- **Voice:** Irama's forceful, rasping melodies; featured rhythmic `rap`; clean and powerful singing. `language=it|es` because `Como Te Llamas`/`Yo Quiero Amarte` introduce Spanish alongside the principal Italian, a track-specific supplement rather than co-dominance.
- **Instruments:** guitar documented by Sky for 'Baby', guitar by Qobuz ('Iride', Danny Bronzini); piano documented by Sky on 'Moncherie'. No specific drum machine, software or recording technique is named by the checked original track credits, so production deliberately blank. No unsourced synthesizer model.
- **Producers:** eleven distinct album song-level production credits from Qobuz: Mace, Swan, Shablo, Giulio Nenna, Luca Faraone (also printed `Faraone`), Junior K, Mr. Naisgai, Ítaca, Dat Boi Dee, Greg Willen, Andrea DB Debernardi. Billboard additionally mentions Merk & Kremont in describing sessions; no extra producer token added because `Ítaca` is the credited name for 'Como Te Llamas'. Shablo's **artistic direction** is distinct from his demonstrable per-song producer credits. Neither featured MCs nor mixers added as producers.
- **Influence caution:** Coez, Rocco Hunt and Fred De Palma under `sounds_like` are editable sonic hypotheses, not proven inspiration; `influenced_by` empty. Overall high confidence in identity/credits/genres; scales editorial.

### No Stress (2023) | studio — **Irama & Rkomi jointly billed**

**Original release:** 7 July 2023, **11 tracks** on Universal/Island. [Universal original track credits](https://www.universalmusic.it/popular-music/album/no-stress_36392838677/); [Qobuz per-song credits](https://www.qobuz.com/it-it/album/no-stress-irama-rkomi/ewtgqbnycjanc); [Billboard interview](https://billboard.it/top-story/rkomi-irama-no-stress-intervista/2023/07/06119246/); [Rockol review](https://www.rockol.it/recensioni-musicali/album/10942/irama-e-rkomi-no-stress).

- **Genre weighting:** two-voice pop-rap / urban-pop album, rap in 'Figlio unico', R&B/funk textures mentioned in Qobuz and various tracks, songwriting and some Latin-pop accents in Rockol, dance-oriented summer songs 'Hollywood' and 'Precipito'. The 1980s sound and Drake resemblance are a critic's descriptive comparisons; no false album-wide synthpop/West Coast hip-hop assertion.
- **Scale reasoning:** `energy=3` for accessible mid-to-uptempo pop/rap alternating with quieter tracks; `electronic=3` for drum-machine beats documented on 'Hollywood'/'Precipito', but song texture is diverse; `mood=3` because carefree summer and attraction coexist with introspection on 'Gravità' and 'Quando piove'; `theatrical=2` for mostly conversational traded verses; `experimental=2` for tasteful genre-combination within accessible short song formulas; `polish=4` for streamlined radio sound and diverse credited producers; `tempo=3` for middle-range rhythmic tracks; `groove=4` because several songs are explicitly dance/beat-oriented despite darker breaks.
- **Voices/instruments:** rap phrasing and raspy/powerful melodic vocals from two co-leads. Qobuz credits **Shablo on drum machine** in 'Hollywood' and **Takagi & Ketra on drum machine** for 'Precipito'; no evidence of specific model or exact programming technique. Thus only `drummachine` is entered for instrumentation; production left empty rather than hallucinate software.
- **Producers:** Shablo, Robert Ner, Luca Faraone, Giulio Nenna, Junior K, Cromático, Cucina Sonora, Eydren, SHUNE, Takagi & Ketra, Zorbeats, Corredor: all individually credited per track. NOTE `Takagi & Ketra` remains one joint production act token because the release credits jointly; separate only if source credits individuals. Irama and Rkomi are **album artists and writers** and must not be placed in production without explicit production credit. Guest vocalists Guè, Ernia, Kid Yugi are also not producers.
- **Shared record:** `CO_BILLED['irama-nostress'] = { credit: 'Irama & Rkomi' }`. `with: ['rkomi']` only once a valid Rkomi artist room exists. Do not duplicate descriptors as `rkomi-nostress` unless schema explicitly requires it; currently one stable shared-album ID.
- **Soundalikes:** Blanco (some melody/voice styling), Coez/Ghali/Drake (editorial, with Drake explicitly invoked as *sound* by Rockol), *not* confirmed musical influences. `influenced_by` blank.

### Antologia della vita e della morte (2025) | studio

**Original edition:** 17 October 2025, **14 tracks**; [Warner label store](https://shop.warnermusic.it/products/antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte-cd); [original-track release announcement](https://billboard.it/musica/pop/irama-trackist-antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte/2025/10/06195761/); [Qobuz credits](https://www.qobuz.com/it-it/album/antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte-irama/pbas3tb3ksd92); [Newsic review](https://www.newsic.it/recensione-irama-antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte-traccia-per-traccia/); [Irama Rockol interview](https://www.rockol.it/news-754768/irama-antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte-nuovo-album-intervista-vide).

**Warning: Qobuz URL now displays a 15-track digital version**, with *Cabana* explicitly ℗ 2026. This package covers the **2025 14-track original** and intentionally excludes *Cabana* and its 2026 contributors/instrumentation/genres. This matters for catalogue chronology, album-level scores and producer roster.

- **Genre weighting:** Italian narrative pop/songwriter ballads form the core; strings and orchestral pop timbres recur (Qobuz credits 'Senz’anima', 'Buio', 'Galassie', 'Mi mancherai moltissimo', 'Giulia', 'Tu no', 'Il giorno'). The stronger guitars of 'Polvere' justify localized pop-rock. 'Arizona' has **artist-documented country and blues** coloration with credited **dobro**; these are clearly track-specific. 'Ex' contributes urban pop texture, whereas a less-authoritative critic calls 'Il giorno' soul and 'Tu no' gospel/soul-style choruses: these latter tokens are included with low strength and song-limited scope; Claude may prune. Do not label the whole album as rap solely from Qobuz's generic retail genre classification.
- **Scale reasoning:** `energy=2` dominated by ballads and slower narrative builds despite 'Arizona' and 'Polvere'; `electronic=2` acoustic piano, string players and guitars contrast with synth and specific programming credits; `mood=1` loss, nostalgia and intimate grief recurring across concept; `theatrical=4` cinematic climaxes, dramatic vocals and large string arrangements (not a sung-through musical); `experimental=2` concept framing and arrangement contrasts within accessible conventional songwriting; `polish=4` multilayered detailed studio production and well-credited orchestral material; `tempo=2` broad slow/mid-tempo repertoire; `groove=1` few dance-led songs, with isolated urban-pop episodes.
- **Voice and themes:** Irama's strained `raspy` and strongly sung `powerful` notes contrast with intimate `soft` and clear piano ballads. Lyrics emphasize grief, mourning, memory, love/heartbreak, and narrative sketches, plus sexuality on 'Arizona'. `storytelling` supported by Newsic's discussion of 'Circo'. Main language Italian.
- **Instrument evidence:** Qobuz explicitly credits piano, synth, electric and acoustic guitar, bass, live drums/percussion, violin, viola, cello, keyboards and **dobro** (Luca Mongia on 'Arizona'). Multiple string orchestration credits; `strings` umbrella plus individual bowed instruments is purposeful, not a duplicate record. Only `dobro` is a new instrument token (a resonator guitar, not a slide pedal steel). Do not introduce uncredited brass or sampling.
- **Production evidence:** `stringarrangements` is extensively documented for Giulio Nenna and Davide Rossi (as arranger, **not** necessarily producer). `vocoder` explicitly credited to Giulio Nenna on 'Galassie'; `programming` credited to ROOM9 ('Ex'), Michelangelo ('Lentamente') and `drumprogramming` to Andrea DB Debernardi on 'Il Giorno'; `liverecording` limited to Irama's reported direct recording of 'Giulia' via Newsic. No named DAW, instrument model, compressor, pitch correction or specific loop is substantiated; do **not** infer `autotune` from one informal review.
- **Producers:** from original 14-song Qobuz song credits: Giulio Nenna; Cromático, Danien, Gow Tribe ('Arizona'); Andrea Biviano and Daniele Mona ('Senz’anima'); Andrea DB Debernardi ('Polvere', 'Tutto tranne questo', 'Giulia', 'Il Giorno'); ROOM9 ('Polvere', 'Ex'); Michelangelo ('Galassie', 'Lentamente'); Juli ('48 ore'); Luca Faraone ('Circo'). **Mix engineers, string arrangers and backing vocalists alone are excluded.** Particularly Davide Rossi is credited for string arranging/performing on 'Mi mancherai moltissimo' but not for producer in Qobuz; a critic loosely calls him producer — follow explicit per-track credit. Vocal production is differentiated from overall production.
- **Influences:** Irama explicitly cited **Fabrizio De André**'s Spoon River-inspired title/concept ([Rockol](https://www.rockol.it/news-754768/irama-antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte-nuovo-album-intervista-vide)); this is **conceptual/literary inspiration, not documented sonic imitation**. In a 2025 interview he explicitly cites **Lenny Kravitz** as a source for the 'Arizona' chorus/attitude, together with LA's country/blues sound ([SuperGuidaTV](https://www.superguidatv.it/irama-presenta-il-nuovo-album-antologia-della-vita-e-della-morte/amp/)). Both credits are deliberately qualified rather than assuming an album-wide Kravitz style. `sounds_like` Diodato, Ultimo, Marco Mengoni, Coldplay are editorial hypotheses, not source-backed influence claims.
- **Critical disagreement:** Newsic calls the work uneven despite recognizing emotional strength (6.75/10); Rockol records Irama's intention to move away from conventional beat-making. Avoid inferring novelty or perfect cohesion from artist claims. Specific weaker urban/soul tags are openly labelled as low-strength rather than hidden.
