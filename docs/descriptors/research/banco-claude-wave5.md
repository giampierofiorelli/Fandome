## banco-darwin (Darwin!, 1972)

- **genres**
  - prog: OR-D genre tag "progressive". Discogs gives "Rock / Prog Rock", hence rock.
  - jazzrock: OR-D says "La danza dei grandi rettili, breve brano strumentale di fattura jazz-rock". That is one track, so it goes after the main genres.
- **energy 4**: OR-D calls L'evoluzione the "vertice assoluto da un punto di vista della complessità musicale" and speaks of "la sfuriata prog di Cento mani e cento occhi". OR-BM hears "Hammond, moog e synth in dialogo frenetico nei primi brani". The piano ballad keeps it below 5.
- **electronic 2**: a rock band with heavy keyboards. OR-D mentions a "tripudio di synth e piano", but piano and guitars are also central.
- **mood 1**: OR-D says "Pesa come un macigno il senso di fatica di un percorso evolutivo fatto di sofferenza ... e morte". It calls 750.000 anni fa "il brano più triste e commovente". The album ends in extinction.
- **theatrical 4**: operatic singing (see voice), plus "ritmi da musica circense" in the last track (OR-D).
- **experimental 3**: OR-D calls it "un'opera pionieristica". Nocenzi on OR-D: "siamo stati però sicuramente avanguardia".
- **polish 2**: OR-BM criticises it: "paga purtroppo una produzione non al livello delle composizioni (mixing scadente, registrazione mediocre...)".
- **tempo**: left empty. Frenetic passages and slow ballads alternate, and no source characterises the album as a whole.
- **groove 1**: no source mentions anything danceable. The long, complex suites point to the low end. This is my judgment; empty would also be defensible.
- **voice operatic|powerful**
  - RS-US: "defined by the operatic bellow of Francesco Di Giacomo". The line is written about the band, inside the Io sono nato libero entry.
  - OR-BM: "la bella voce tenorile di Francesco Di Giacomo".
- **instruments**: DG credits D'Angelo (Fender bass, double bass), Calderoni (drums, timpani), Todaro (electric and acoustic guitar), V. Nocenzi (organ, harpsichord, synthesizer) and G. Nocenzi (piano, piccolo, given here as flute). OR-D names "il piano e l'hammond".
- **production moog**: OR-BM, "utilizzo copioso di Hammond, moog e synth".
- **lyrics**
  - storytelling: the concept runs from the birth of the Earth to extinction (OR-D).
  - love: "750.000 anni fa… l'amore?" (OR-D).
  - social: "primi esperimenti di socializzazione", the tension between individual freedom and community (OR-D).
- **producers**: DG credits "Produzione Diretta Da: Alessandro Colombini". "Dischi Ricordi" is credited as Producer, but it is the label company, so it was dropped.
- **sounds_like**
  - Emerson, Lake & Palmer (Tarkus) [2/7]: OR-BM, "leggere assonanze stilistiche con la suite Tarkus degli Emerson, Lake & Palmer ... nei primi brani". The first two tracks are L'evoluzione and La conquista. OR-D also calls one passage "prog emersoniano".
  - Gentle Giant [1/7]: OR-D says "Cento mani e cento occhi ricorda i Gentle Giant sia nei cori che nei virtuosismi". OR-BM agrees: "richiama i migliori Gentle Giant".
- **influenced_by**: empty. No album-specific statement was found. Artist-level influences are in artists.md.
- **landmark 1**: OR-D calls it "emblematico di tutte queste caratteristiche" and "un'opera pionieristica". It is also an OndaRock pietra miliare and #92 in RSI-100.

## banco-iosononatolibero (Io sono nato libero, 1973)

- **genres**
  - prog: RS-US says "the album represents Rock Progressivo Italiano at its purest". Discogs gives Rock / Prog Rock.
  - symphonicrock: RS-US speaks of "the 15-minute symphonic-rock pummeling of Canto Nomade". OR-BM places that suite among the one-side tracks "del rock sinfonico".
  - ballad: RS-US calls Non Mi Rompete a "serene ballad", and OR-BM an "allegra ballata". It was the single (Discogs master 820641).
  - psychedelic: OR-BM, "Puro delirio psichedelico" for La città sottile, 1 of 5 tracks.
- **energy 3**: the suite is a "pummeling" (RS-US), but the album also has a "serene ballad".
- **electronic 2**: keyboards, but the album is guitar- and piano-led as well.
- **mood 2**
  - Dark side: OR-BM describes the condemned prisoner waiting for execution, reflections on war, "cantare sofferto". RK73 adds the Chile coup.
  - Light side: one "allegra ballata".
- **theatrical 4**: "operatic bellow" (RS-US).
- **experimental 3**: La città sottile is psychedelic (OR-BM). RS-US hears "refined arrangements".
- **polish 3**: RS-US says it was "perfected ... with cleaner production" than Darwin!.
- **tempo**: empty. **groove 1**: as for Darwin!.
- **voice operatic|powerful**: same evidence as Darwin!. OR-BM adds "il cantare sofferto di Di Giacomo".
- **instruments**: DG credits Maltese (acoustic and electric guitar), Todaro (guitar), D'Angelo (listed as "Guitar" on this release, so bass is not entered), Calderoni (drums, percussion), Perosa and Aliotta (percussion), V. Nocenzi (organ, harpsichord, synthesizer) and G. Nocenzi (piano, keyboards). OR-BM mentions "romantici passaggi di piano" and "una lunga coda strumentale di chitarre ritmiche e percussioni".
- **lyrics political|social**: RK73, "i testi aprono le porte sul colpo di stato in Cile, si soffermano sul senso della libertà e sull'antimilitarismo". OR-BM: "Opera di denuncia politica".
- **producers**: Alessandro Colombini (DG). DG also says it was recorded in October 1973 at Studi Fonorama, Milan.
- **sounds_like**: Alan Sorrenti (Come un vecchio incensiere all'alba di un villaggio deserto) [1/5]. OR-BM says the coda of Canto nomade has "chitarre ritmiche e percussioni che echeggiano l'intro di 'Come un vecchio incensiere all'alba di un villaggio deserto' di Alan Sorrenti". That is the title track of Sorrenti's 1973 album. OR-BM's comparison with Lizard, Supper's Ready and Plague of the Lighthouse Keeper is about rank, not sound, so it was not used.
- **landmark 1**: RS-US says it "perfected the approach". OR-BM calls Canto nomade "il loro capolavoro".

## leorme-felonaesorona (Felona e Sorona, 1973)

- **genres**
  - prog: Discogs Rock / Prog Rock. OR-O: "Felona e Sorona: cosmologia del prog italiano". RSI-prog lists it among the 20 essential Italian prog albums.
  - symphonicrock: OR-O calls the trilogy "Collage, Uomo di pezza e Felona e Sorona, vertice aureo del rock sinfonico italiano". OR-O also says "Sospesi nell'incredibile introduce ... con una vera e propria ouverture rock".
  - folk: "Felona si presenta come una luminosa ballata acustica e folk" (OR-O), one short section.
  - avantgarde: "Attesa inerte, dove i sintetizzatori e il Moog di Pagliuca esplorano territori cosmici e ruggenti dissonanze d'avanguardia" (OR-O), one section.
- **energy 3**: a "progressivo accumulo di tensione" and a closing "cataclisma sonoro monolitico e travolgente", set against a solo-piano piece and ballads (OR-O).
- **electronic 3**: keyboard trio: "Hammond e basso", "Moog dissonante", "i sintetizzatori e il Moog di Pagliuca" (OR-O). The keyboards lead more than in an average band.
- **mood 2**: light against darkness. Sorona is "immersa in scure tonalità minori", and the record ends in "monumentale conflagrazione apocalittica" (OR-O).
- **theatrical 4**: RSI-prog speaks of "afflato cosmico fatto di grandiose atmosfere tastieristiche", and OR-O of an "imponente suite continua".
- **experimental 3**: the continuous suite was born from an hour of free improvisation in the studio, and it includes avant-garde dissonances (OR-O).
- **polish 3**: OR-O says producer Reverberi was "decisivo nel rifinire le giunzioni ... donando perfetto ordine architettonico all'album". This reads as a normal, careful studio record.
- **tempo**: empty. **groove 1**: no dance elements are mentioned. It is a "ritmi sincopati" keyboard suite.
- **voice clear|soft**: "La voce limpida di Tagliapietra" (OR-O) and "l'aggraziata voce di Aldo Tagliapietra" (RSI-prog). OR-AT (Sechi) mentions "il suggestivo falsetto che rese particolari Le Orme", but that is about the band in general, so falsetto was not entered.
- **instruments**: DG credits Pagliuca (keyboards), Tagliapietra (voice, bass, guitar) and Dei Rossi (drums, percussion). OR-O adds Hammond (organ), Moog and synthesisers, "sorretta dal solo pianoforte" (piano) and "campane tubolari" (tubularbells).
- **production moog**: OR-O, as quoted above.
- **lyrics**
  - storytelling: two twin planets (OR-O).
  - social: "una serie di metafore sulla diseguaglianza" (RSI-prog).
  - introspection: "riflessione esistenziale" (OR-O).
- **producers**: Gian Piero Reverberi (DG; OR-O).
- **sounds_like / influenced_by**: empty. No critic compares this album's sound to another act, and no artist statement is specific to it. Peter Hammill rewrote the lyrics for the English version (OR-O), which is a different release.
- **landmark 1**: OR-O says "il trio tocca l'apice della propria ambizione". Tagliapietra (OR-AT): "Credo che Felona e Sorona rappresenti al meglio la nostra carriera".

## jannacci-vengoanchionotuno (Vengo anch'io. No, tu no, 1968)

**This row is thin. Confidence is low.** I found no critic's review of the LP as a whole. The sources describe its songs, arrangements and success, but not its sound. Most scales are left empty on purpose.

- **genres pop**: Discogs genre "Pop", and LB ends its page with "Genre pop". No source names another genre for this LP. Il Post's "capolavoro jazz" refers to Quelli che… (1975).
- **instruments orchestra**: DG credits "Luis Enriquez E La Sua Orchestra" on 9 of 11 tracks. LB: "Arrangements by Luis Bacalov except for 'Vengo anch io no tu no' and 'Giovanni telegrafista'".
- **production choirvocals**: DG credits "I Cantori Moderni di Alessandroni" (choir) on Domenica 24 marzo (B2), 1 of 11 tracks.
- **lyrics**
  - LB on Ho visto un re: "a sarcastic mockery of power".
  - RK-J on Vengo anch'io: the theme of the excluded outsider ("il tipico testo ... dell'Escluso", quoting Gianfranco Manfredi).
  - LB on Giovanni telegrafista: love seen "for a brief moment", the story of a telegrapher.
  - Hence irony, social, political, storytelling and love.
- **voice**: left empty. Jannacci on the title song (RK-J, from L'Europeo 1968): "da uno che fa la faccia da deficiente e strilla come un cane sgozzato". That covers one song, and no voice token fits it exactly.
- **language it**: the titles and LB point to Italian. LB says Hai pensato mai is a "language version of a song from Veneto" (Lino Toffolo); whether any dialect is sung was not verified.
- **producers**: Nanni Ricordi (DG). RK-J: "La canzone, come tutto il nuovo album, fu prodotta da Nanni Ricordi". Mixing by Lilli Greco is not a production credit.
- **landmark 0**: no source calls the LP defining. RK-J's "stavolta Jannacci fa boom" is about the single.
- Bacalov is in the catalogue (`bacalov`), but he is the album's arranger, so per the collaborator rule he gets no link.

## bennato-burattinosenzafili (Burattino senza fili, 1977)

- **genres**
  - pop and songwriter: OR-B genre tag "pop, songwriter".
  - canzonedautore: OR-B, the record "resta un modello di cantautorato anticonvenzionale e creativo".
  - rockandroll and poprock: Discogs styles "Rock & Roll, Pop Rock". OR-EB also speaks of "omaggi al rock delle origini (In prigione in prigione, Il Gatto e la Volpe)".
  - bubblegumpop: Il Gatto e la Volpe, the hit, is an "irresistibile bubblegum pop modellato sul rock americano" (OR-B), so it is placed fairly high.
  - ballad: "dolci ballate (È stata tua la colpa, La Fata e Quando sarai grande)" (OR-EB), 3 of 8 tracks.
  - blues: "il blues, prima acustico e poi elettrico, di Tu Grillo Parlante" (OR-B), 1 track.
  - folk: "alle tradizioni popolari italiane (Dotti, medici e sapienti e Mangiafuoco)" (OR-EB), 2 tracks.
- **energy 3**: soft openers and ballads ("incipit sommesso ma struggente"), but also "sfrenato rock pianistico" and a closing "sarabanda" (OR-B).
- **electronic 1**: acoustic guitar, harmonica, piano, strings and horns. Only "qualche riverbero elettrico" and electric blues are mentioned (OR-B).
- **mood 3**: songs that are "struggente" and "commovente" set against "sarcasmo tagliente" and an irresistible pop hit (OR-B).
- **theatrical 3**: OR-B notes "un sottofondo di archi da accompagnamento teatrale" and a style "sempre sopra le righe", within a song-form record.
- **experimental 1**: OR-EB says it is "più compatto dei precedenti", and the record "devoto alla forma-canzone".
- **polish 3**: a normal studio record with string and horn arrangements by Antonio Sinagra (DG).
- **tempo**: empty. **groove 2**: two rock'n'roll numbers, the rest ballads.
- **voice**: empty. No source describes Bennato's singing on this album. OR-EB's description of his "singhiozzi" refers to his second LP.
- **instruments**
  - From DG credits: Bennato (vocals, harmonica, guitar), Bob Fix (alto and tenor sax), Gigi De Rienzo (bass), Roberto Ciotti (dobro), Tony Di Mauro (guitar), Tony Esposito (percussion), Ernesto Vitolo (piano), and string and horn writing by Antonio Sinagra.
  - From OR-B: È stata tua la colpa is "giocato quasi esclusivamente su chitarra acustica, armonica e le percussioni del fedele Tony Esposito". Quando sarai grande opens on "pochi accordi di pianoforte" and swells with "i fiati di Robert Fix". Tu Grillo Parlante has an electric part.
- **production**: stringarrangements and hornarrangements, from DG "Scrittura Archi E Fiati: Antonio Sinagra".
- **lyrics**
  - storytelling: the Pinocchio concept.
  - social and political: OR-B lists "il potere, l'industria discografica, la situazione femminile, l'intellighenzia ... l'ipocrisia degli adulti".
  - irony: "le armi del sarcasmo tagliente" (OR-B).
  - Bennato (OR-EBI) says he "ribaltai completamente la morale della favola".
- **producers**: Alessandro Colombini and Edoardo Bennato. DG credits "Produzione: Alessandro Colombini, Edoardo Bennato".
- **sounds_like**: Jerry Lee Lewis [1/8]. OR-B: "In Prigione, in Prigione sfoggia uno sfrenato rock pianistico alla Jerry Lee Lewis".
- **influenced_by**: empty for the album. Artist-level influences are in artists.md.
- **landmark 1**: OR-B says the Pinocchio concept "resterà il vertice della sua produzione". OR-EB: "il capolavoro definitivo".
- **legacy 3**: SOR (editorial text): "ha costituito una svolta vera nel concept narrativo della musica cantautorale, capace di cambiare l'approccio e il modo di comporre".
  - Not used: the same article quotes Sony's Paolo Maiorino ("ha stabilito un nuovo parametro"). That is a label value judgment, so it is not evidence.
  - The value is only 3 because the source speaks of a change in approach but names no later records or artists.

## Reception reasoning (reception.csv)

- **Darwin!**: importance 4 (the second album, and "emblematic"), acclaim 5 (RSI-100 #92, OndaRock pietra miliare, Cristadoro's top 100), mainstream 2 (cult and avant-garde prog; it peaked only at 21).
- **Io sono nato libero**: importance 4, acclaim 5 (RS-US top 50 of all prog), mainstream 2.
- **Felona e Sorona**: importance 5 (the band's own pick and its international breakthrough on Charisma), acclaim 4 (RSI-prog and Cristadoro, but it is not in RSI-100, where Uomo di pezza is listed), mainstream 2 (a concept suite, even though it reached No. 2).
- **Vengo anch'io. No, tu no**: importance 4 (the commercial breakthrough), acclaim left empty (no critical ranking found), mainstream 3 (popular auteur songs).
- **Burattino senza fili**: importance 5, acclaim 4 (an OndaRock pietra miliare, but it is missing from RSI-100, which lists I buoni e i cattivi), mainstream 3 (an auteur record with a radio hit).
