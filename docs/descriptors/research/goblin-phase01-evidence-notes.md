# Goblin — Phase 01 | evidence notes

Research checked 26 October 2026. Descriptors describe **original album configurations**, not later expansions, anniversary reconstructions, re-recordings or contemporaneous film editions. The numeric scales are editorial judgments anchored to the repository guide, **not measured data**. Each rating uses the album-specific critical evidence below. Low-confidence claims are excluded instead of backfilled by association.

## goblin-profondorosso — *Profondo rosso* (1975)

- **Identity, billing, history:** Original seven-track 1975 Cinevox soundtrack, rather than expanded 28-track CD or a modern Claudio Simonetti’s Goblin recording. Fandome id retained. The original LP blends three Goblin originals with reworked Giorgio Gaslini material; Gaslini is **a composer**, not a producer or a member of the band. [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/release/34ac8454-c210-4765-8bc8-638f471b9b83); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [Terra di Goblin](https://www.terradigoblin.it/photo-gallery/profondo-rosso/).
- **Genres:** Prog/rock, jazz and hard-rock/metal pressure arise in reviews of the 1975 album. Jazz-rock is particularly identified in “Deep Shadows”; funk and the critic’s unusual “gothic-prog” expression are secondary textures. **New tokens** `jazzrock`, `heavymetal` and `gothicprog` preserve terms used by the critics, instead of silently turning jazz-rock into jazz-fusion or heavy metal into generic metal. Not all are dominant. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/profondo-rosso-original-score--mw0001920058); [Head Heritage](https://www.headheritage.co.uk/unsung/reviews/goblin-profondo-rosso); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Scales:** Energy 4 follows emphatic themes, aggressive drumming and sharp dynamics. Electronic 2 reflects a live rock core with organs, synth and Minimoog, far from a wholly electronic record. Mood 1 is largely threatening, relieved by jazz and quieter cues. Theatrical 4 arises from the horror film’s dramatic themes; experimental 3 from metric changes and unusual studio sound work. Polish 3 denotes clear period studio recording; tempo 3 and groove 2 reflect varied pacing with recurring pulse, not dance-first purpose. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/profondo-rosso-original-score--mw0001920058); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Instruments:** OndaRock explicitly names organ, Minimoog, harpsichord, guitar, bass, drums, sax, piano and flute in the original music; archival personnel confirm Simonetti, Morante, Pignatelli, Walter Martino and track-specific guest sax/drum contributions. Do **not** move the celesta of the later *School at Night* alternate version into the 1975 LP. Wind instruments are minor tracks and placed last. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [archive](https://www.terradigoblin.it/photo-gallery/profondo-rosso/).
- **Production:** `minimoog` appears explicitly for the famous title melody. `sonicmanipulation` tracks the described use of altered guitar-string damping and deliberately made noises. Neither tape format nor specific mixing console is verified for this original LP. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Voice, lyrics, language:** Instrumental original album, no sung-language/lyric themes entered. Expanded editions include cues not part of the seven-track reference album.
- **Producers:** blank. The evidence identifies Argento as director/collaborator and Gaslini as composer; these roles are NOT sufficient for the producer field. Later re-recording metadata attributes producer roles to a different incarnation and is excluded.
- **Sounds-like / influences:** Critic comparisons to King Crimson, Can and the *Tubular Bells*-era Mike Oldfield justify `sounds_like`. OndaRock reports Argento requesting a *Tubular Bells*-type segment: this does not turn an artist resemblance into a documented album-wide influence. `influenced_by` remains blank. [Head Heritage](https://www.headheritage.co.uk/unsung/reviews/goblin-profondo-rosso); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Landmark and confidence:** 1/high. The title score established the Goblin/Argento association, well documented by first-person and archival histories. Some sources disagree on minor personnel and count of Gaslini compositions; defer to the original-track account and specify scope. [RBMA](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/claudio-simonetti-gothic-disco-godfather/); [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/release/34ac8454-c210-4765-8bc8-638f471b9b83).

## goblin-roller — *Roller* (1976)

- **Identity:** Six-track instrumental stand-alone studio LP, NOT a soundtrack, even though several compositions were later reused in Romero’s *Martin/Wampyr*. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/roller-mw0000341984); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Genres:** Core instrumental/symphonic prog and progressive rock, jazz-rock influence and a focused funk excursion in “Snip Snap”; album publicity calls out psychedelia, but this is a texture. **New token** `instrumentalrock` comes from label tagging, which is not a substitute for `prog`. Generic `soundtrack` and film-music retailer tags excluded as an album-type error. [label Bandcamp](https://goblinrockband.bandcamp.com/album/roller); [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/roller-mw0000341984); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Scales:** Energy 3 (epic passages balanced with quiet acoustic material); electronic 2 (Moog synths alongside guitars, acoustic instruments and acoustic drums); mood 2 (ominous but with serene “Aquaman”); theatrical 4 (large-scale progressive arrangements); experimental 3 (jazz/funk turns in a largely structured prog LP); polish 3; tempo 3; groove 2 (one overtly funky track does not define the whole disc). [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/roller-mw0000341984).
- **Instruments:** Original-lineup credits establish Morante electric/acoustic guitar, Pignatelli bass, Marangolo drums/percussion, Simonetti organ/piano/Clavinet/Minimoog, Guarini Moog/piano/electric piano and clarinet. OndaRock describes sax in “Il risveglio del serpente”, so `saxophone` stays at the tail and needs confirmation in first-pressing track credits. The 2025 reissue text mentions Mellotron, but detailed 1976 original instrument credits do not establish this model: **omit Mellotron** rather than infer from a later marketing description. [Strawberry Bricks](https://www.strawberrybricks.com/guide/releases/roller); [Clockwork Peach](https://prog.clockworkpeach.jp/item/200093); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [label](https://goblinrockband.bandcamp.com/album/roller).
- **Production:** `minimoog|moog` are explicit model/family credits for separate players. `clavinet` belongs in instruments. Engineering by Giorgio Agazzi and artistic coordination by Carlo Bixio do **not** establish either as album producer. [Strawberry Bricks](https://www.strawberrybricks.com/guide/releases/roller).
- **Voices/lyrics/language:** `instrumental`; no lyric theme or language. Producers and album-specific influences blank for lack of qualifying attribution. `sounds_like` uses critic-linked King Crimson and Pink Floyd affinities and a weaker Yes comparison; this is resemblance, not influence documentation. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [Strawberry Bricks](https://www.strawberrybricks.com/guide/releases/roller).
- **Landmark and confidence:** 1/high for historical position as defining independent studio LP. Critical differences: some treat it as a self-contained prog work, while the dark, cinematic mood connects it to the scores. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/roller-mw0000341984).

## goblin-suspiria — *Suspiria* (1977)

- **Identity:** Original eight-track 1977 Goblin/Cinevox soundtrack, not Claudio Simonetti’s Goblin’s anniversary remake or expanded modern issues. [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/); [official history](https://www.goblinofficial.com/biography.php).
- **Genres:** Electronic/prog core; dark ambient, minimalism and avant-garde sonic work; world-music, folk, krautrock and drone elements; funk, disco and jazz are *track-level* (“Markos”, “Black Forest”, “Blind Concert”) and intentionally ranked last. `ambient` is a genuine descriptive label, not just the fact that film music is atmospheric. `soundtrack` is type, not musical genre. [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/); [GDM](https://www.archiviocolonnesonore.com/goblin/biografia/).
- **Scales:** Energy 3 balances bursts of ritual drumming with extended ominous textural passages; electronic 3 signifies modular Moog, organ/Mellotron and synthesised noise mixed with guitars, tabla, bouzouki and acoustic drums. Mood 0 is overwhelmingly terrifying and ritualistic. Theatrical 5 and experimental 5 follow the deliberate theatrical screaming/whispers, atypical instruments and manipulation; polish 3 for period-recorded score; tempo 2 and groove 1 because most movements are slow or atmospheric though one cue briefly uses disco-funk. [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/); [FACT](https://www.factmag.com/2014/10/31/suspiria-is-the-masterpiece-of-goblin-claudio-simonetti-reflects-on-the-best-horror-soundtrack-of-all-time/).
- **Instruments:** Detailed LP credits name Moog/Minimoog, Mellotron, organ, celesta, electric/acoustic guitar, bouzouki, tabla, drums/percussion, bass, electric and acoustic piano. OndaRock also documents the vibraphone music-box line (`mallets`) and an occasional sax. Simonetti says a sequencer was deployed, included under instruments rather than erroneously as production token. [Strawberry Bricks](https://strawberrybricks.com/guide/releases/suspiria); [Style Weekly](https://www.styleweekly.com/interview-goblin-founder-claudio-simonetti-talks-about-his-pioneering-work-on-the-original-suspiria-soundtrack/); [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/).
- **Production:** `sonicmanipulation` supported by Simonetti's accounts of recording self-made noises and studio experimentation; `moog` and `minimoog` identify confidently supported synthesiser family/model. **A modular model disagreement is documented:** FACT says *System 50*, while Style Weekly and another interview say *System 55*. Do not enter `moogsystem55` or `moogsystem50` pending primary documentation; no invented model alias. [FACT](https://www.factmag.com/2014/10/31/suspiria-is-the-masterpiece-of-goblin-claudio-simonetti-reflects-on-the-best-horror-soundtrack-of-all-time/); [Style Weekly](https://www.styleweekly.com/interview-goblin-founder-claudio-simonetti-talks-about-his-pioneering-work-on-the-original-suspiria-soundtrack/).
- **Vocals/language/lyrics:** Instrumental score with whisper-spoken English “witch”, cries and chant-like ensembles. `instrumental|spoken|choral` captures these limited additions, without pretending the album has lead song lyrics; language remains empty, as an isolated utterance is not a main singing language. [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/); [official site](https://www.goblinofficial.com/biography.php).
- **Production personnel disputes:** Guarini claims participation; Simonetti denies he played on the final score, while an OndaRock retrospective includes him. Leave his role unresolved in notes; he is not listed as producer either way. Argento served as an artistic advisor, not independently verified as a credited record producer. [FACT](https://www.factmag.com/2014/10/31/suspiria-is-the-masterpiece-of-goblin-claudio-simonetti-reflects-on-the-best-horror-soundtrack-of-all-time/); [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/).
- **Sounds-like / influence:** OndaRock compares Kraftwerk to the electronic patches; this belongs in `sounds_like`, *not* `influenced_by` because it cites demo instructions and perceived resemblance rather than a clearly credited musical model shaping the released score. **Emerson, Lake & Palmer** is documented influence: Simonetti explicitly says his admiration for Keith Emerson led him to use the big Moog for this album. [OndaRock](https://ondarock.com/reviews/italian-milestones/goblin-suspiria/); [FACT](https://www.factmag.com/2014/10/31/suspiria-is-the-masterpiece-of-goblin-claudio-simonetti-reflects-on-the-best-horror-soundtrack-of-all-time/).
- **Landmark and confidence:** 1/high; multiple contemporary-history interviews and decades of critical assessment independently identify it as a defining horror score. High applies despite the contained model/personnel dispute, which is not forced into the row.

## goblin-ilfantasticoviaggiodelbagarozzomark — *Il fantastico viaggio del bagarozzo Mark* (1978)

- **Identity:** Original eight-track 1978 standalone studio LP. Some tracks were reused in *Martin/Wampyr* and *L’altro inferno*; that reuse doesn't transform the album into a soundtrack. The 13 October 2026 Bandcamp date is a DIGITAL REISSUE, not first publication. [Bandcamp](https://goblinrockband.bandcamp.com/album/il-fantastico-viaggio-del-bagarozzo-mark); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Genres:** Mostly prog rock, with symphonic prog, selected hard-rock passages and jazz/blues inflections; reviewer points to occasional electronic updates. The album is not consistently psychedelic, so generalized artist tags are excluded. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/il-fantastico-viaggio-del-bagarozzo-mark-mw0000342684); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [AOTY](https://www.albumoftheyear.org/album/74965-goblin-il-fantastico-viaggio-del-bagarozzo-mark.php).
- **Scales:** Energy 3 across active rhythm tracks and quieter/lyrical detours; electronic 2 because keys/synth coexist with a traditional band; mood 2 combines fairy-tale fantasy with darker anxieties; theatrical 4 (narrative concept and expansive prog); experimental 3 (varied styles, less radical than Suspiria); polish 3; tempo 3, groove 2. This set is a sourced editorial judgment, not a claim of measured bpm. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Instruments:** Original LP credits establish Morante guitar, Pignatelli bass, Marangolo drums and percussion, Simonetti keyboards; other reviews name piano, synth and Mellotron, and archival credits name Antonio Marangolo on saxophone. A source listing generic 'guitar' does not justify splitting into *electricguitar* and *acousticguitar* for this record. Mellotron is from weaker retailer prose, so confidence medium rather than high. [Clockwork Peach](https://prog.clockworkpeach.jp/item/201031); [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/il-fantastico-viaggio-del-bagarozzo-mark-mw0000342684).
- **Production:** blank; Giorgio Agazzi recorded, Gaetano Ria mixed, Arun Chakraverty transferred the recording. All are engineering/transfer roles. No source confidently gives record-production techniques or credited producer on the original LP; none are inferred merely because a synthesizer is present. [Clockwork Peach](https://prog.clockworkpeach.jp/item/201031).
- **Voice/lyrics/language:** Original vocals by Morante, in Italian. The guide’s restricted voice list lacks an evidenced neutral 'sung' category and reviewers describe his voice as awkward rather than technically raspy, clear or powerful. **Leave `voice` empty instead of inventing a timbre.** Themes `storytelling|social` refer to fairy-tale plot and documented drug allegory. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/); [Goblin official site](https://www.goblinofficial.com/biography.php).
- **Producers and associations:** Empty `producers` and `influenced_by`. Critic compares parts of the music to Emerson, Lake & Palmer, therefore `sounds_like` only; *Yellow Submarine* refers to a possible illustrated-film **concept** and is NOT a documented musical influence. [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).
- **Landmark/confidence:** `0`/medium. An important cult studio record but not as universally defining as the two Argento scores or *Roller*; reviews disagree on vocals and artistic success. Its cultural significance as a cult record should not force a landmark flag by itself. [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/album/il-fantastico-viaggio-del-bagarozzo-mark-mw0000342684); [OndaRock](https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/goblin/).

## Deliberate absences and open questions

- All four `producers` cells blank: historical credit listings are too inconsistent to responsibly identify LP producers. The band, composer, arranger, artistic director, engineer, mixer, film producer, executive and label manager are not interchangeable roles.
- Album-specific `influenced_by` is filled only for *Suspiria*. General band influences are separately documented in `CLAUDE_IMPORT.md`.
- Machine-disagreement rule enforced. No Moog System 50 / System 55 model token is proposed for import.
- New genre tokens need adding to `scenes.ts`, `genreFamilies.ts` and the validator's vocabulary *before* descriptor validation. No unsupported new instrument/production tokens were introduced.
- `La via della droga` music dates from 1977, but AMS says official full soundtrack release waited until the 1990s; date-as-film-year is not reliably original LP year. Verify first album issue before incorporating it among studio-era LPs. [AMS](https://ams-music.it/wp/la-via-della-droga-crystal-vinyl/?lang=it).
- `Squadra antimafia` original score is reportedly lost, and a modern cover recording is billed to Girodivite. A film credit is not a Goblin album release; exclude modern re-recordings from the original Goblin discography. [Beat Records reissue notice](https://www.soundtrackcorner.de/squadra-antimafia-performed-by-girodivite-p9434.htm).
