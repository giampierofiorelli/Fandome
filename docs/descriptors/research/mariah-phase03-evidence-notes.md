# Mariah Carey — Fandome Phase 03 evidence notes (2002–2009)

Research verified against `descriptors` branch on 2026-10-25. Exactly four new album rows; Phase 01/02 rows are *not* repeated. The 0–5 values below are editorial inferences from independent signed reviews and track-by-track credits, **not measured acoustic features or critics' scores**. All values are proposals, `status=draft`.

## Scoring anchors and voice taxonomy

- `mood`: 0 dark through 5 euphoric; **do not** equate rhythm with happiness. `energy` and `groove` summarize whole-album propulsion and dance-floor focus respectively.
- `voice`: `soft` includes breathy, whispered passages; `powerful` covers forceful belting; `raspy` only when album criticism identifies audible grit. `rap` here flags significant *credited guest verses* and **does not mean Mariah herself raps**. A high whistle register is not a separate allowed token; distinguish it in notes.
- `electronic`: production using synthesized timbres, drum machines, processed loops; recording digitally does not alone count. The album balances must be judged across the original tracklist.
- `producers` includes producers and explicitly credited co-/additional producers on the original core album; excludes mere writers, mixing engineers, featured artists and remixers. Artist Mariah is a credited producer on tracks, not an inference based on celebrity.
- `sounds_like` and `influenced_by` are intentionally empty: critical evocations of Diana Ross/Stylistics etc. and samples or covers are NOT direct documented artist influences for the full album. Do not auto-promote those to causal links.

## Charmbracelet (2002) — `mariah-charmbracelet`

**Evidence**: `C_SL`, `C_GUA`, `C_AM`, `C_Q`, `C_BB`, `C_AOTY`, `C_SH`, `C_MB`.

- Genres: conventional pop/contemporary R&B and soul alongside hip-hop features; neo-soul pertains notably to Dre & Vidal’s two productions. Gospel is clearly present on *My Saving Grace*; the Def Leppard cover is a rock *source song*, rearranged for this album, so rock as genre is **peripheral** and needs review. Track-level mapping is in genre_evidence. `hiphopsoul` and `nusoul` partly rely on discographic taxonomy plus named critic.
- `energy=2`, `tempo=2`, `groove=2`: expansive reflective ballads and a minority of more driving rap-linked cuts; not club-forward.
- `electronic=2`, `polish=4`: drum tracks and pop-R&B programming coexist with guitar, keyboards and documented live arrangement; editorial balance, not extracted waveform metrics.
- `mood=2`: bereavement (*Sunflowers for Alfred Roy*), vulnerability (*Through the Rain*), resilience and gospel hope. Emotional shading predominates over upbeat moments.
- `theatrical=3`, `experimental=2`: dramatic voice and genre changes but standard R&B structures; moderate evidence, re-evaluate after independent listening or additional review.
- `voice=soft|powerful|raspy|rap`: predominantly whispered/breathy and sometimes belted lead; rasp is a less certain timbral qualifier; rap applies to guests Cam’ron, Jay-Z etc., not Mariah.
- Producers: track credits Qobuz plus original Billboard interview. *Just Black* in one 2002 press archive conflicts with the explicitly credited **Just Blaze**: use Just Blaze. James 'Big Jim' Wright and Bryan-Michael Cox are documented co-producers. `7 Aurelius` and I.G. on *Subtle Invitation*; co-/producer roles are separate from songwriter roles.
- Critical disagreement: Slant praises organic, soulful warmth, AllMusic and Guardian are much less enthusiastic. Quality evaluations must not be converted into sonic values.
- Original program: 15 tracks including closing *Through the Rain* remix; a remix does not define original album sonic profile.

## The Emancipation of Mimi (2005) — `mariah-theemancipationofmimi`

**Evidence**: `M_PF`, `M_SL`, `M_Q`, `M_AMUS`, `M_WP`, `M_MB`, `M_CAREY`.

- `rnb|contemporaryrnb|pop|hiphop|hiphopsoul|soul` describe the two substantial interwoven poles; gospel on *Fly Like a Bird*, club pop mainly on selected tracks. Do **not** tag original 14 songs from bonus *Don't Forget About Us*.
- `energy=3`, `tempo=3`, `groove=3`: uptempo *It's Like That* and *To the Floor*, restrained midtempo *Shake It Off*, ballads *We Belong Together*, *Mine Again*, *Fly Like a Bird*. No full-length dance-floor assertion.
- `electronic=3`, `polish=5`: Atlanta-era drum machines/synths are balanced by sample-led/live-sounding soul with organ, Rhodes, sax and gospel. Tight commercial studio finish; sources do not provide actual synthesizer ratios.
- `mood=3`: simultaneous confidence and celebration, relationship rupture and gospel pleading. 5 would erase the long heartbreak section.
- `theatrical=3`, `experimental=2`: strong emotive peaks but mainstream compact forms.
- `voice=soft|powerful|raspy|rap`: critics discuss restrained, near-whispered pop-R&B vocals alternating with forceful soul belting; guest rappers on multiple tracks. `raspy` supported on *Stay the Night* but peripheral.
- Producer list follows original 14-song Qobuz and release list. Neptunes (Pharrell Williams/Chad Hugo) are a **duo credit**, not two extra contributors; James Poyser, The Legendary Traxster, Scram Jones and Young Genius are separately credited. `Manuel Seal` and `Bryan-Michael Cox` are co-producers; keep role qualification for review.
- 2005 Slant was cooler than the detailed 2024 Pitchfork retrospective, which praises restraint and historic impact; editorial score `landmark=1` is justified by sustained influence, not a borrowed reviewer number.

## E=MC² (2008) — `mariah-emc` (IMPORTANT ID)

**Evidence**: `E_GUA`, `E_SL`, `E_AM`, `E_Q`, `E_HIST`, `E_GUA2`, `E_CRED`.

- **Literal Fandome slug** strips superscript ²: `slug('E=MC²') = 'emc'`, so correct ID is `mariah-emc`, NOT `mariah-emc2`. The title retains `²`. Claude should not silently normalize the title and create two album entities.
- `rnb|contemporaryrnb|pop|hiphop` album-wide dominant; `dancepop` significant, disco mainly *I'm That Chick* / *O.O.C.*, reggae colouring limited to *Cruise Control* (Damian Marley), gospel on *I Wish You Well*. **A featured reggae singer alone does not establish full reggae genre**; keep genre evidence track-level and review.
- `energy=3` versus `groove=4`: more nightclub rhythm and tracks than *Mimi*, but a significant ballad stretch prevents 5s or uniformly fast tempo. `electronic=4`, `polish=5`: reviews describe synth/drum-machine pop/R&B and elaborate vocal layers.
- `mood=3`: sexual playfulness and exuberance compete with the explicit dark *Side Effects*, grief (*Bye Bye*) and earnest spiritual closing. Never use `groove` to set `mood`.
- `voice=soft|powerful|rap`: breathy intimate delivery, climactic belting and guest rap; whistle notes present but not an allowed schema token.
- Original 14-song sequence only, without MC30 add-ons. Credits Qobuz confirmed partly on the remaster; compare original version before final review. Producers DJ Toomp, Stargate, Scott Storch, Swizz Beatz, James Poyser and will.i.am require per-track audit against source E_Q; do not import unsupported contributor roles automatically if reissue metadata varies.
- Critical disagreement: Guardian strongly praises fusion of camp and sentiment, Slant sees excessively manufactured pop.

## Memoirs of an Imperfect Angel (2009) — `mariah-memoirsofanimperfectangel`

**Evidence**: `I_GUA`, `I_SL`, `I_AM`, `I_Q`, `I_Q_INT`.

- A coherent 17-track late-2000s contemporary R&B album, supported by The-Dream and Tricky Stewart production. `hiphop` at the **beat/sampling level**, not guest rapper presence (Slant specifically notes an absence of rap guests). `soul` and `adultcontemporary` are significant secondary styles; gospel finale only. Do not label retro harmony as doo-wop without a specific album review; deliberately omitted.
- `energy=2`, `tempo=2`, `groove=2`: mostly mid-/downtempo layered R&B; *Obsessed* / *Up Out My Face* break up the subdued flow.
- `electronic=3`, `polish=4`: synthetic/sampled beat production and intricate vocal overdubs, but reviews differ over whether the production is polished or cheap/flat. The scoring expresses technical layering, not critical approval.
- `mood=2`: betrayal, grudge, breakup, sarcasm, intimacy; moments of humour do not make it celebratory.
- `theatrical=3`, `experimental=2`: unconventional lyrical jokes and occasional abstract textures, but recurrent standard R&B hooks.
- `voice=soft|raspy|powerful`: Guardian reports layered whisper-like vocals and ragged midrange; forceful delivery on closing Foreigner cover, which alone should not dominate the profile.
- Production credits: Qobuz original songs show Mariah, The-Dream, Tricky Stewart, James Big Jim Wright, Los Da Mystro, Heatmyzer and Randy Jackson (*additional producer* on cover track). No remix CD producer belongs in this row.
- Critical disagreement: Guardian praises abstraction and intimacy; Slant calls much of the production repetitive and cheap. This does not justify an invented acoustics-derived experimental score.

## Candidate genres withheld pending stronger album-specific proof

- `doowop` for Memoirs: retro vocal textures alone do not establish named style.
- `funk` for Mimi: rhythmic vocabulary is insufficient without direct specific classification.
- House versions of any singles, or 2010 bonus mixes, are NOT the standard album's genre.
