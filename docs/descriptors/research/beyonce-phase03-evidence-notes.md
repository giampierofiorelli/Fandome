# Evidence notes — Beyoncé Phase 03 (original-release descriptors only)

## 2019 — *The Lion King: The Gift* (`beyonce-thelionkingthegift`)

- **Eligibility, artist credit, type.** This is a Beyoncé-curated film-companion album **with new recordings** featuring African and American collaborators, not the 1994 soundtrack and not a retrospective of older hits. The 2019 original contains songs plus scene-dialogue interludes. It is classified as `soundtrack` because the project was designed around Disney's 2019 film, even though [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/the-lion-king-the-gift-beyonce/e569fahldj1rb) and some other discography systems use broad `compilation` tags to describe its multiple primary performers. See the [release report](https://pitchfork.com/news/listen-to-beyonces-new-album-the-lion-king-the-gift-with-jay-z-kendrick-blue-ivy-childish-gambino-more/) and [review](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/various-artists-the-lion-king-the-gift/). **Do not import it as a conventional Beyoncé solo studio album or a recycled compilation.** The recommended Fandome `coBilled.ts` credit is `Beyoncé Presents: The Lion King: The Gift` (album's curator credit), with separate featured/main artists reflected by the release metadata, not fabricated as a co-equal single artist. A track-by-track artist model would be more exact than a single co-billed record credit.
- **Sound/genres.** [Pitchfork's close reading](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/various-artists-the-lion-king-the-gift/) details West African Afropop and Afrobeats, South African gqom on *My Power* and *Don't Jealous Me*, house, dancehall on *Already* and a Fela-like Afrobeat feel on Burna Boy's *Ja Ara E*. Afrobeats (contemporary Nigerian pop) and Afrobeat (the older Fela Kuti style) are **different** tokens. All geographic or subgenre tags at the end of the row are **track-scoped**, not claimed as comprehensive African music coverage. Pitchfork points out the absence of Eastern/Northern African featured artists. Film dialogue is not a `spokenword` music genre. Note *Spirit* is the track appearing in the film; other songs are film-inspired rather than film-score cues.
- **Eight scales.** energy **3** (dance tracks balanced with ballads and short interludes); electronic **3** (rhythmic programming plus documented strings, piano and live guitars); mood **4** (celebratory/praise messages, sombre material on *Otherside*); theatrical **4** (cinema-associated dramatic ballads, animated-dialogue passages); experimental **2** (cross-market cultural combination but familiar radio-ready song structures); polish **5** (globally coordinated studio production); tempo **3** (dance cuts and slow songs balance); groove **3** (repeated dancehall/gqom pulse interrupted by ballads and film dialogue). Evidence: [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/various-artists-the-lion-king-the-gift/), [NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/beyonce-lion-king-the-gift-review-2531330). Scores are research judgments, not BPM measurements.
- **Instruments.** [Original Qobuz musician credits](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/the-lion-king-the-gift-beyonce/e569fahldj1rb) explicitly identify a string section on *Bigger*, strings and Jeff Lorber piano on *Spirit*, percussion/drums/keyboards (Labrinth, Ilya) on *Spirit*, Pino Palladino bass on *Brown Skin Girl*, and Rod Castro guitar/Courtney Leonard bass on *Keys to the Kingdom*. No specific synthesizer hardware or drum machine is identified. Thus the `instruments` list avoids model guesses and leaves out imagined African folk instruments.
- **Production.** Qobuz names both Beyoncé and Labrinth/Ilya as *Spirit* programmers; the album also uses an Oumou Sangaré recording as a documented **sample** in *Mood 4 Eva* ([Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/various-artists-the-lion-king-the-gift/); [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/the-lion-king-the-gift-beyonce/e569fahldj1rb)). `programming|sampling` is documented, but string players alone do not establish a credited `stringarrangements` technique. Film interludes are original-dialogue excerpts; they do not, alone, prove `fieldrecordings`.
- **Producer credit distinction.** [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/the-lion-king-the-gift-beyonce/e569fahldj1rb) explicitly labels Beyoncé (many tracks), Derek Dixie (*Bigger*), Bubele Booi and Robert Magwenzi (*Find Your Way Back*), P2J (*Don't Jealous Me*, *Water*, etc.), Sounwave/Keanu Beats/Johnny Kosich (*Nile*), Danja/DJ Khaled (*Mood 4 Eva*), Norhtboi/GuiltyBeatz/P2J/Derek Dixie (*Keys to the Kingdom*), Diplo/Picard Brothers/GuiltyBeatz (*Already*), Nicky Davey (*Otherside*) and DJ Lag (*My Power*) as main or co-producers. *Spirit* credits include Labrinth and Ilya, while *Scar* dialogue features actors, **not** musical producers. Tim Suby, Dave Hamelin and Ari PenSmith are credited on *SCAR* (070 Shake / Jessie Reyez), a separate song from the dialogue interlude *uncle scar*. Neither guest performers nor Beyoncé's album-wide **executive producer** credit alone licenses listing someone as a track producer. [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/release/c92eec0a-e578-40c0-858f-bcf5c72b1cd2) aids role disambiguation. Note that some reprint sites have inconsistent producer-summary rosters; granular Qobuz remains the primary evidence.
- **Lyrics, language, voices.** Identity/solidarity, empowerment, love, ancestry and mortality described by [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/various-artists-the-lion-king-the-gift/); main vocal passages are English, with identifiable Swahili passages in *Spirit* and *Otherside*. `spoken` refers to included film dialogue, `rap` to rap features, without implying Beyoncé sings every track.
- **Influences:** Empty. Fela Kuti is evoked by *Burna Boy's track*, while Oumou Sangaré is **sampled** on *Mood 4 Eva*. Neither independently documents Beyoncé crediting those musicians as album-wide artistic influences. `sounds_like` lists stylistic comparisons, including artists actually contributing to this curated record, **not** influence assertions.
- **Edition caution:** Do not substitute the 2020 deluxe edition or soundtrack to *Black Is King*, and do not treat short dialogue interludes as standalone songs to score.

## 2022 — *Renaissance* (`beyonce-renaissance`)

- **Genres and scoring.** [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/beyonce-renaissance/) describes the club-driven through-sequenced record, its sexuality and Black queer context, with house, disco, techno and ballroom throughout. [Guardian Jul](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2022/jul/29/beyonce-renaissance-review-joyous-soundtrack-to-a-hot-girl-summer) explicitly names house, disco, gqom, Afrobeats, Jersey Club, new jack swing, bounce and trap; [Guardian Aug](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2022/aug/06/beyonce-renaissance-review-a-breathtaking-maximalist-tour-de-force) identifies soul, dancehall and hyperpop. Each specific genre has a scope in the genre-evidence CSV, with gospel solely a sampled **texture** of *Church Girl* rather than the dominant style.
- **Eight scales.** energy **4** (sustained four-to-the-floor and percussive propulsion, dynamics within slower cuts); electronic **4** (drum-machine programming and synths, with several live musicians including Nile Rodgers); mood **4** (liberation and sensual confidence with political framing); theatrical **4** (layered diva delivery, larger-than-life vocal and club staging); experimental **3** (collage-like genre transitions but pop structures); polish **5** (dense multitrack, carefully sewn segues); tempo **4** (mostly up-tempo groove with occasional mid-tempo respite); groove **5** (virtually uninterrupted dancefloor focus). Evidence: [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/beyonce-renaissance/), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2022/aug/06/beyonce-renaissance-review-a-breathtaking-maximalist-tour-de-force).
- **Instruments.** The complete [original track-by-track credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-releases-new-album-renaissance-listen/) identify synths (Mike Dean, The-Dream, BloodPop), drum machine programming (Luke Solomon on *Cozy* and *Alien Superstar*), keys, live guitars (Nile Rodgers and other credited performers), bass, drums, piano, clavinet, horns/sax/trumpet/trombone, percussion (Sheila E.), strings. `drummachine` is supported by credited **drum machine programming**; this does **not** specify a Roland TR-808/909 as a physical machine used on the record. `electricguitar` is named only where guitar credits/performing style support it; the credits say guitar rather than exact acoustic/electric for every cut, so using `guitar` would be the safer generic token if reviewing strict instrument specificity.
- **Production.** [Pitchfork full credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-releases-new-album-renaissance-listen/) list drum programming, synth programming/arrangements (Mike Dean), horn arrangements on *Pure/Honey*, sampling of ballroom recordings, Pro Tools engineering. [Guardian Aug](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2022/aug/06/beyonce-renaissance-review-a-breathtaking-maximalist-tour-de-force) independently describes delicate multi-tracking on *Plastic Off the Sofa*: `layeredvocals`. `protools` is software explicitly credited, not inferred. Do not confuse `samples/interpolations` with direct career influence or include unnamed hardware just because Chicago house is referenced.
- **Producers.** Main and **co-production** credit roles are present in [full credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-releases-new-album-renaissance-listen/) and [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/release/a319ff79-8bd7-4677-b41d-e7ae17920ac4). The list includes Beyoncé; Honey Dijon/Chris Penny/Luke Solomon (*Cozy*, *Alien Superstar*); Kelman Duran/Mike Dean (*I'm That Girl*), NovaWav, Raphael Saadiq/Rissi (*Cuff It*); The-Dream/Tricky Stewart/Jens Christian Isaksen (*Break My Soul*); Skrillex, Al Cres, Beam (*Energy*); Syd (*Plastic Off the Sofa*), Leven Kali (*Virgo's Groove*), P2J/GuiltyBeatz (*Move*), and Beat producers on *Heated*, *Thique*, *All Up in Your Mind*. **Leven Kali is a full producer on *Virgo's Groove* even though his *Plastic Off the Sofa* role is additional-only**. Studio engineers Stuart White, Brandon Harding, John Cranfield and Andrea Roberts do not enter `producers`. Guest vocalists Grace Jones/Tems likewise do not enter `producers` unless separately credited for producing.
- **Influences:** [Guardian's original review](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2022/jul/29/beyonce-renaissance-review-joyous-soundtrack-to-a-hot-girl-summer) explicitly calls parts of the record *Diana Ross-inspired disco*, sufficient for the album-specific `Diana Ross` influence claim under the qualified-critic test. **Donna Summer's *I Feel Love* is sampled/interpolated on *Summer Renaissance*, which by itself does not make Donna Summer an influence claim**; she remains a sonic comparison. Beyoncé's uncle Johnny is an explicitly credited cultural inspiration (see [L.A. Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/music/story/2022-07-28/6-takeaways-beyonce-renaissance)), but is not a musical artist and therefore cannot appear in `influenced_by`.
- **Disagreements/cautions:** Some critics see a celebratory unified dance record; others stress disparate slices of house, new jack swing, hyperpop and Afrobeats. The album genres reflect *all sourced styles*, while **dance/house/disco** carry most algorithmic weight. *Renaissance: Act I* is the same 2022 studio release, and tour recordings should never be added here.

## 2024 — *Cowboy Carter* (`beyonce-cowboycarter`)

- **Genre identity.** [Sony's official label statement](https://www.sonymusic.ca/press_release/beyonce-releases-cowboy-carter) calls it an Americana/country-rooted, deliberately multi-genre album and names blues, zydeco and Black folk traditions. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/beyonce-cowboy-carter/) calls it country-rooted with American folk, hip-hop and rock; [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/03/29/1241672819/beyonce-cowboy-carter-10-takeaways) explicitly identifies bluegrass, surf rock, Jersey club and operatic singing; [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/mar/28/beyonce-cowboy-carter-review-from-hoedown-to-full-blown-genre-throwdown) notes house on *Riiverdance*/*II Hands II Heaven* and bluesy funk on *Oh Louisiana*. Most genres here are **track-scoped**, unlike the overall country/Americana identity. Do not treat every track as uniform modern Nashville country.
- **Eight scales.** energy **3** (slow acoustic meditations, rap bursts, stomps and arena-gospel climaxes); electronic **2** (documented acoustic/roots arrangements predominant, but synths, loops and 808 beats persist); mood **3** (anger, memorial, reassurance, vindication and joy); theatrical **4** (radio-show interludes, vocal extremes, concept sequencing, opera passage); experimental **4** (fluid cross-genre contrasts, recontextualised roots traditions); polish **5** (extensive vocal and instrumental credits, detailed engineering); tempo **3** (ballads and shuffles versus dance/hip-hop numbers); groove **3** (significant dance cuts but not a continuous club album). Evidence: [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/beyonce-cowboy-carter/), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/apr/06/beyonce-cowboy-carter-review-country-music-album).
- **Instruments.** [Official published credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-shares-full-credits-for-new-album-cowboy-carter/) specify acoustic guitars (*II Most Wanted*), banjo/viola (Rhiannon Giddens, *Texas Hold 'Em*), steel guitar (*16 Carriages*, *II Most Wanted*), strings/violin, bass, drums, piano, organ, harmonica (Stevie Wonder and Ross Garren), percussion, synth, sitar (*Ameriican Requiem*), trumpet/brass, Wurlitzer electric piano, handclaps. [Sony](https://www.sonymusic.ca/press_release/beyonce-releases-cowboy-carter) independently names accordion in the album instrumentation; [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/03/29/1241672819/beyonce-cowboy-carter-10-takeaways) describes accordion on *Riiverdance*. Some instruments occur on one song only, but these are cited, not inferred from `country`. Sony also cites a **washboard** and bass ukulele, omitted from main instruments due to limited prominence rather than fabricating sources or tokens.
- **Production.** [Credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-shares-full-credits-for-new-album-cowboy-carter/) document string arranging on *Daughter* and *Flamenco*, sampled earlier recordings on *Blackbiird*/*Spaghettii*, drum programming (*Ya Ya*), digital editing on *The Linda Martell Show*, and explicit Pro Tools engineering throughout. `belting` is tied to [Pitchfork's vocal analysis](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/beyonce-cowboy-carter/) of Beyoncé's performance, not inferred from a general Beyoncé persona. The phrase “808 by The-Dream” for *II Hands II Heaven* doesn't prove the **Roland TR-808** drum machine was used, so the precise `tr808` hardware token is withheld.
- **Producers.** [Full official credits](https://pitchfork.com/news/beyonce-shares-full-credits-for-new-album-cowboy-carter/) separate recorded-track producers and co-producers from additional/guest/writer roles. Example: *Ameriican Requiem* Beyoncé/No ID/Jon Batiste, Tyler Johnson co; *Blackbiird* Beyoncé/Paul McCartney; *Texas Hold 'Em* Beyoncé/Killah B/Nate Ferraro with Raphael Saadiq **co**, Hit-Boy **additional** and excluded on that ground; *Daughter* Beyoncé/Cam/Simon Maartensson, Derek Dixie co; *Spaghettii* Beyoncé/Swizz Beatz, Khirye Tyler co; *II Most Wanted* Beyoncé/Shawn Everett/Michael Pollack/Miley Cyrus; *Ya Ya* Beyoncé/The-Dream with Cadenza/Harry Edwards co; *Tyrant* Dave Hamelin and D.a. Got That Dope. Guest Rhiannon Giddens, Dolly Parton, Willie Nelson, Post Malone, Miley Cyrus and Nile Rodgers do **not** enter `producers` on guest status alone (Miley Cyrus enters only because independently credited as actual producer on *II Most Wanted*).
- **Language.** Predominantly English, with clearly audible and explicitly identified **Italian** classical-aria passage (*Daughter*), hence `en|it` is track-scoped and not a second major album-wide language. The aria supports a track-scoped `opera` genre, but **not** an `operatic` voice classification because formal opera training is not documented. Interludes justify `spoken` as a secondary voice type.
- **Influences.** Beyoncé explicitly says the experience led her to investigate **Black country music history**, without assigning a singular album-wide named artist ([GQ interview](https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/beyonce-knowles-carter-october-2024-cover-interview); [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/the-inspirations-behind-beyonces-genre-defying-album-cowboy-carter); [Sony press release](https://www.sonymusic.ca/press_release/beyonce-releases-cowboy-carter) names film and soundtrack inspirations). **No named artist added**: Linda Martell and Willie Nelson speak as guests; Dolly Parton and the Beatles are covered; Son House/Sister Rosetta Tharpe/Chuck Berry appear in excerpts; none alone demonstrates direct artistic influence. These can be artist-link relations of different types, not `influenced_by`.
- **Editorial disagreement/edition.** Beyoncé's label emphasises country roots, while the [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2024/mar/28/beyonce-cowboy-carter-review-from-hoedown-to-full-blown-genre-throwdown) questions coherence of such a wide-ranging 27-track record; do not collapse the genres or treat marketing category as all 27 tracks. Some early vinyl/CD editions differ from the main **27-track digital original**, and some 2024 credits were published in April after release. Use the fully published standard digital sequence for this descriptor. Exclude derivative recordings and concert versions.

## Cross-phase safeguards

Only new rows `beyonce-thelionkingthegift`, `beyonce-renaissance`, `beyonce-cowboycarter` are present. No Phase 01/02 rows are repeated. All subjective scales are 0–5 whole numbers and subject to editorial checking. All genres have one separate evidence row with a scope. Only the original 2019/2022/2024 releases are considered. New tokens must be imported into the genre `Genre` type and validator before running the validator. No new instrument or production model is claimed without evidence. All descriptors are `draft`.
