## buscaglione-chebambola (1955)
- year 1955: Discogs master 421214, original Cetra DC 6421 78 rpm dated 1955 (r19140109, r27265374); Doppiozero "il primo disco, inciso nel novembre 1955 proprio per la Cetra, dal titolo Che bambola!"; Il Foglio "(Che bambola!, 1955)". Prompt suggested 1956: not supported by the pressings.
- genres swing|jazz|pop: Discogs genres Jazz, Latin, Pop; style Swing (all pressings); Il Foglio on this song: "al broccolamento swing reagisca così: «Poi si mette bene in guardia come Rocky...»". "Latin" left out (vague, no other source). Label text r27265374: "Side A: Canz. ritmo stamper" (= stomp rhythm; no token, not proposed).
- energy 3 / groove 3: label "ritmo stamper" + Il Foglio "broccolamento swing"; moderate, no source says fast.
- mood 4 / lyrics irony|storytelling: Il Foglio retells the story (the doll punches him into a lamppost "come Rocky, il gran campion"); Rockit "una vena ironica e disincantata"; Doppiozero: Chiosso's songs are "veri e propri sketch musicali attraversati da una vena parodistica", "con una storia e dei personaggi, un inizio, uno sviluppo, una fine".
- theatrical 4: Rockit on his Cetra recordings: "più che in uno studio, Fred sembra in un cabaret durante una sua folle nottata"; Doppiozero "quadro spettacolare, oscillante fra avanspettacolo e cinema d'ambiente".
- voice raspy: Doppiozero "La sua voce è bassa e graffiante, piena di fumo e di whisky".
- instruments left empty: no musician credited on the record (Discogs credits writers only). Sound effects (shot, whistle, siren) are described by Doppiozero for Chiosso's sketches in general, not for this song: production left empty.
- landmark 1: Doppiozero "Il successo di Fred esplose di lì a poco con il primo disco ... Che bambola!"; Rockit "canzoni immortali quali «Che bambola!»".
- sounds_like / influenced_by: none documented.
- Reception 5/4/3 (see reception.csv). Not in HPI year-end 1955-57 lists (only Porfirio Villarosa, 1956 #56). Legacy: no source on influence for this song (Rockit's influence quote, Jannacci/Capossela, is about Porfirio Villarosa): no row.

## peppinodicapri-sttropeztwist (1962)
- genres twist|poprock: original label Carisch VCA 26148 "A: Twist B: Twist" (r1063150, label speaks for the artist); Discogs styles Pop Rock, Twist (r812729, r8701566). Il Post: twist "una specie di declinazione del rock and roll" (genre-level, so rockandroll not added).
- energy 4 / groove 5: Il Fatto Quotidiano "autentico ciclone moderno che porta il twist in ogni sala da ballo del paese"; Il Post on the twist dance "ruotando il bacino e i piedi".
- mood, tempo, voice, instruments empty: no source describes them for this recording (TicketOne piece unreadable). Band: "Peppino Di Capri e i suoi Rockers"; written by Giuseppe Faiella (Di Capri) and guitarist Mario Cenci (Discogs).
- landmark 1: named among his signature songs by Il Post, QN ("autore di brani indimenticabili come 'Champagne', 'Roberta' e St Tropez twist'") and L'Espresso ("un'eredità che canzoni come «Champagne», «Luna caprese» e «Saint Tropez Twist» continuano a portare fino a oggi").
- Reception: HPI 1962 #10 (weekly #2); acclaim 3 (no critical judgment found); mainstream 4: it rides the twist craze he had opened with the Chubby Checker cover (QN "A dicembre 1961 lancia il twist in Italia con 'Let's Twist Again' ... Segue 'St. Tropez Twist'"), though self-written.
- Year conflict: Il Post says 1958; all Carisch pressings and FQ say 1962.

## brunomartino-estate (1960)
- genres jazz|chanson|easylistening|pop: Discogs genres Jazz, Pop; styles Chanson (both 1960 pressings), Easy Listening (7MQ 1443); Il Giorno "l'unico nostro standard nel jazz", "Atmosfera jazzy e blu del Bussolotto"; RS Italia "uno degli standard jazz più famosi al mondo".
- mood 1 / lyrics heartbreak|nostalgia: Il Giorno "la malinconia che accompagna la fine di un'estate e di un amore"; RS Italia "Il tema è il dolore di aver condiviso momenti amorosi con una persona che già alla fine dell'estate non c'è più", in a list of "celesti nostalgie" songs whose "boss è da sempre Bruno Martino"; Metropolis "Martino canta ... di un amore perduto associato con tristezza alla stagione estiva".
- instruments orchestra: Discogs r7656461 "Ib Glindemann – Conductor [Orchestra]". Piano not entered: Metropolis's "frasi veloci di stampo jazzistico che Bruno Martino utilizza al pianoforte" is about his music in general.
- landmark 1: Il Giorno (only Italian jazz standard); De Simone's book title "il più famoso standard jazz italiano"; Metropolis "la celeberrima «Estate»".
- energy, tempo, voice empty: no description of the 1960 recording itself found. Confidence low.
- Legacy 4: see legacy.csv (adopted into jazz repertoire: Chet Baker, João Gilberto, Petrucciani per Il Giorno). Note: this is repertoire adoption, as with Carosone in wave 5; the owner may cut it.

## piaf-milord (1959)
- genres chanson: Discogs style Chanson (r927670 and all 1959 pressings); Le Temps: "On parle alors d'un «standard»". (Chanson réaliste: only on Wikipedia, not used.)
- instruments accordion|orchestra / tempo 2: Histoire des chansons: "C'est finalement le tempo lent, habilement contrasté par une orchestration aux accents populaires menée par l'accordéon" (Monnot wrote a slow and a charleston version); Discogs "Robert Chauvigny – Conductor [Orchestre, Direction]". Tempo 2 not 1 because the source frames it as the slower of two versions; Songfacts gives 2/4 metre.
- mood 2: Histoire des chansons: Piaf asked Moustaki for "une chanson mélancolique qui évoquerait un morne dimanche à Londres"; the Olympia audience "retient son souffle face à cette composition poignante".
- lyrics love|storytelling: NPR (Blair 2013): "a song about a working-class girl who falls for an English gentleman".
- voice powerful: NYRB (Robb): "the untrained voice that could penetrate the noise of the street (her «assaultive vibrato,» Carolyn Burke calls it)" (artist-level).
- landmark 1: Histoire des chansons "le plus grand triomphe international de sa carrière discographique"; INA 1977, Moustaki: "Milord was a song made on Piaf like a dress of great couturier" (description only).
- Histoire des chansons is a documented song-history site (record sheet, dates, catalogue numbers) but not a major outlet: confidence low.

## piaf-nonjeneregretterien (1960)
- genres chanson: Discogs style Chanson (r927663); Hewett (Telegraph) calls Piaf the icon of "that unique ... form of French popular song known as chanson".
- energy 4: Unidivers (Bensoussan): "la chanson débute par des accords un-deux-deux-deux, plaqués et uniformes"; "cette chanson martelée"; "la musique, qui martèle sans cesse ce refrain à deux temps, comme un roulement incessant".
- experimental 1 / instruments orchestra|strings / production stringarrangements: Hewett: on paper the song is simple, its harmony nothing like Rossini, "until the final cadence when, for a moment, a Broadway-style glitz bursts through the strings"; "the simplicity of the music leaves all attention on Piaf". Discogs: "Robert Chauvigny – Conductor".
- theatrical 4: Hewett: "Piaf's ecstatic and desperate interpretation speaks to anyone who took risks".
- lyrics introspection: Bensoussan: the music "dit bien la détermination de celle qui avoue ne rien regretter de tout ce qu'a été sa vie"; Tribunal du Net "Le texte, d'une fierté tranquille, colle à son parcours cabossé".
- voice powerful: NYRB (Robb, artist-level) as above.
- landmark 1: Tribunal du Net "Voici sans doute le titre qui résume le mieux Piaf"; Hewett "if any song deserves to be called iconic".
- Dates: recorded 10 Nov 1960, released mid-December 1960 (Histoire des chansons; Discogs Dec 1960).
- Legacy 4: Hewett, many covers; Hans Zimmer built references to it into the Inception score (legacy.csv).
