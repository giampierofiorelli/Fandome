## Profound Mysteries II — original 10-track album, 19 August 2022

Sources: [II_OFF] Röyksopp primary release statement, [II_QOB] official credits via Qobuz, [II_MB] credited recording metadata, [II_AM] Timothy Monger, [II_PF] Ben Cardew, [II_NME] and [II_BC] for release context. The **Bandcamp page currently says 17 November 2022** even though Röyksopp's official release page, AllMusic, Pitchfork and Qobuz explicitly say **19 August 2022**: *do not* propagate Bandcamp's later display date. For year-field purposes there is no conflict. Ten-track edition excludes 2022 album I and III.

- `genres`: each of fourteen tags has an evidence line in genre CSV. Core electronics and synth-based dance pop [II_AM,II_PF], with a slower emotional half [II_AM]. `ambientbreakbeat`, `downbeat`, `downtempo`, `triphop`, `eurodance` are album-specific AllMusic style designations, not fabricated subgenres. `italodisco` and `rave` are explicitly admitted formative references in artist statements reported by Pitchfork; since these are source-attested sound references they are included at lower ranking and **not** claimed to dominate the record. `rnb` is a secondary Pitchfork *Pop/R&B* classification, rather than an assertion of traditional rhythm-and-blues instrumentation. `ambient` is an atmospheric subcomponent, not leading style.
- `energy=3;tempo=3;groove=3`: alternation between high-impact rave/dance tracks (especially Unity) and slower cinematic songs [II_AM,II_PF,II_NME]. Averaging entire edition; not marketing a uniformly intense club record.
- `electronic=5;polish=5`: records and synth programming largely electronic, immaculately edited/produced [II_AM,II_QOB]. A few session instruments on *Let's Get It Right* do not justify pretending this is purely acoustic [II_QOB].
- `mood=2;theatrical=3;experimental=2`: heartache and regret coexist with cathartic dance tracks [II_OFF,II_AM]; big reverbed hooks and vocal dramaturgy, but obvious classic/rave references rather than radical songwriting invention [II_PF]. Pitchfork's assessment is critical/negative; AllMusic finds it enjoyable and well crafted. Scores reflect sound, not review score.
- `voice=clear|soft|powerful|instrumental`: distinct guest voices from Astrid S, Karen Harding, Susanne Sundfør, Jamie Irrepressible and Pixx, plus non-vocal tracks; **not** one singer or a chorus-dominated record [II_QOB,II_OFF].
- `lyrics=love|heartbreak|introspection|nostalgia;language=en`: official album concept lists love/loss/despair/reconciliation [II_OFF]. English vocal tracks and separate instrumentals [II_QOB].
- `producers=Röyksopp`: per-track credits on Qobuz and MusicBrainz [II_QOB,II_MB]. Svein Berge and Torbjørn Brundtland are members of Röyksopp, **not additional production entities**, and Mike Marsh is **masterer**. Featured singers and Jørn Øien et al. are not blindly credited as producers.
- `sounds_like=Depeche Mode|Kraftwerk;influenced_by=Kraftwerk|Depeche Mode`: the former is editorial sonic resemblance, the latter independently supported by Berge's **explicit acknowledgment** of shaping influences [II_PF]. Do not auto-add every named genre to `influenced_by`; Italo disco is a style not an artist.
- `landmark=0`: distinct installment, but not clearly defining across the duo's career; this is editorial, not a false claim of commercial failure. `confidence=high;status=draft` reflects robust source corroboration with disclosed aesthetic disagreement.

## Profound Mysteries III — original 10-track album, November 2022

Sources: [III_AM] AllMusic, [III_SLANT] Thomas Bedenbaugh, [III_QUI] The Quietus, [III_RFS] Grace Twomey musician/reviewer, [III_QOB] Qobuz label credits, [III_MB] MusicBrainz, [III_BC] artist tracklist, [III_MC] Metacritic factual summary. Release dates vary 17 November (artist Bandcamp) vs 18 November (stores), so report the robust 2022 year, **do not force one day** without distributor corroboration.

- `genres`: seventeen genre labels supported by the per-genre source CSV. Core electronica/synthpop/downtempo plus obvious dance tracks [III_AM,III_SLANT]. `nudisco` **only** applies to *Me&Youphoria* [III_RFS]; `tropicalhouse` and `rnb` to *Just Wanted to Know* [III_QUI]; `edm` to *Stay Awhile* [III_QUI]; `disco` to critic's *Speed King* space-disco characterization [III_RFS]; `electro` to a single electro beat. These **non-dominant** tags come LAST and should not be marketed as album-wide motifs. `ambientbreakbeat`, `eurodance`, `downbeat` and `triphop` are AllMusic's album style tags, not extrapolations from prior installments.
- `energy=3;tempo=3;groove=3`: coexistence of `Speed King` (9:53), extended dance pieces and pensive songs [III_SLANT,III_AM]. Slant describes *this* volume as the trilogy's most introspective; not as uniformly downtempo as Nebulous Nights.
- `electronic=5;polish=5;experimental=3`: densely engineered synthesis, extended multi-stage arrangements and conceptual sequencing [III_AM,III_SLANT]; intermittent strings do not turn the record acoustic. `mood=2;theatrical=3`: melancholy opening and closing alongside the euphoria of fast tracks.
- `voice=soft|clear|powerful|instrumental`: variety of guests (Jamie Irrepressible, Alison Goldfrapp, Pixx, Susanne Sundfør, Astrid S and Maurissa Rose) and instrumental compositions; varied projection, not one singing style [III_MB,III_AM]. `lyrics=introspection|heartbreak|love` is a qualified album-wide thematic judgment, **not** a transcript-based lyrical corpus measurement.
- `producers=Röyksopp` [III_QOB,III_MB]; guests are performers, not automatically producers. `sounds_like=Air|Portishead|Daft Punk` is editorial *track-level similarity* named in critics [III_RFS,III_QUI]. `influenced_by` is empty: sonically resembling any of these artists does not prove they shaped this album.
- `landmark=0`: a trilogy closing volume and ambitious music, but restrained career-defining rating. Professional reviews disagree about artistic execution. Confidence high on **descriptive** fields, not certainty about subjective scales.

## Nebulous Nights — 30-piece live-recorded studio ambient album, 13 December 2024

Sources: official Röyksopp statement [N_OFF], Bandcamp [N_BC], Apple Music specialist editorial [N_APPLE], Pitchfork [N_PF], DJ Mag [N_DJ], Qobuz production credits [N_QOB], MusicBrainz relationships [N_MB]. Artists explicitly frame it as an **ambient re-interpretation** of the *Profound Mysteries* trilogy, recorded live in an after-hours **studio session**, with analogue warmth and new sequencing of 30 pieces. It is a distinct 2024 release, **not** a second copy of the 2022 albums, not an ordinary 2022 deluxe edition, and not a concert document.

- **Type uncertainty flagged:** row provisionally uses `studio` because it is a newly released full-length, studio-recorded live performance of reinterpretations. `live` in a music discovery catalogue ordinarily means a concert album; `remix` ordinarily means track remix collection. Some editors might reasonably prefer `remix` because of reinterpretations. Claude should **choose per catalog conventions and document the choice**, not auto change based solely on 'live' adjective.
- `genres=ambient|electronic|deephouse|house`: ambient is explicit artist and specialist genre of the **whole set** [N_OFF,N_PF,N_DJ]. Electronic analogue production is documented [N_QOB,N_APPLE]. **Deep house** is documented for the bass pattern of *Waiting Still* **alone** [N_APPLE], so house family goes LAST with *one-track* scope. Do not infer dark ambient, new age, ambient techno, trance or drone simply because the project sounds meditative.
- `energy=1;tempo=1;groove=1;theatrical=1`: Apple editorial explicitly contrasts its calmer, meditative arrangement with dance-driven 2022 trilogy; spaciousness and contemplative presentation dominate 2h+ play [N_APPLE,N_BC]. `electronic=4`: electronics prevail but live analogue performance and piano/bass-like textures add organic elements; `polish=3`: artist describes deliberately raw live analogue warmth rather than immaculate high-gloss sound [N_OFF]. `experimental=3`: turning 30 electronic pop compositions into extended ambient suite is a substantial formal re-contextualization, not an algorithmic measure. `mood=2`: contemplative and mostly subdued, not uniformly miserable or joyous.
- `voice=instrumental|soft|clear`: numerous instrumentals punctuated by hushed contributions by Astrid S, Alison Goldfrapp, Pixx, Susanne Sundfør, Jamie Irrepressible and Karen Harding [N_OFF,N_APPLE,N_QOB]. `lyrics` empty because no reliable new album-wide lyrical analysis exists; primarily instrumental reinterpretations. `language=en` applies to the vocal sections, **not** the instrumental pieces.
- `producers=Röyksopp`: this is explicit on Qobuz tracks [N_QOB]. Zino Mikorey is **mastering engineer**; guest singers are performers/writers, not producers. `sounds_like` and `influenced_by` left empty: artist's mentions of callbacks to Röyksopp's own music are not evidence that an outside act influenced this album. `landmark=0` is a preliminary catalog editorial call.
