## Etta James

### ettajames-atlast (At Last!, 1960)
Sources opened:
- Rolling Stone 500 (2020) #191 (page JSON via curl): "Against Riley Hampton's meaty orchestrations, James wraps her husky voice around strange bedfellows such as 'Stormy Weather' and Willie Dixon's 'I Just Want to Make Love to You,' injecting them with rock & roll heart. She hit the pop and R&B charts with three of the songs here and, in the process, created a new vocal model: the crossover diva." https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/best-albums-of-all-time-1062063/etta-james-at-last-2-1062890/
- Pitchfork review, Amanda Petrusich, 2016, score 9.0: "one of the greatest records about loneliness ever made: a languid, jazz-inflected paean"; "Most of At Last! concerns itself with unmitigated heartache"; "'Sunday Kind of Love,' ... over a spare, barely-tinkling piano melody (interrupted, on occasion, by rising swells of strings)"; "there's no moment on At Last! that feels explicitly or distractingly performative"; "James's masterwork". https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/21797-at-last/
- Pitchfork 200 Best Albums of the 1960s, #62 (Quinn Moreland): "the microphone can hardly contain her outbursts of desire. But only in its famous title track ... does James' voice get to relish in unbridled softness"; "candid ballads"; "broadcast the blues like no one else". https://pitchfork.com/features/lists-and-guides/the-200-best-albums-of-the-1960s/
- AllMusic, Stephen Cook (via Qobuz): "James expertly handles jazz standards like 'Stormy Weather' ... as well as Willie Dixon's blues classic"; "moves from powerful blues shouting to more subtle, airy phrasing; her Ruth Brown-inspired, bad-girl growl"; "a swinging and varied program of blues, R&B, and jazz standards". https://www.qobuz.com/ie-en/album/at-last-etta-james/0000881120172
- KXCI Classic Pick (2015): "blues, r&b, and jazz standards"; Phil and Leonard Chess "paid for the orchestral arrangements on many of the album's tracks", believing in her "crossover" potential; Chess brothers credited as producers. https://kxci.org/2015/11/etta-james-at-last-kxci-classic-pick/
- Discogs (Argo LP 4003, 15 Nov 1960): Arranged By, Conductor – Riley Hampton; styles Rhythm & Blues, Soul. https://www.discogs.com/release/4181251-Etta-James-At-Last

## Minnie Riperton
