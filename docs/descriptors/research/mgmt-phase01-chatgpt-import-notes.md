## ARTIST INFLUENCES — phase 01 only
These artists are identified directly by MGMT as important to their musical outlook, not automatically linked to all album rows:
- **The Flaming Lips** — MGMT discuss their influence on *Time to Pretend*: [Song Exploder / Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/news/62970-mgmt-dissect-time-to-pretend-on-song-exploder/).
- **Psychic TV**, **Chrome**, **Spiritualized** and **Spacemen 3** — explicitly named in [2008 Caught in the Crossfire interview](https://www.caughtinthecrossfire.com/music/interviews-2/mgmt-interview/).
- **Suicide**, **Royal Trux**, **Television Personalities**, **Roxy Music** and **Neil Young** — described as admired, or as sources of an outsider/performance approach in the [2010 Pitchfork interview](https://pitchfork.com/features/interview/7791-mgmt/). Royal Trux also has a guest vocalist on *Congratulations*: that guest credit alone is not an influence.
- **Skip Spence** and **Mayo Thompson** — Andrew VanWyngarden describes their outsider solo albums as central inspirations for *Congratulations* in [Electronic Beats, 2013](https://www.electronicbeats.net/everything-is-not-ok-an-interview-with-mgmt).
- **Phish** — VanWyngarden identifies himself as a major fan, and discusses their approach to breaking habits, [Relix/Jambands 2013](https://jambands.com/features/2013/11/14/mgmt-from-phish-to-faine-jade/). Historical artist influence only, not specific to any album row.

Caveat: the list distinguishes a named artist's broad influence from an album-specific production source; it contains neither unconfirmed retrospective guesses nor guest appearances masquerading as influences.

## Research / import cautions
- **Original date**: *Oracular Spectacular* began release in 2007, despite its US/UK physical-distribution prominence in 2008. Preserve the catalogue's 2007 year.
- **Producer separation**: *Time to Pretend* David Molinari, *Oracular Spectacular* MGMT and Dave Fridmann; *Congratulations* MGMT and Sonic Boom (Fridmann **mixing**, not production); *MGMT* MGMT and Fridmann.
- *Time to Pretend* EP has early recordings of two compositions that reappear on the debut album. The EP is included because those **different recordings** matter; never duplicate the EP row in the next batch.
- Liner note source MusicBrainz is community-maintained. The album recording credits are cross-checked with AllMusic and the Columbia label where possible.
- Avoid inferred instrument models, unsourced guitar subtypes or vocal-layering techniques; the evidence notes explain omissions.
- Individual scale values are research-grounded assessments (0–5), not claims of instrument-specific audio analysis.
- `psychedelicfolk` on 2013 is a limited track/cover association, not an album-wide classification.
