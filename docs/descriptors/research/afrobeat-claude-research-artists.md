- id: `femikuti` · display: Femi Kuti · country: NG · languages: pcm, en · start: 1980s (in Fela's Egypt 80 from 1979; own band Positive Force 1986)
- genre families: world, funk
- tagline: "Afrobeat's torch-bearing son" · colour: `#2F8F5B`

- Artist influences:

  - John Coltrane, Duke Ellington, Herbie Hancock — same interview ("I used to listen to a lot of jazz", "I used to like Duke Ellington", on Hancock "I loved them so much when I was young"): medium strength, he frames them as youthful listening.
