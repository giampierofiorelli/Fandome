# Ligabue — Phase 01: evidence and interpretive decisions

## Method

- Source review date: 2026-10-29. Core credits are cross-checked against the artist site, original-credit transcriptions, Musicisti Reggiani, AllMusic, OndaRock, and discography data. Spotify metadata and reissues are **not** assumed to establish original recording dates.
- Scales are editorial estimates using Fandome 0–5 anchors, not measured audio features. `confidence` measures the support for the full row; `status` is `draft`.
- Token `countryfolk` is used for OndaRock's actual term *folk-country*, not because of a banjo alone. `raprock` is lead-track-only and was prioritised because that track names the 1994 release.
- The `influenced_by` field is **empty unless evidence ties the named artist to this specific album**; the wider influences are in CLAUDE_IMPORT.md. Comparisons, covers, collaborators, and guest performers do not become influences.
- Carrara's production credits conflict between providers. The first three sleeve-derived credits distinguish `produzione artistica: Ligabue` and `produzione esecutiva: Angelo Carrara`; secondary sources including AllMusic call Carrara a producer. Fandome rules exclude *executive* producers, so `producers` contains Ligabue and the conflicting Carrara credit remains flagged for physical-sleeve adjudication. This is **not** a claim that Carrara did no artistic work.

## 1. Ligabue (1990) — `ligabue-ligabue`

**Original and id.** 11 May 1990 studio debut, already in catalog as `['Ligabue',1990]`. Do not create a second item. Musicisti Reggiani has original players and track assignments; AllMusic confirms Italian Pop and Contemporary Pop/Rock. Sources: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/ligabue/, https://www.allmusic.com/album/ligabue-mw0000461645, https://www.ligabue.com/biografia/.

**Genres and sonic relationships.** Rock and pop-rock are main. The official biography explicitly calls out the integration of storytelling and rock, supporting `canzonedautore`. OndaRock describes *Bar Mario* as blues-rock, *Marlon Brando è sempre lui* as rock and roll, *Non è tempo per noi* as folk-country, *Figlio d'un cane* as punk-adjacent. The latter four tags are one-track textures, ordered in the tail. OndaRock **compares** *Non è tempo per noi* with Neil Young, *Sogni di rock'n'roll* with the Smiths, and *Piccola stella senza cielo* with U2's *With or Without You* (original studio album **The Joshua Tree**); all are `sounds_like`, never `influenced_by`. Source: https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

**Instruments.** Cottafavi electric guitar, Ligabue acoustic, Ghezzi bass, Cavalli Cocchi drums; Aguzzi/Feiez Hammond, piano, keyboards; Feiez sax on *Piccola stella*; De Carli slide guitar; Dentes banjo on *Angelo della nebbia*. Sources: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/ligabue/, https://ligaterminifanclub.wordpress.com/discografia/ligabue-1990/.

**Production and voice.** The artist himself characterises the very short sessions as an unpolished recording. No named studio technique or equipment is securely evidenced, so `production` deliberately blank. `raspy|clear|spoken` from OndaRock's timbral and spoken *Bambolina e Barracuda* observations. The album alternates energetic rock and reflective songs (3/0/3/2/1/2/3/2). Source: https://www.allmusicitalia.it/news/ligabue-ora-e-allora.html.

**Reception.** OndaRock regards the first album as a strong early work; official biography describes a successful debut. `landmark=1`. More granular acclaim scores are not encoded in the requested 27-column descriptor.

## 2. Lambrusco coltelli rose & pop corn (1991) — `ligabue-lambruscocoltellirosepopcorn`

**Identity.** 20 September 1991 studio album, **absent from Fandome catalog**. OndaRock stresses a greater folk-country component, alongside stadium-ready rock. Musicisti Reggiani lists pop and rock. Album also has hard-rock passages (*Urlando contro il cielo*), blues (*Camera con vista sul deserto*), acoustic ballads, a jazz texture in one song and rock-and-roll on title single. Grunge is **weak**: only a DeBaser listener's interpretive comparison, last-ranked and explicitly disputed; Claude may drop it. Sources: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/lambrusco-coltelli-rose-pop-corn/, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/, https://www.debaser.it/luciano-ligabue/lambrusco-coltelli-rose-and-popcorn/recensione.

**Instruments.** Cottafavi electric guitar with bottleneck / slide guitar, Ghezzi bass, Cavalli Cocchi drums/percussion, Ligabue acoustic guitar, Marani Hammond/piano, Lugli harmonica, Borghi accordion, Bolchi violin; *Libera nos a malo* has Monte Cusna choir (backing choir is not lead-voice tag `choral`). Source: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/lambrusco-coltelli-rose-pop-corn/.

**Influences.** OndaRock explicitly attributes to Ligabue the wish to write an ending modeled on **Simple Minds** for *Lambrusco & pop corn*; this is the one defensible album-specific `influenced_by`. This is reported attribution, not a transcript of a contemporary interview; medium strength. OndaRock calls *Urlando contro il cielo* Springsteen-like and *Libera nos a malo* guitar Guns N' Roses-like. These are critic comparisons in `sounds_like`, not musical-source claims. OndaRock's comparison to Fellini is cinematic homage, not a musical influence. Source: https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

**Production.** Original-credit transcriptions identify Ligabue as artistic producer and Carrara as executive producer, with Lovat recording. The 1991 original-vinyl transcription also says produced by Carrara; flag ambiguity, do not silently conflate his credit. A named audio process is not evidenced; `production` blank. Slightly greater studio polish than debut (3). Source: https://ligaterminifanclub.wordpress.com/discografia/lambruscocoltellirose-e-popcorn-1991/, https://vinylstory.wixsite.com/brunomalasomma/post/ligabue-1991-lambrusco-coltelli-rose-pop-corn.

## 3. Sopravvissuti e sopravviventi (1993) — `ligabue-sopravvissutiesopravviventi`

**Identity.** Official release 22 January 1993, **absent from catalog**. AllMusic presents 18 October 1993 as release date, which conflicts with the original Italian date; use artist's official date. OndaRock documents unsuccessful contemporary reception, later reappraisal, more deliberate darker concept and voice-forward mix. Music is conventional rock in structure, hence `experimental=2`, not 4 or 5. Sources: https://www.ligabue.com/discografia/sopravvissuti-e-sopravviventi/, https://www.allmusic.com/album/sopravvissuti-e-sopravviventi-mw0000539974, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

**Genre distinctions.** Predominant rock/pop-rock with explicitly described grunge on *Dove fermano i treni* and *Ancora in piedi*. Blues on *Ho messo via*, ballads *Ho messo via* and *Walter il mago*, Italian Pop / Contemporary Pop-Rock as AllMusic retailer metadata. Never call the entire album grunge. OndaRock calls the concept a tragicomic stage work, justifying `theatrical=4`; darker themes make `mood=1`. Sources: https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/, https://www.allmusic.com/album/sopravvissuti-e-sopravviventi-mw0000539974.

**Instruments.** Guitar variants and acoustic/electric bass explicitly credited by AllMusic (not proof of upright `doublebass`); drums/tambourine and organ/piano from detailed criticism and personnel summaries. Demo Morselli trumpet and flugelhorn, Gabriele Zanetti cello, Ligabue kazoo, string arrangements by Morselli, and brass in late sections. The marching Asioli Jazz Band on *Quando tocca a te* is documented, but the isolated track does not justify tagging the entire album jazz. Sources: https://www.allmusic.com/album/sopravvissuti-e-sopravviventi-mw0000539974, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

**Production.** `distortion`: heavy electric-guitar distortion is explicitly described by OndaRock; `stringarrangements`: Morselli credited by AllMusic. No claim of a specific analogue multitrack or named console. The artist's official credits state artistic production Ligabue, executive production Carrara; AllMusic also credits Carrara as producer. For strict Fandome import, retain Ligabue pending physical sleeve verification. Source: https://www.ligabue.com/discografia/sopravvissuti-e-sopravviventi/.

**Comparisons, not influences.** OndaRock likens the coda of *Lo zoo è qui* to The Doors. It also claims that contemporary grunge albums shaped the band, but without a direct member quote; **do not** enter Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Alice in Chains or Soundgarden in `influenced_by`. Romantic/survival storytelling outweighs overt politics. Source: https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

## 4. A che ora è la fine del mondo? (1994) — `ligabue-acheoraelafinedelmondo`

**Status, source conflict, exclusions.** Official issue 28 October 1994. It is the fourth original studio album by the official artist discography and Italian discographies; AllMusic's artist bio calls it an EP and its album page lists **1999**, likely a later edition. Keep **`type=studio` provisionally** as the primary original-discography classification and document the EP disagreement. The release is eight disparate recordings, mostly earlier outtakes or reworkings, including a **single live closer** *Urlando contro il cielo*. The user excludes **live albums**, not studio albums with one live bonus track. No reissue-only songs are being added. Source: https://www.ligabue.com/discografia/a-che-ora-e-la-fine-del-mondo/, https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/a-che-ora-e-la-fine-del-mondo/, https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_che_ora_%C3%A8_la_fine_del_mondo, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

**Genres.** Musicisti Reggiani lists alternative rock, Italian original discography adds pop-rock. OndaRock calls the lead single an Italian rap-rock reworking of R.E.M. and *Cerca nel cuore* an acoustic ballad. Because the title hit is the lead single, `raprock` is allowed as the third tag despite one-track scope. AllMusic lists Italian Pop / Euro-Pop / Contemporary Pop-Rock for a later catalog listing: genre sources mark that edition caveat. Mood and energy are mixed across sessions (3/0/2/3/1/3/3/2). Source: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/a-che-ora-e-la-fine-del-mondo/, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/, https://www.allmusic.com/album/a-che-ora-%EF%BF%BD-la-fine-del-mondo--mw0000539971.

**Instruments.** Earlier ClanDestino and replacement sessions: Cottafavi guitar, Ghezzi bass, Cavalli drums, Fornaciari Hammond/piano/voice, Marani piano; Lele Melotti drums, Braido guitar, Romani bass on selected pieces. No synthesis or drum machines documented, hence `electronic=0`. `concertrecording` only because original issue explicitly contains one live track; mark as **track-specific**, not an album recorded live. Source: https://www.musicistireggiani.it/album/a-che-ora-e-la-fine-del-mondo/.

**Production, cover and likeness.** Official credits assign artistic production to Ligabue and executive production to Valerio Soave. The title is an **adaptation of R.E.M.'s *It's the End of the World as We Know It (And I Feel Fine)* from *Document* (1987)**. It is a cover/adaptation, hence R.E.M. belongs in `sounds_like` but must not be entered in `influenced_by`. Negrita participate on *L'han detto anche gli Stones*: collaboration, not influence. Likewise writing with Fornaciari is not proof of influence. Source: https://www.ligabue.com/discografia/a-che-ora-e-la-fine-del-mondo/, https://www.ondarock.it/monografie/ligabue/.

## Disputes requiring second-person review

1. Producer: on all of the first three, artistically produced by Ligabue vs Carrara as executive; multiple secondary sites list Carrara as general producer. Claude should inspect original LP booklets and follow the project's rule for separating these credits.
2. The 1994 `type`: official original studio album vs AllMusic bio EP. Do not drop merely because the program is short or includes one live song; document how importer classifies hybrid releases.
3. AllMusic dates 1993 (October vs official January) and 1994 (1999 release reissue vs October 1994), so official dates take precedence.
4. `grunge` on Lambrusco is weak and contested. Track-only and retailer-only styles are explicitly flagged in genre evidence.
5. 1990 `countryfolk` / `punkrock` based on specific tracks, and 1994 `europop` based on AllMusic's later metadata are low-weight.
6. `sounds_like` is judgement supported by named critical comparisons, not artist admissions. 1991 `Simple Minds` influence is a documented attribution to Ligabue's own stated compositional method for one song only.
