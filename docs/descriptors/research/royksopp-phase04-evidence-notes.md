# Röyksopp — phase 04: True Electric (2025)

## Scope and decisive classification issue
**One new row ONLY**: *True Electric* (2025); all 9 earlier Röyksopp phases 01–03 rows excluded. Original digital release 11 April 2025, physical editions 9 May 2025 [TE_NEWS, TE_BAND, TE_STORE].

The artists themselves [TE_OFF, TE_NEWS] state it consists of **19 new studio recordings and reconstructions** of performances arranged for the 2023 True Electric tour, predominantly previous songs/remixes plus one new composition, The “R”. This is **not a concert recording** and NOT a standard fully new-original-compositions studio album. The `type=studio` proposal refers to fresh studio recordings. Fandome taxonomy allows `remix`, and Claude should consciously decide whether it is editorially preferable to classify reworked multi-origin song collections as `remix` or perhaps new category `re-recording`. Either way retain the fact that recordings are newly made and no physical live set audio is claimed. Avoid treating the streaming store's combined 38 entries as 38 different compositions: it is mixed/individual versions of the same 19 songs [TE_MB].

## Sonic descriptors and source hierarchy

These are not all equal as whole-album descriptors: dominant electronic and club/dance/house; harder techno and trance recur; trip-hop/downtempo/ambient-breakbeat occur in inherited material and received AllMusic taxonomy but do not define this rework record. Voice: cross-artist diverse featured vocals, clear, soft, powerful and instrumental moments; includes Alison Goldfrapp, Robyn, Susanne Sundfør, Jamie Irrepressible, Beki Mari, Fever Ray and others. These **featured vocalists are NOT album producers or influences by default**. Language English dominant, some tracks instrumental. Lyrics drawn from varied existing songs: love, break-up, reflection, identity, celebration; confidence lower for any broad generalized theme.

Producers: Duo **Röyksopp (Svein Berge and Torbjørn Brundtland)** re-create arrangements. No re-crediting featured singers as producers without explicit track credits. *What Else Is There* includes Trentemøller remix provenance in database [TE_MB]; prior remix author not automatically album producer. In `sounds_like` and `influenced_by` abstain: no specific external artist's documented causal influence established for *this* rework record.
