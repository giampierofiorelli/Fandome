# Rondò Veneziano — Phase 04 evidence notes (1992–1997)

Research is for **specific releases**, not traits guessed from the artist's general sound. Source URLs and roles are recorded in `rondoveneziano_phase04_sources.csv`; genre-by-genre attributions in the genre evidence CSV. The independent media on these albums are sparse: most precise evidence comes from label metadata, liner-credit transcriptions, AllMusic's structured album descriptions, and the artist's own discography. **All scale judgments are provisional and deliberately sparse.** Genre orders are evidence-weighted, not claims of listening to every track. 

## 1. Stagioni di Venezia (1992) — `rondoveneziano-stagionidivenezia`

**Identity:** The original 1992 work is also called *G. P. Reverberi – Rondò Veneziano* on Italian discographies. The artist's current discography and label metadata use *Stagioni di Venezia*. The term *Le stagioni di Venezia* in earlier planning is not established as the original printed title. Qobuz preserves the 1992 phonogram even though AllMusic primarily displays a 1993 issue. The 14 tracks contain mostly new pieces and newly recorded versions of earlier themes “Rondò Veneziano” and “La Serenissima”, so the work is **studio**, not a recycled best-of (Official, Qobuz; secondary liner transcription).

**Sound/genres:** Album-specific AllMusic metadata lists Classical Crossover, Easy Listening, Neo-Prog and Prog-Rock, and Classical as a genre; Qobuz supplies Pop; the Italian album article explicitly names *pop barocco*. `neoprog` is a new Fandome token, not a synonym invented for `prog`. Treat the prog tags with modest weight: structured AllMusic genre metadata may use a broad artist framework (AllMusic S92, Qobuz S92).

**Instrumentation/production:** Its **Rondò Veneziano orchestral sound** is described as orchestral/easy-listening by AllMusic; separately a specific liner transcription documents **Ossi (Ozzy) Shaller on electric guitar on track 10**. The electric guitar is supplementary rather than claimed album-wide. I did not infer synthesizer or a particular keyboard model from previous albums. The documented mixing credit does not equal `remixing` or any of the listed production techniques. Hence production is empty.

**Scales:** `theatrical=4` is a restrained editorial reading of AllMusic's *theatrical*, refined orchestral characterization, not a quantified technical measurement. Energy, electronic, polish, tempo, groove, mood and experimental remain unknown. **Producers:** the Qobuz 1992 track credits explicitly label Gian Piero Reverberi producer; Ivano Pavesi is **composer**, not a producer. `sounds_like` is only an editorial similar-artist proposal; it is not evidence of influence. `influenced_by` blank. **Disagreement:** date 1992 vs AllMusic 1993 export; early works are rerecorded, not duplicated album rows.

## 2. Il mago di Venezia (1994) — `rondoveneziano-ilmagodivenezia`

**Identity:** Official listing comprises 12 tracks. The 1994 DDD/BMG record documented by Recordsale and the Apple metadata establishes original issue; Italian encyclopedic secondary metadata gives a different October day. Neither day is necessary for Fandome's year. The 1994 track “Buon Natale, Mondo” is not to be duplicated as the 1995 *Sinfonia di Natale* album.

**Genres/sound:** AllMusic gives **Classical** and **Easy Listening**, with orchestral presentation; Apple gives **Pop**. A reproduced CD seller page names **Baroque** and **Neo-Classical**, included after stronger classifications because the description is retail secondary metadata, not an expert assessment. No separate `classicalcrossover` genre is added here by inference from the artist alone. AllMusic moods *lush, gentle, dramatic, elegant, passionate* ground `mood=3` as a mixture of tender and dramatic and `theatrical=4` as a distinct dramatic orchestral treatment, with uncertainty.

**Instrumentation/production:** Ozzy/Ossi Schaller's **electric guitar soloist** role is documented in a transcription of original label credits and by AllMusic; orchestrated ensemble is described on album-specific AllMusic page. Producer **Gian Piero Reverberi** verified directly; **Klaus Strazicky = recording engineer**, not a producer; **Ivano Pavesi = composer**, not a producer. The recording at Arcu/Pilot studios is a venue, not evidence of `digitaltape`, `analogtape` or an instrument. Thus `production` is blank.

## 3. Sinfonia di Natale (1995) — `rondoveneziano-sinfoniadinatale`

**Identity:** Studio holiday album with 13 tracks, mainly adaptations and suites including “Ave Maria”, “White Christmas”, “Stille Nacht”, plus original material. German *Weihnachten mit Rondò Veneziano* and French *Symphonie de Noël* editions are **not new studio albums**. Original Apple Classical phonogram is 1995; the Italian reference gives November 13 versus an October 31 release field in Apple Classical. Date does not change year.

**Genres:** `christmas` explicitly listed on Apple Music; AllMusic uses Classical, Classical Crossover, Orchestral/Easy Listening, Neo-Prog and Prog-Rock; an Italian album discography supplies pop barocco. Last two AllMusic styles are downweighted as a potentially generic database taxonomy; the album's strongest identity is a Christmas classical-crossover record. Covers of Schubert, Gounod, Bach and other composers are **not musical influences** in the strict Fandome `influenced_by` sense.

**Instruments/voice/production:** German first-edition listing labels all 13 tracks **instrumental**, supporting `voice=instrumental`; orchestral setting described in AllMusic, `orchestra` only. Credited **Franco Fochesato: Programming** (AllMusic); supports `programming`, but not a named sequencer, synth, drum machine or software. Reverberi and Strazicky carry remixing credits, but whether this denotes album-wide remixes or routine mixing is not established: no `remixing` token has been asserted. Apple Classical labels P.E.M. under Production; interpretation as a co-producer versus a production company is ambiguous. Use **Gian Piero Reverberi**, unequivocally named *Producer* by AllMusic, in the producers field, with P.E.M. mentioned here for second-person review.

**Scales:** Neither the festive track titles nor the band's established arrangement template justifies a precise 0–5 energy/mood/groove. Left all eight numbers empty rather than fabricated, `confidence=medium` because date, tracklist, styles and composer/producer credits are still solid.

## 4. Marco Polo (1997) — `rondoveneziano-marcopolo`

**Identity:** Original **Koch 33502-2 1997** release and artist website contain 15 movements forming a concept album on Marco Polo's eastbound travels. AllMusic's displayed 23 March **1999** release is a subsequent market issue, not the original year. Primary edition was successful enough to chart at #13 in Switzerland and #52 in Germany. 1997 *Gian Piero Reverberi Conducts Rondò Veneziano – In concerto* is an independent live album; not included in this studio phase.

**Genres:** Album-specific AllMusic labels Classical, Easy Listening, Pop/Rock, New Age, Neo-Prog and Prog-Rock; the release article describes **classical crossover** and **world music** (Tibetan/Mongolian traditional pieces). `newage` is therefore directly tied to this record and should not be projected backwards; pop is corroborated by the Italian album discography. Tracks of traditional material are repertoire/adaptations, not named influence artists. The composer's **Giuseppe Zuppone** collaboration on several cues is not an `influenced_by` artist.

**Instrumentation and production:** AllMusic credits **Klaus Strazicky: Piano**; **Sergio Barlozzi: Drum Programming / Drum Recordings**; **Franco Fochesato** and **Gian Piero Reverberi: Programming**. Hence `orchestra|piano`, `drumprogramming|programming`. `drummachine`, specific sampler, instrument models and world instruments are not supported by those credits. `theatrical=4` reflects album-specific theatrical AllMusic mood + a narrative, multi-movement orchestral presentation; it is an editorial estimate.

**Voice uncertainty:** A physical pressing inventory marks 14 of 15 tracks as instrumental but **does not** mark “Il canto: la meditazione”. A missing “instrumental” marker does **not prove** voice is present. Main album characteristic is nevertheless instrumental; do not add `choral`, `spoken`, a lyric theme or a singing language without recording-specific vocal credits. Qobuz-style composer credits are not proof of singers. **Producers:** Gian Piero Reverberi credited specifically as producer; other contributors were arrangers, composers or recording personnel.

**Duration differences:** The original Austrian pressing listings, the artist site and Lescharts have differences of a few seconds, and a few substantial track timings. Treat original 1997 Koch listing as definitive for release identity; no need to copy durations into Fandome descriptor rows. `landmark=0` because chart success alone is not sufficient for a defining career record, despite its distinctive concept; retain as editorial review item.
