# Sabrina Salerno — Fandome Phase 01 | Evidence and decisions

Research date 2026-10-27. Primary data: original 1987/1988/1991/1995 discography entries, credited 1987 releases, original 1991 CD, self-described artist biography, 1995 musician testimony. Score estimates are editorial and distinct from verified release facts.

## Original album chronology and 27-column descriptors

- **1987, Sabrina**: Original Five Record FM 13593 Italian LP has **nine songs**. Original 1987 international versions include different mixes and CD bonus tracks: **not** represented as a new album. Original sleeve release credits transcribed by Chartsurfer specify **R. Rossi: all keyboards and programming; C. Bazzari: guitar** for most recorded tracks. RapReviews describes the squeaky synth timbres of Boys (Summertime Love) and Malcolm Charlton's short rap. The latter is a guest vocal segment, not a lead vocal category, therefore album `voice` stays `clear|soft`. `instruments=keyboards|synth|guitar`, `production=programming` supported by original credit. Executive producers not blindly promoted into album-wide producer names (track producer wording differs by edition). Sources: https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/sabrina-it/sabrina-album_ffeuep.html ; https://www.rapreviews.com/2022/06/early-sightings-of-rap-in-1980s-pop-italy/ ; https://musicbrainz.org/release/cffaad97-9f7a-4fc2-b400-7e3c06bf9204 ; https://www.45cat.com/vinyl/album/fm13593.

- **1988, Super Sabrina**: Original Italian **10-track Videogram LP** (1988-10-31), 8-track and 10-track foreign CDs differ in programme; some editions re-use Boys and Sexy Girl. 'All of Me (Boy Oh Boy)' is independently verified as written and produced by Stock Aitken Waterman, while Rockol and the artist attribute Like a Yo Yo to Giorgio Moroder; Giampiero Menzione and Elvio Moratto appear in album credits. Giorgio Moroder **writing** Pirate of Love alone does NOT make him producer of that track. The 1990 German CD credit transcription differs; production field remains **empty** because documented originals do not identify album-wide machine models/technical methods, and no artist-specific instrument credit was securely found for these tracks. Do not import any PWL Fairlight/LinnDrum/DX7 by association. Sources: https://musicbrainz.org/release-group/6f235645-cc73-32cd-8db7-7ca5ee343325 ; https://www.chartsurfer.de/artist/sabrina-it/super-sabrina-album_ffeerr.html ; https://www.stockaitkenwaterman.co.uk/sabrina ; https://www.rockol.it/news-720822/sabrina-salerno-icona-degli-anni-ottanta-le-canzoni-i-video.

- **1991, Over the Pop**: Original 1991 **12-track Casablanca LP** is the anchor. A contemporaneous **14-track CD** has Siamo donne (feat. Jo Squillo), a remix of Yeah Yeah and an a cappella bonus version: genre selections **exclude** a general disco classification based purely on the variant Siamo donne. Underlying album is pop/dance-pop/pop-rock; original expanded CD is tagged electro/eurodance, lower-ranked in `genres`. A May 1991 Music & Media review, reproduced in later discography, identifies **R. Ravenscroft sax** solo on Yeah Yeah, hence the sole documented album instrument `saxophone`. Although CD includes remixes, no `remixing` production token applied to the **standard** album programme. Producers are album/track names **not** `influenced_by`; artist gains documented writing/production control. Sources: https://www.45cat.com/vinyl/album/8464131 ; https://www.musik-sammler.de/release/sabrina-over-the-pop-cd-1372949/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Pop ; https://www.sabrinasalerno.com/en/biografia/ ; https://www.qobuz.com/no-en/album/over-the-pop-sabrina-salerno/3614971525546.

- **1995, Maschio dove sei**: **Use 1995**, not later popular-discography 1996: MusicBrainz gives a 1995 Italian NAR original CD, Apple Music a 1995 original phonogram and first-generation recording drummer Andrea Ge states he played on this album in 1995. Artist Italian biography agrees 1995; artist English biography and some encyclopaedias say 1996. The contradiction is **resolved in favour of documented earliest release**, not ignored. Genre **poprock**; first full Italian-language album and rock pivot. **Studio drummer Andrea Ge** names **Maurizio Solieri (guitars), Demo Morselli (brass) and James Thompson (sax)** on the actual album: instruments are solidly attributed. Ge also describes **Geppi Frattali on bass / Giacomo Giannotti on keyboards for the tour**: these two instruments are **NOT added to studio album credits** because concert membership does not prove session participation. Massimo Riva, Enrico Monti and Sabrina Salerno are credited as producers. Source: https://www.andreage.com/biography/ ; https://musicbrainz.org/artist/3a4fc9e1-81af-4f1e-8ccf-e8e9ab77b3c1/releases ; https://music.apple.com/us/album/maschio-dove-sei/1021554311 ; https://www.sabrinasalerno.com/biografia/ ; https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maschio_dove_sei.

## ARTIST INFLUENCES — separate from production, collaborations, covers and editorial sonics

In a first-person 2022 interview, Sabrina says she has not had artistic role models. Source: https://www.ilmessaggerocasa.it/news/casa-news/sabrina_salerno_uomini_padre_rapporto_bellezza_instagram_dove_vive_sorella_anni_80_intervista/6647864.html. This general statement does not prove an album had no influences, but it provides no album-specific documented influence to enter. Thus `influenced_by` **deliberately empty for all four albums**. These are **NOT influences**: Prince (cover Kiss), The Knack (cover My Sharona), Labelle (cover Lady Marmalade), Richard Carpenter/Carpenters (cover Yesterday Once More), Jimmy Cliff (cover You Can Get It If You Really Want), Jo Squillo (collaborator), Claudio Cecchetto and Giorgio Moroder (producers), Kylie Minogue or Spagna (sonic editorial comparisons only). The `sounds_like` values are **explicitly editorial judgments**, not claims of artist testimony.

## Production and uncertainty rules

Do not infer specific synthesizer/drum-machine models merely because an album is Italo disco or made by Stock Aitken Waterman. `voice` represents main singer only; background guests and one brief male rap on Boys do not make her primary voice rap. Genre token `hinrg` appears **last**, supported only for select defining singles, not asserted as a classification of every track. Genre ranking intentionally prioritises original LP/release style over reissue tags. Subjective eight-scale numbers are not a substitute for factual claims.

## Excluded and deferred issues

- Excluded collections: Something Special (1988) and Super Remix (1990), including reused original tracks and extended versions.
- Excluded reissues: Numeri (1997) and Fatta... Rifatta (1999) derive from Maschio dove sei and are not distinct new studio sessions.
- Phase 02: A Flower's Broken (1999) and **Erase/Rewind Official Remix (2008)**, which contains both newly recorded and remixed material and needs track/format classification rather than being automatically accepted or excluded.
