## endrigo-sergioendrigo (Sergio Endrigo, 1962)
- Credits:
  - Discogs master 1082862 / release 5459331 (https://www.discogs.com/release/5459331): genre Pop, style Chanson; "Orchestra: Luis Enriquez E La Sua Orchestra"; Aria di neve: "Choir: Coro Di Franco Potenza"; Il soldato di Napoleone written by Pier Paolo Pasolini and Endrigo.
  - DDS http://discografia.dds.it/scheda_titolo.php?idt=726: orchestra Luis Enriquez Bacalov, "Coro di Franco Potenza (cori)", back-cover note by Gaio Fratini.
- genres:
  - canzonedautore: canzoneitaliana.it (ICBSA), La nascita dei cantautori (https://canzoneitaliana.it/la-nascita-dei-cantautori/): "saranno loro, Gino Paoli, Luigi Tenco, Sergio Endrigo e Bruno Lauzi, a cambiare la canzone italiana… Questa nuova canzone verrà chiamata 'canzone d'autore'"; "la melodia scarna e l'armonia raffinata".
  - chanson and pop: Discogs (above).
- energy 1, mood 1, groove 1:
  - luisbacalov.com (Bacalov heirs, https://luisbacalov.com/en/produzione-artistica/io-che-amo-solo-te-vecchia-balera-2/): "a melancholic and elegant song"; "opens with a long, non-danceable introduction… the rhythm only appears in the second part of the song"; "wide-ranging melodies filtered through an intimist taste far from concessions to the general public".
  - Il Post / Luca Sofri, Sei canzoni di Sergio Endrigo (https://www.ilpost.it/2013/06/15/canzoni-di-sergio-endrigo/): Endrigo "aveva una sua sobria eleganza"; Aria di neve is "meteorologia sentimentale" about a relationship that is "finita, tesa e glaciale".
- theatrical 1: intimist, sober (same sources).
- experimental 1: a long non-danceable intro that was "revolutionary" for the time, but the forms are classic.
- polish 3: RCA orchestral studio production. tempo 2: slow-to-mid ballads.
- instruments orchestra; production choirvocals (one track, Coro Franco Potenza).
- lyrics: love and heartbreak (Il Post: Io che amo solo te a "dichiarazione d'amore"; Aria di neve a failing relationship).
- voice left empty: no source on the voice.
- producers: none credited. Bacalov is credited as orchestra leader and arranger, not producer.
- landmark 0: no source calls the LP defining; the praise is for the single.
- Chart: Hit Parade Italia 1963 (https://www.hitparadeitalia.it/hp_yenda/lpe1963.html): "#3 … MAX: 1 (20/04/1963) | NS: 40".

## caselli-cascodoro (Casco d'oro, 1966)
- Credits, Discogs master 449135 / release 3512058:
  - Rock/Pop; styles Chanson, Beat, Vocal.
  - "Arranged By, Directed By – Franco Monaldi" (all but A4); Guglielmo (Willy) Brezza on I Believe to My Soul.
  - Covers: Kicks (Mann/Weil), I Put a Spell on You, I Believe to My Soul (Ray Charles), Paint It Black, Remember the Rain (Bob Lind).
- genres:
  - beat: Rolling Stone Italia, Francesco Brusco, "10 album per riscoprire il beat italiano" (https://www.rollingstone.it/?p=770140): "E in questo esordio beat di Caterina c'è anche tanto altro".
  - canzoneitaliana.it (https://canzoneitaliana.it/en/canzone/nessuno-mi-puo-giudicare-2-en/): "Caterina Caselli immediately became the beat girl par excellence".
  - pop and chanson: Discogs.
  - Not added: rnb. Discogs styles do not name it and no critic calls the album R&B; the R&B covers are only mentioned in the notes.
- energy 3, voice powerful:
  - canzoneitaliana: "that song that she sang with such rage".
  - Brusco: "una rivendicazione di libertà, urlata e declinata al femminile".
- mood 3: freedom anthem beside the dark Tutto nero (Paint It Black); a mix.
- groove 3, tempo 3: beat rhythm. In Rolling Stone Italia (Gaspare Baglio, 28 Jan 2026, https://www.rollingstone.it/musica/interviste-musica/caterina-caselli-nessuno-mi-puo-giudicare-e-ancora-un-manifesto-di-liberta/1017950/) Caselli says the song "in origine era un tango… abbiamo cambiato il ritmo".
- instruments:
  - orchestra (Monaldi arranged and directed).
  - bass: Vice Italia interview (https://www.vice.com/it/article/caterina-caselli-intervista/): "Suono io il basso in 'Tutto Nero', sai!?" The interviewer mentions a Fender VI; she does not confirm the model, so sixstringbass was not added.
- lyrics identity|love|social:
  - Caselli in Rolling Stone Italia 2026: "un vero e proprio manifesto di libertà e parità di dignità della donna nelle relazioni".
  - Brusco (introduction): beat songs "intrise di costume, protesta, proto-femminismo".
  - canzoneitaliana: it "perfectly embodied the growing generation gap of the mid-1960s".
- language it|en: I Believe to My Soul is credited to Ray Charles alone with no Italian adapter (Discogs, Wikipedia track list), so it is probably sung in English: **check**.
- influenced_by: Caselli (Rolling Stone Italia 2026) on reworking Nessuno mi può giudicare: "avevo in testa i Beatles, i Rolling Stones, i Kinks, Bob Dylan. Così lì… abbiamo cambiato il ritmo". This applies to 1 of 12 songs, hence [1/12]. The Rolling Stones are also covered on the album (Tutto nero), but this link rests on her statement, not on the cover.
- producers: empty. Wikipedia names Ivo Callegari, but no credit source opened confirms it.
- landmark 0: the Rolling Stone Italia list is a rediscovery list and does not call the album defining.

- lyrics: political|social.

- Chart: Hit Parade Italia 1965 (https://www.hitparadeitalia.it/hp_yenda/lpe1965.html), #18 of the year, "MAX: 3 (24/04/1965)".

## ritapavone-gianburrasca (Gian Burrasca, 1964, type soundtrack)
- **Year 1964:** Discogs master 864313 / release 3693099 dates it 1964. Hit Parade Italia 1965 has it on the album chart "Dal: 26/12/1964", peak #1 on 02/01/1965, #3 of 1965. Release type in the catalogue: soundtrack.
- Credits, Discogs:
  - Written by Nino Rota and Lina Wertmüller.
  - "Arranged By, Directed By [Orchestra] – Luis Enriquez".
  - "Zither [Cetra] – Anton Karas".
- Teddy Reno, *Ricordi* (Gremese 2001, pp. 135-136, excerpt on canzoneitaliana.it, https://canzoneitaliana.it/viva-la-pappa-col-pomodoro/). He was Pavone's mentor, later her husband, and producer of the LP:
  - "Dovevo sovrintendere, negli studi RCA, alla realizzazione della colonna sonora e del relativo LP… Mia era la responsabilità… della puntuale realizzazione tecnico-artistica di tutte le musiche".
  - On Viva la pappa col pomodoro: Rota's rhythm was "una specie di minuetto all'antica". Reno had him add "un 'bounce-tempo' che avrebbe reso il tutto anche 'ballabile'" and had "tutta l'introduzione" played by Karas on the cetra (zither).
- producers: Teddy Reno, from that account. Ferruccio Ricordi, Reno's real name, is the producer Wikipedia gives.
- groove 2: bounce tempo added to the lead song only.
- theatrical 3: TV musical songs.
- instruments: orchestra|zither.
- lyrics: storytelling (songs of the TV adaptation of Vamba's novel).
- genres:
  - soundtrack: the type of the release.
  - pop: Discogs.
  - ballad and surfrock: Discogs styles Surf and Ballad. Surf is mapped to surfrock as the wave-1 rule says, and put last because the evidence is weak.
- Energy, mood and tempo were left empty: no source describes the album's sound.
