# Cuban son and salsa: three landmark albums added by Claude (Oct 2026)

Owner asked to fill the catalogue gap around Buena Vista Social Club (its suggestions fell back on Latin pop).

- **Buena Vista Social Club Presents Ibrahim Ferrer (1999)**: World Circuit/Nonesuch; producers Ry Cooder, Nick Gold, Juan de Marcos González; styles named canción, bolero, son cubano, guajira, guaguancó, son montuno; players include Rubén González (piano), Manuel Galbán and Ry Cooder (electric guitar), Cachaíto López (bass), Eliades Ochoa (guitar), Papi Oviedo (tres), Barbarito Torres (laúd), horns and strings. Reviewers compare it with the Buena Vista album. Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, fRoots, Grammy/Latin Grammy, charts).
- **Celia & Johnny (1974)**: Vaya/Fania; producer Jerry Masucci; arrangements by Johnny Pacheco, Felipe Yanes, Papo Lucca; salsa (Quimbara, a guaguancó-based salsa; Canto a La Habana). Instruments from the conjunto line-up of the period (medium confidence). Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, National Recording Registry, Billboard, Rolling Stone 2024).
- **Siembra (1978)**: Fania; Willie Colón (producer, arranger, trombone), Rubén Blades (vocals, songs); José Torres piano and Fender Rhodes; trombone section; disco intro on Plástico (AllMusic); Pedro Navaja modelled on Brecht/Weill's Threepenny Opera. Source: en.wikipedia (AllMusic, EPM, Rolling Stone 2024, Latin Grammy Hall of Fame).
