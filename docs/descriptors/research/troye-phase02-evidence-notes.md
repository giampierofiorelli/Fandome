## 2023 — Something to Give Each Other — `troye-somethingtogiveeachother`
- Original: 13 October 2023, 10 tracks (32:40), EMI/Capitol (T23_Q, T23_AM). Do not integrate the Rush remix with PinkPantheress and Hyunjin or anniversary extras.
- **Genres**: `pop|dancepop|dance|house|electronic|rnb|ukgarage|frenchtouch` ordered by album-wide importance. Pop/Dance-Pop/Club-Dance taxonomy (T23_AM), house beat on *Rush* (T23_PF/T23_NME), R&B taxonomy in T23_PF, two-step UK-garage comparison ONLY *Got Me Started* (T23_PF), and French-touch ONLY *One of Your Girls* (T23_NME). Source strength for electronic as a genre is inferential on synth/sample prevalence, NOT independently labelled primary album genre. Two minor genre tags are retained under the owner's no-genre-limit instruction, with *track-specific scope explicitly visible*.
- `energy=4, electronic=4, mood=3, theatrical=3, experimental=3, polish=4, tempo=3, groove=4`. NME says MOST of the record is midtempo, despite the driving opener Rush; hence tempo 3 and energy 4 (contrast with softer tracks). Mood deliberately mixed: pleasure, liberation, lonely/post-breakup longing. House/dance dominance raises groove 4, **not** mood 5. Pitchfork praises diverse samples without framing it as maximal experimentalism. Polish comes from deliberately arranged electronic studio production, not assumed budget.
- `voice=soft|clear`, principally close-mic breathy sung vocal (T23_PF, T23_NME). `lyrics=sensuality|love|heartbreak|identity|party|introspection`. `language=en|es`, the duet *In My Room* contains sung Spanish by Guitarricadelafuente (T23_PF, T23_Q); French is not on this record's standard edition.
- **Producers** verified on original track metadata Qobuz T23_Q: Oscar Görres (also OZGO), Styalz Fuego (also Kaelyn Behr), Novodor, Zhone, Ian Kirkpatrick and A. G. Cook. Includes bona fide co-producers; excludes singer features and songwriters who are not producers. Alias forms collapsed. Sample rights/credits of Bag Raiders and Jessica Pratt **do not** make them producers or creative collaborators on the 2023 recordings.
- `influenced_by=Janet Jackson`: artist firsthand Apple Music interview reported July 2023 (T23_JJ) specifically attributes direction of the **forthcoming album** to Jackson's Hollywood Bowl performance. Other pop star comparisons are excluded. `sounds_like` left blank: track-scoped Janet/Jessica Pratt comparisons are insufficient for album-wide similarity.
- `landmark=1` editorial career shift; confidence high on record identity/generic sound, medium on narrow tags and ordinals.

## 2026 — She's the Best — `troye-shesthebest`
- **Original edition**: 9 October 2026, 12 songs, 41:55 (Universal label announcement T26_UM and Qobuz T26_Q). Crucial Qobuz URL caveat: alternate Qobuz catalogue ID ending `eo0qj6qafzbty` is ONLY the **14 August 2026 single**, while the full 12-track edition is `f1wvsbkiki6zu`. Never conflate them.
- `genres=pop|leftfieldpop|electronic|dancepop|rnb|house|triphop|breakbeat|alternativedance|shoegaze|orchestralpop|acousticpop|drumandbass|balearicbeat`. Genre scope is heterogeneous: major album styles (pop, leftfieldpop, electronic, dancepop, some house), secondary / track-specific styles explicitly retained under unlimited genre rule. Vogue (T26_V) documents trip-hop, house, shoegaze, breakbeat and orchestral pop. Guardian (T26_G) identifies acoustic pop on *Embarrassingly*, DnB influence on *Troye Meets World*, and Balearic British pop style on *Canada Dry* and *I Do This For You*. NME corroborates 1990s alt-dance. Album-level pop/R&B is Pitchfork's review category (T26_PF). Do NOT claim shoegaze, orchestral pop or drumandbass dominate all 12 songs.
- **New proposed genre token:** `balearicbeat`. Guardian calls the tracks a Balearic sound; NME late 90s alternative-dance references. This is **medium-confidence normalized genre classification for multiple tracks**, not a declared self-identification. Claude must decide whether to accept `balearicbeat` or store Balearic texture only. If approved, add to `docs/album-descriptors-guide.md`, `src/data/scenes.ts`, `src/data/genreFamilies.ts`, `scripts/validate-descriptors.ts`; family suggest `dance|electronic|house`, but review taxonomy. Until registered the validator will correctly reject this candidate genre; do NOT silently drop it.
- `energy=3, electronic=4, mood=2, theatrical=3, experimental=4, polish=4, tempo=3, groove=3`. First and middle contain driving dance, but many songs are laid-back, acoustic, wistful or dissonant: unlike 2023, record is not sustained club music. `mood=2` captures pervasive longing and loneliness despite ecstatic *Party* (Pitchfork T26_PF, label T26_UM). `experimental=4` comes from deliberate weird samples, harsh vocal fx, dissonant arrangements and artist's explicit ambition (Pitchfork, Vogue), **relative scale** not a claim of avant-garde genre. `groove=3` keeps house cuts without forgetting acoustic/low-drive second half.
- `voice=soft|spoken|clear`: breathy singing and deadpan spoken/speak-sung verses (T26_PF, T26_N), not necessarily rap; loud drums do not imply powerful belting. `lyrics=sensuality|love|heartbreak|introspection|identity|party`. `language=en|fr` only because guest **Lily-Rose Depp** sings a French intro on *Lost Time* (T26_V, T26_Q). This does not establish French as Troye's primary language.
- **Producers** (T26_Q and T26_MB) Troye Sivan, Oscar Görres, Jack Antonoff, George Daniel, Styalz Fuego, Tyde Levi, Rob LFO, FINNEAS, Cirkut, Jasper Harris. Named producers from **one or more original tracks**, not a claim each person produced all 12. Important: MusicBrainz calls producer `Kaeyln Behr` on some tracks, while Qobuz calls producer `Styalz Fuego`; these are the same person, avoid duplicate. In Qobuz's *Party* credit Madonna and Mirwais are **ComposerLyricist because of the sample**; neither produced Sivan's recording. Featured Lily-Rose Depp on *Lost Time* is not a producer. Leland is writing/background vocalist unless a specific producer role is found.
- `influenced_by=Madonna`: directly recorded *Music* sample in *Party* (T26_Party/T26_NPR), PLUS firsthand artist testimony on Madonna shaping his musical taste (T26_AMUS/T26_B). Distinguish song-level sample from broader documented artist admiration. No claim Madonna co-produced record.
- `sounds_like=Saint Etienne|Air` **critical comparisons**, narrowly scoped: Guardian links opener and 1990s British dance-pop to Saint Etienne; Qobuz critic compares *Le Boy* to Air. Provisional album-level recommendation; secondary reviewer may omit if deeming track-only analogues. `landmark=1` provisional 2026 expressive turning point (artist took producer role, larger sonic range), not retrospective long-term influence already established.
- **Critic divergence:** NME and AllMusic value late-night intimacy; Pitchfork praises new sonic freedom but criticizes emotional distance on some songs. Vogue emphasizes experimentation. No review rating is transferred into Fandome scores.
