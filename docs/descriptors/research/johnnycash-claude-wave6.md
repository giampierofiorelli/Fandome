## New tokens
- genre `countrypolitan` (Patsy Cline, Showcase): Jeff Giles "classic countrypolitan sound"; American Hit Network "the 'Countrypolitan' sound". Not in the list; not a synonym of countrypop (different, Nashville-era style).
- genre `jumpblues` (Sam Cooke, Night Beat): ABKCO/AllMusic, his reworkings of "jump blues standards" (on some tracks, so last).
- production `echochamber` (Elvis, 1956): American Hit Network, Sun's slapback echo replaced by RCA's "'professional' echo chamber (a speaker in a hallway)".

## chuckberry-afterschoolsession (After School Session, 1957)
Sources: Jazz Journal 2024 https://jazzjournal.co.uk/2024/05/07/chuck-berry-after-school-session/ ; PopMatters https://www.popmatters.com/berrychuck-afterschool-2495844985.html ; The Recoup 2016 https://therecoup.com/2016/03/06/chuck-berry-after-school-session-hoodoo-records/ ; George Starostin, Only Solitaire https://onlysolitaire.substack.com/p/review-chuck-berry-after-school-session ; American Hit Network https://americanhitnetwork.com/shows/the-alternative-top-40/chuck-berry-after-school-session/2023-07-05
- genres: rockandroll (Starostin "Rock'n'roll"; JJ "inventors of rock 'n' roll"); blues (JJ "the instrumental blues Deep Feeling"; Recoup "deep, straightforward blues numbers"); calypso (JJ "the calypso-flavoured Havana Moon"; Recoup "blending calypso and Latin rhythms"); latin (Recoup; Starostin "a Latin jingle" Berry Pickin'); doowop (Recoup "a hint of doo-wop"); rockabilly (Starostin: Down Bound Train "rockabilly stomper", one track, last).
- energy 4, tempo 4: Starostin "mostly fast and light". groove 4: JJ "dance-inducing instrumentals". mood 4: Starostin "mostly upbeat and optimistic"; Recoup "fun, potent". polish 2: JJ aim "to capture the excitement of a live recording, in contrast to a more polished studio finish"; Starostin "slightly crackling distortion". theatrical 2, experimental 1: plain classic structures, my judgment from the above.
- voice clear: PopMatters "smooth, light voice".
- instruments: electricguitar (JJ credits "Berry (v, elg)"); piano (Starostin: Johnnie Johnson; PopMatters "boogie piano"); bass (Starostin: Willie Dixon's bass); slideguitar (Starostin, slide on Deep Feeling; AHN "slide guitar"); maracas (PopMatters, Berry Pickin' "complete with maracas").
- lyrics: party/social/love: PopMatters "songs about school days, fast cars, and dates and other teenage experiences"; Starostin "school boredom, juke joints, teenage escapism, cars, a warning about alcohol abuse, romantic self-confidence".
- producers: empty. Only Wikipedia names Leonard and Phil Chess as session producers; Starostin names Leonard Chess only as label head.
- sounds_like: Nat King Cole [1/12] (PopMatters: Together shows Berry's "fondness for Nat King Cole"); Bo Diddley [1/12] (PopMatters: Berry Pickin' "Berry's take on Bo Diddley").
- landmark 0: no source I opened calls this LP defining (PopMatters: "a singles artist, not an album artist"; AHN: plays "like a hit collection"). The AOTY reviewer reportedly calls it foundational but the page was 403.

Sources: Starostin https://onlysolitaire.substack.com/p/review-buddy-holly-the-chirping-crickets ; Jeff Burger, Americana Highways 2026 https://americanahighways.org/2026/04/05/rediscover-buddy-holly-chirping-crickets/ (also Best Classic Bands https://bestclassicbands.com/buddy-holly-chirping-crickets-review-4-7-26/) ; Colin's Review https://colinsreview.com/2019/10/29/the-chirping-crickets/ ; Sputnikmusic user review https://www.sputnikmusic.com/album/31413/Buddy-Holly-The-Chirping-Crickets ; American Hit Network https://americanhitnetwork.com/shows/the-alternative-top-40/the-crickets-the-chirping-crickets/2023-08-23 ; ncpodcasts https://www.ncpodcasts.com/500-music-history/420-buddy-holly-and-the-crickets-the-chirping-crickets-1957 ; Bear Family https://www.bear-family.com/holly-buddy-the-chirping-crickets-lp-180g-vinyl.html ; Underappreciated Rock https://underappreciatedrock.org/node/3530
- genres: rockandroll (Starostin; Colin "Rock & Roll"); pop (Starostin "jangly, guitar-based pop"; Sputnik "bouncy 50's/60's pop"); rockabilly (Burger: undubbed Oh Boy "more of a rockabilly feel"); doowop (Starostin: Picks' "doo-wop-style backing"); country, rnb (AHN: covers balance him "on the knife edge between country music and R&B"; Sputnik "feint bluegrass/country vibe").
- mood 4: Starostin "sweet, energetic, totally innocent happiness". energy 4: Starostin "energetic"; Sputnik "fast paced rocker". polish 2: Starostin "no strings, pianos, or production slickness". tempo 3, groove 3: mix of rockers and "more ballady" songs (Sputnik).
- voice soft: Starostin "sweet, shaky, naturally-stuttery"; Sputnik "Hiccup" vocals.

- lyrics love: Starostin "a happy celebration of love and fidelity".
- producers Norman Petty: Burger ("producer Norman Petty"); ncpodcasts.
- sounds_like: Bill Haley (Colin: closer to Bill Haley than Elvis at this stage); Carl Perkins (Starostin: guitar phrasing "sometimes echoes Carl Perkins or Scotty Moore"; Moore left out as a sideman). Sputnik's "Brian Wilson if he were a hillbilly" is a playful user remark: not used.
- influenced_by empty: ncpodcasts' "fell under the rock and roll influence of Elvis Presley" is a writer's statement, not Holly's.
- landmark 1: Rolling Stone 500 (2003 #421, Underappreciated Rock); Starostin, a record that "re-formats pop music"; Bear Family's "most famous and important albums" not used (label value judgment).

## johnnycash-johnnycashwithhishotandblueguitar (1957)
Sources: johnnycash.com https://www.johnnycash.com/?p=7623 ; Johnny Cash Infocenter https://johnny-cash-infocenter.com/music/releases/with-his-hot-and-blue-guitar/ ; Starostin https://onlysolitaire.substack.com/p/review-johnny-cash-with-his-hot-and ; Sputnikmusic user review https://www.sputnikmusic.com/review/61235/Johnny-Cash-Johnny-Cash-With-His-Hot-And-Blue-Guitar ; Colin's Review https://colinsreview.com/2019/11/08/with-his-hot-and-blue-guitar/ ; American Hit Network https://americanhitnetwork.com/shows/the-alternative-top-40/johnny-cash-with-his-hot-and-blue-guitar/2023-08-09
- genres: country, folk (Starostin "a folk-and-country man through and through", "middle-o'-the-road country"); rockabilly (Colin "The rockabilly backdrop"; Starostin disagrees: "not rockabilly", noted); blues (Sputnik "country and blues roots"; johnnycash.com "blue ballads"); gospel (AHN "gospel songs"; johnnycash.com, one song "of a spiritual nature").
- polish 1: Starostin "earthy, dirty, rusty lo-fi sound". production lofi: same. mood 2: Sputnik "An early audible example of American despair" vs Colin "youthful exuberance". energy 3, tempo 3: johnnycash.com "a strong country beat that never lags"; Starostin songs vary "a little slower, a little faster". groove 2: Starostin "Technically, this is danceable music" but meant for listening. theatrical 1: AHN "talking to you from the pit of his heart".
- voice empty: sources describe it as deep/bass ("deep, paternal bass tone", Starostin; "rangy, big, hollow voice", johnnycash.com): no matching token.
- instruments: acousticguitar, electricguitar, doublebass: Infocenter (Cash vocals/acoustic guitar, Luther Perkins electric guitar, Marshall Grant bass); Sputnik "acoustic guitar, electric guitar, and double bass".
- lyrics: Sputnik "love, crime, rural living, and prison time"; johnnycash.com themes "loneliness, women, and God"; Starostin "marital fidelity", "moralistic"; AHN train, gospel, prison songs.
- producers: johnnycash.com: "Tracks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 Produced by Jack Clement"; "Tracks 5, 7, 9, 11 Produced by Sam Phillips" (Clement first, more tracks).
- sounds_like: Hank Williams [1/12] (Starostin: Cry, Cry, Cry could be "a Hank Williams song"); Ernest Tubb [1/12] (Starostin on So Doggone Lonesome). Starostin's Gordon Jenkins remark is about the Folsom melody, not the sound: not used.
- landmark 1: Colin "this might be Cash's most essential album"; AHN "provided the blueprint for all that followed".

## samcooke-nightbeat (Night Beat, 1963)
Sources: ABKCO (AllMusic John Bush review) https://www.abkco.com/store/nightbeat-rca/ ; Derek's Music Blog https://dereksmusicblog.com/2012/01/21/sam-cooke-night-beat/ ; Chimes Freedom https://chimesfreedom.com/tag/ed-hall ; 1001 Albums blog (Mojo #32) https://1001albums.blogspot.com/2009/12/1047-mojo-32.html
- genres: blues (ABKCO/AllMusic "intimate blues"; Chimes Freedom "steeped in the blues"); soul (Derek "blues and soul"); rnb (Chimes Freedom "rhythm and blues"); jumpblues (new; AllMusic, jump blues standards, a few tracks).
- tempo 1, energy 2: Chimes Freedom/AllMusic "taken at the pace of a late-night stroll"; Derek "most tracks are slow and bluesy", Little Red Rooster and Shake, Rattle and Roll the exceptions. groove 1 same. mood 1: Derek "drenched in sadness and regret"; Chimes Freedom dark, heartbreak (AllMusic counters: "difficult to become depressed"). theatrical 1: "intimate", "low-key" (AllMusic). polish 3: sparse but professional RCA studio session ("eschewing overly sleek arrangements", 1001albums).
- voice empty: praised ("transcendent") but not described in a way matching a token.
- instruments: Derek "Hammond organ, piano, and searing guitars"; Chimes Freedom personnel: Ray Johnson piano, Billy Preston organ, Barney Kessel guitar, Hal Blaine/Ed Hall drums, Cliff Hils/Clif White bass, René Hall rhythm guitar.
- lyrics heartbreak|love: Derek "sadness, regret, and loneliness", songs about relationships; Chimes Freedom "heartbreak as a recurring theme".
- producers: Derek "Hugo and Luigi produced" (Hugo Peretti, Luigi Creatore); René Hall arranger (not producer).
- landmark 1: Chimes Freedom "moody masterpiece", many fans' favourite Cooke album; 1001albums "often considered one of Cooke's best"; Mojo list.
