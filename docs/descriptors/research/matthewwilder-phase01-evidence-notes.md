# Matthew Wilder — album evidence notes (Phase 01; 28 October 2026)

## matthewwilder-idontspeakthelanguage — I Don't Speak the Language (1983)

**Release/credits.** Original 1983 Private-I/Epic. Credits are to three producers: Peter Bunetta, Rick Chudacoff, Bill Elliott (some short resources abbreviate as 'Elliot'). Source: https://sessiondays.com/2024/02/14/1983-matthew-wilder-i-dont-speak-the-language/; album overview https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Speak_the_Language.

**Genres.** New wave from AllMusic; synth-pop from album's published genre metadata and Prophet synthesizer credits. Contemporary pop/rock per AllMusic exact style. Broad pop is the label genre's umbrella (not plain rock), used last. No claim that the entire album is reggae simply because the hit has an off-beat feel; no unsupported reggae tag. Sources https://www.allmusic.com/album/i-dont-speak-the-language-mw0000196324 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Speak_the_Language https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/i-dont-speak-the-language-matthew-wilder/0884977526424.

**Instruments.** Sessiondays lists Wilder / Elliott / Cuomo synthesizers, Wilder/Elliott piano; Bunetta drums; Herring/Griffin/Weston guitar; bass from Chudacoff, McBride and Johnson; Paulinho Da Costa percussion; saxes on The Kid's American, trumpets on Love Above the Ground Floor, E-mu Emulator by Paul Fox on World of the Rich and Famous and I Was There; mandolin by John McFee on final track. The biggest hit also has synth-generated drums. Source https://sessiondays.com/2024/02/14/1983-matthew-wilder-i-dont-speak-the-language/; named Prophet-5/10 and Emulator from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Don%27t_Speak_the_Language. **Production** `prophet5` and `oberheimdmx` are particularly documented for Break My Stride in MusicBrainz, https://musicbrainz.org/release/ac8d5fd5-9379-450c-82b9-f7cfa1ba83b5; Prophet-10, Emulator and horn arrangements are documented elsewhere on the LP. Not a whole-record claim about drum machine usage.

**Voice/lyrics/scale.** Clear male lead with strong layered backing vocals; no supported fully choral lead (background voices don't determine lead-voice category). 4 energy / 3 electronics / 4 mood / 2 theatrical / 2 experimental / 4 polish / 3 tempo / 4 groove are authorial album-wide assessments, motivated by buoyant hit plus mid-tempo songs and live players; expressive but generally radio-accessible. Love and class/mobility (The Kid's American, World of the Rich and Famous), independence/identity (Break My Stride), old memories (Dreams Keep Bringing You Back). Sources https://www.allmusic.com/album/i-dont-speak-the-language-mw0000196324 https://sessiondays.com/2024/02/14/1983-matthew-wilder-i-dont-speak-the-language/ https://www.culturesonar.com/catching-up-with-matthew-wilder-no-breaking-his-stride/.

**Influence vs similarity.** Wilder says the new UK-wave sounds including Culture Club and Thompson Twins were directly influencing creation of Break My Stride. They go in album-specific `influenced_by`, not producers, guest performers or 1970s folk influences. Source https://www.culturesonar.com/catching-up-with-matthew-wilder-no-breaking-his-stride/. Artists in `sounds_like` are editorial sonic comparisons to 1980s pop production, not cited statements of influence.

## matthewwilder-bouncinoffthewalls — Bouncin' Off the Walls (1984)

**Release/producer.** First 1984, confirmed by Apple Music and physical edition; AllMusic style metadata. Sole credited record producers Peter Bunetta and Rick Chudacoff. Shingo Take is associate producer only, hence omitted. Sources https://music.apple.com/us/album/bouncin-off-the-walls/359713241, https://www.allmusic.com/album/bouncin-off-the-walls-mw0001050698/credits, https://labibledelawestcoast.blogspot.com/2012/08/matthew-wilder-bouncin-off-walls-1984.html.

**Genres.** AllMusic: New Wave and Contemporary Pop/Rock. Original physical issue: Synth-pop and Pop Rock. Specialist sleeve source: High Tech AOR. Apple Music: broad Pop. AOR is a specialist secondary style, not claimed as consensus. Sources https://www.allmusic.com/album/bouncin-off-the-walls-mw0001050698/credits https://vinylpursuit.com/products/matthew-wilder-bouncin-off-the-walls https://labibledelawestcoast.blogspot.com/2012/08/matthew-wilder-bouncin-off-walls-1984.html https://music.apple.com/us/album/bouncin-off-the-walls/359713241.

**Instruments.** AllMusic album personnel lists bass Nathan East/Reggie McBride, guitar Dann Huff/Danny Jacob/Brian Ray, drums Bunetta/Colaiuta/Grimaldi, horns Doug Norwine/Kurt McGettrick/Steve Madaio/John Liotine/Dick Hyde, keyboards/synths Wilder/Buxer/Cuomo/Gibbs, plus percussion and vocalists. The specialist original-LP transcription supplies track-level Chapman Stick and specific Simmons drums, Linn drum programming and vocoder; higher precision but less independently verified. Instruments `drummachine` and `electronicdrums` complement conventional drums. Sources https://www.allmusic.com/album/bouncin-off-the-walls-mw0001050698/credits https://labibledelawestcoast.blogspot.com/2012/08/matthew-wilder-bouncin-off-walls-1984.html.

**Production.** Track-level synthesizer credits include Emulator, Yamaha DX7, Prophet-5, Jupiter-8, Korg Poly-61, LinnDrum, Oberheim OB-Xa and vocoder. Do not assert that every machine appears on every track; all appear at least once in transcription. `korgpoly61` and `simmonsdrums` need enumerations. If Claude checks and disagrees with this weaker secondary sleeve source, remove specific token but keep verified broad instrument. Source https://labibledelawestcoast.blogspot.com/2012/08/matthew-wilder-bouncin-off-walls-1984.html.

**Voice, themes and feel.** Wilder still sings largely clear melodic lead over stacked harmonies, occasional emphatic passages. The second set mixes jaunty pop, relationship songs, cynicism/scandal and slower material. Editorial 4/3/3/3/2/4/3/4 scoring with fewer through-album electronic assumptions than synthetic personnel might suggest. A contemporary UK review snippet likened the vocal on Cry Just a Little to Elvis Costello, but the underlying PDF was inaccessible for confirmation. Consequently no Elvis Costello album-wide comparison or influence is stored. Relevance of synthesizer and live funk groove documented in credits; do not add R&B genre based on session musicians alone.

## matthewwilder-mulan — Mulan: An Original Walt Disney Records Soundtrack (1998)

**Attribution.** Disney official site: songs music Matthew Wilder, lyrics David Zippel, original orchestral score Jerry Goldsmith. Multiple original issues are co-billed slightly differently. MusicBrainz separates producer at track level: Wilder for Honor to Us All, Reflection, I'll Make a Man Out of You, A Girl Worth Fighting For, True to Your Heart and pop Reflection; Goldsmith for the instrumental cues. Source https://disney.go.com/disneyrecords/mulan/index.html https://musicbrainz.org/release/afb288ee-771e-4382-9cb4-15fbbb0d64e4 https://musicbrainz.org/release/c672369d-61fa-4cef-92cf-14fd86c413c7.

**Genres.** Soundtrack (format and genre for catalogue); Musical Theatre, Pop, Operatic Pop are explicit album genres in published reference, so `operaticpop` is a newly required token. Modern Classical from edition metadata covers score cues, not the pop singles. Sources https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulan_%281998_soundtrack%29 https://musicbase.net/disneys-mulan-an-original-walt-disney-records-soundtrack.

**Instruments/production.** Goldsmith orchestral score with strings, busy percussion, harp and intermittent electronic textures described by specialist film score critic. Cast numbers were orchestrated by Douglas Besterman and conducted by Paul Bogaev; Vanessa-Mae violin appears on a variant bonus song and should not be assumed central to the base 12-track edition; some original editions still have orchestral string parts. `stringarrangements` refers to documented orchestration. Avoid claiming particular Chinese traditional instruments without track-level credits. Sources https://www.musicweb-international.com/film/1998/dec98/mulan.html https://musicbrainz.org/release/afb288ee-771e-4382-9cb4-15fbbb0d64e4.

**Voice/mood.** Contrast between Lea Salonga/Donny Osmond cast songs, powerful Aguilera ending, Stevie Wonder and 98 Degrees pop track, collective choruses, and Goldsmith instrumental score. Set `clear|powerful|choral|instrumental`; `instrumental` is album component, not Wilder's lead voice. Mood 3 combines battle tension and optimism; energy3, electronics1, theatrical5, polish5, tempo3, groove2. Themes identity (Reflection), social expectations / gender (Honor to Us All / I'll Make A Man), storytelling, love. Note CD sequence has substantial score proportion; do not classify as standard Wilder pop album.

## matthewwilder-especiallyonbirthdays — Especially on Birthdays (2021)

**Release/identity.** US music score by Wilder written for Dave Brown / The PaperBoats' nonverbal children's visual theatre production; 2018-19 staging predates the separate album release of 3 March 2021. Eleven tracks, ~36 minutes, available digitally via Amazon. Source https://thepaperboats.org/birthdays/ https://music.amazon.com/albums/B08XZVD9JQ https://www.broadwayworld.com/adelaide/article/ESPECIALLY-ON-BIRTHDAYS-Comes-To-Adelaide-Festival-Centre-20190404.

**Critical separation.** The Australian score was Zephyr Quartet & voiceROM, the Singapore version Stan&Soap, US version Wilder. Attributing quartet, voice ensemble or any string instrumentation to the 2021 US album is unsupported and would create false recommendations. The PaperBoats describes the recording as 'composed, arranged and performed by Wilder', **not** 'produced by Wilder'. The `producers` field must therefore be empty; same for instrumentation and production models. Sources https://www.thebarefootreview.com.au/menu/news-opinion/123-2014-interviews/2090-interview-dave-brown-patching-the-paperboats.html https://thepaperboats.org/birthdays/.

**Genre and missingness.** `soundtrack` describes the staged work; no source verifies enough precise musical styles (e.g. childrensmusic, musicaltheatre or contemporaryinstrumental). Those tags are NOT included simply because the target audience are children or the performance uses little speech. Similarly blank energy/electronics/mood/theatrical/polish/tempo/groove, voice and language: music could still contain sung material, and the nonverbal nature of the performance does not prove score without vocalise. `confidence=low`, `status=draft`. Deliberate exception to the 4-genre and instrument enrichment target; do not pad. Further high-quality audio/sleeve verification needed.
