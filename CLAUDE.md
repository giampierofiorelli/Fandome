# Fandome: notes for Claude

- Independent, English-UI social network for music fans. React 18 + TypeScript + Vite, deployed to GitHub Pages by `.github/workflows/pages.yml` (tests, nightly concert fetch, build).
- No paid backend for now (owner's decision): state lives in the browser (IndexedDB via `src/lib/mediaStore.ts`, loaded in `src/store/store.tsx`).
- Keep the current design (calm glass, one column, big light numerals, Doto dot-matrix accents, floating tab bar). The listening party look must not change.
- GDPR: nothing from third parties loads before consent or a user tap; fonts are self-hosted.
- Album descriptors (`docs/album-descriptors-guide.md`, branch `descriptors`): owner's rule, no limit on the number of values in any column; every genre a source names goes in, new genres are added to the list.
- **Descriptors scope (owner, Oct 2026):** live albums and compilations / greatest hits are not a priority: don't research them for now (studio albums, EPs, soundtracks and documented collaborations first). The ones already described stay; they may be added in the future.
- Discovery (suggestions, Bridge, "A scene you don't know yet") never targets the global markets (US, UK; CA, AU, IE too for now, to rethink later) or English-language pop: only local scenes, singing mainly in their own language (owner's rule; `isLocalArtist` in `src/lib/local.ts`). Global crossover stars (two or more key albums sung mainly in English, e.g. Shakira) are not a local scene, and an album counts as a local discovery only if sung mainly in its scene's language.
- **Scenes data (Italian classics' descriptors, connections file, point 8 in ROADMAP): the owner will bring it up; don't remind them (Oct 2026). Never list unreleased albums as "to research".**
- **Descriptor schema v4 (Oct 2026):** 27 columns, `instruments` and `production` after `voice` (lists in `src/data/instruments.ts`, `src/data/production.ts`). Both weigh more than era; language almost nothing (owner). Older artists get them via `<artist-id>_instruments.csv` (album_id,instruments,production,sources).
- **Reception (Oct 2026, owner):** `docs/descriptors/reception/<artist-id>.csv` (album_id,importance,acclaim,sources), 0–5 each: career importance and critical standing today (at release or later, the same). Used as tie-break for artist links and to never suggest a panned album from an acclaimed one. Claude researches it (owner, Oct 2026), not ChatGPT: after importing a phase, add its albums' rows with sources (Metacritic, AllMusic, Pitchfork, best-of lists, certifications; for Italian artists Rockol, OndaRock, Rolling Stone Italia, FIMI). Leave a value empty when nothing supports it.
- **Thin descriptors (owner, Oct 2026):** on every import, check rows with low confidence or many empty cells and enrich them yourself from sources (fill only what a source supports).
- **ChatGPT descriptors prompt:** `docs/chatgpt-descriptors-prompt.md`, one prompt for every artist (only the last line names the artist). Keep it in sync when the guide or genre list changes.
- **Recommendation engine (owner's rule):** when the owner flags a bad suggestion, it is a signal that the algorithm is wrong: find the root cause and fix it with a general rule, the same for every album. Never special-case an album, artist or result. Record the owner's judgment in `docs/similarity-judgments.csv` and check every change against all judged pairs and the whole catalogue (`src/lib/judgments.test.ts`, `src/lib/recQuality.test.ts`).
- **Before planning new work, read `ROADMAP.md`**: it lists features we decided to do later (e.g. Apple Music in the listening party, collector marketplace) and what is waiting on the owner.
- The owner writes in Italian: answer in Italian, short, outcome first.

## Manifesto (owner's words, shown in the footer)

Fandome is made by music lovers for music lovers, and above all to promote and support the artists. The internet and social media promised to be democratic and accessible, for listeners and for artists; instead algorithms and playlists decide what people hear, and artists who aren't on the right lists stay unknown. Fandome wants a truer, more open, more democratic way to discover music and to be discovered: explainable feeds and suggestions, fans bringing artists to other fans, artists presented at their best. Note: do NOT promise "no ads" anywhere: the site may need ads, a marketplace or tips in the future (owner's decision); sponsored content must always be clearly labelled.

No hate, no bullying, no body shaming of artists, no fan wars: fans of different artists should discover each other's music, not fight. Fandome is only about music: every post is tied to at least one artist, off-topic content is discouraged and can be reported ("Not about music").
